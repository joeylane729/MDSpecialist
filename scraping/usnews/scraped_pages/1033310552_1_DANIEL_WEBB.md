## Overview

Dr. Daniel Webb is a neurosurgeon in Rome, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AdventHealth Redmond](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/redmond-regional-medical-center-6380960) and [Atrium Health Floyd Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/floyd-medical-center-6380950). He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Daniel Webb has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-webb-629566#expertise). Dr. Daniel Webb accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-webb-629566#insurance). Dr. Daniel Webb is highly recommended by patients.
