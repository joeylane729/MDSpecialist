## Overview

Dr. Howard Eisenberg is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northwest Medical Center-Tucson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/northwest-medical-center-6864010) and [Tucson VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/southern-arizona-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6860520).
