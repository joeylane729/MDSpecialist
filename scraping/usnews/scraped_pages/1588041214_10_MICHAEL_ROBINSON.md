## Overview

Dr. Michael Robinson is a pediatric hematologist-oncologist in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Monroe Carell Jr. Children's Hospital at Vanderbilt](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/monroe-carell-jr-childrens-hospital-at-vanderbilt-PA6521060) and [Vanderbilt University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/vanderbilt-university-medical-center-6521060). He received his medical degree from Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Michael Robinson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-robinson-1425190#insurance).

### Pediatric Hematology-Oncology

Pediatric hematologists/oncologists deal with diseases of the blood, spleen and lymph glands in children. They treat conditions such as anemia, clotting disorders, sickle cell disease, hemophilia, leukemia and lymphoma.
