## Overview

Dr. Chikezie I. Eseonu is a neurosurgeon in Hagerstown, Maryland and is affiliated with [UPMC Harrisburg](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/pinnaclehealth-hospitals-6230039). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Chikezie I.
