## Overview

Dr. Elias Dagnew is a neurosurgeon in Johns Creek, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory Johns Creek Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-johns-creek-hospital-6380400) and [Northside Hospital Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northside-hospital-6380171).
