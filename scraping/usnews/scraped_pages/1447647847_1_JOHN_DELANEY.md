## Overview

Dr. John M. Delaney is a psychiatrist in Indianapolis, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-academic-health-center-6420020) and [Riley Hospital for Children at IU Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/riley-hospital-for-children-at-iu-health-PA6420020). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John M. Delaney has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-delaney-371306#expertise). Dr. John M. Delaney accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-delaney-371306#insurance). Dr. John M. Delaney is highly recommended by patients.
