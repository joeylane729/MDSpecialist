## Overview

Dr. John J. Wilson is a radiation oncologist in Voorhees, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Virtua Voorhees Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/virtua-voorhees-6221600) and [Penn Presbyterian Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-presbyterian-medical-center-6232170).
