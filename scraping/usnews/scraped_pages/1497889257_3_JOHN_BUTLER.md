## Overview

Dr. John M. Butler is an infectious disease specialist in Sugar Land, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Houston Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/houston-methodist-hospital-6741960) and [Houston Methodist Sugar Land Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-sugar-land-hospital-6740138).
