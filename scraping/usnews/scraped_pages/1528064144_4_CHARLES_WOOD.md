Dr. Charles Wood is a pediatrician in Durham, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Duke Children's Hospital and Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/duke-childrens-hospital-and-health-center-PA6360355) and [Duke University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/duke-university-medical-center-6360355).
