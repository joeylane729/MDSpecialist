## Overview

Dr. John B. Butler is a neurosurgeon in Fairlawn, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [Cleveland Clinic Akron General](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/akron-general-medical-center-6410010). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Georgia and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John B. Butler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-butler-596751#expertise). Dr. John B. Butler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-butler-596751#insurance). Dr. John B. Butler is highly recommended by patients.
