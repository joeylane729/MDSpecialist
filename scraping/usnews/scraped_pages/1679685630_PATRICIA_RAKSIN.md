## Overview

Dr. Patricia B. Raksin is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois. She received her medical degree from University of Maryland School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Patricia B. Raksin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patricia-raksin-528419#expertise).
