## Overview

Dr. Joshua Lim is a neurosurgeon in Greenbelt, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Maryland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/university-of-maryland-medical-center-6320330), [Adventist Healthcare Shady Grove Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/shady-grove-adventist-hospital-6320775), [NSPC Surgery Center of Maryland](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/nspc-surgery-center-of-maryland-A3522311) and [Surgcenter of Silver Spring](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/surgcenter-of-silver-spring-A1094217). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Joshua Lim has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-lim-985452#expertise). Dr. Joshua Lim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-lim-985452#insurance).
