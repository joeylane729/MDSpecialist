## Overview

Dr. Paul W. Hartzfeld is a neurosurgeon in Akron, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic Akron General](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/akron-general-medical-center-6410010) and [Summa Health-Akron Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/summa-akron-city-and-st-thomas-hospitals-6410012). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Ohio and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul W. Hartzfeld has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-hartzfeld-623189#expertise). Dr. Paul W. Hartzfeld is highly recommended by patients.
