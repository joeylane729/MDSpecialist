## Overview

Dr. Kenneth Lee is a neurologist in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Chicago Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/university-of-chicago-medical-center-6431210) and [Loyola Medicine MacNeal Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/macneal-hospital-6430240).
