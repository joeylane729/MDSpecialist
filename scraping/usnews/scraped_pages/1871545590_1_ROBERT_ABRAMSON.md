Abramson is a neurosurgeon in Augusta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Piedmont Augusta Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/university-hospital-6380270) and [Select Specialty Hospital-Augusta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/select-specialty-hospital-augusta-6380042).
