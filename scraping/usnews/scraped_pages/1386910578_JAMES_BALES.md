## Overview

Dr. James W. Bales is a neurosurgeon in Anchorage, Alaska and is affiliated with [Alaska Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ak/alaska-regional-hospital-6940015) and [Creekside Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ak/creekside-surgical-center-A4069456). He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James W. Bales has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-bales-1069891#expertise). Bales is highly recommended by patients.
