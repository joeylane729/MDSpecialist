## Overview

Dr. Mitesh V. Shah is a nephrologist in Clinton, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Inova Fairfax Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-fairfax-hospital-6340490) and [Inova Mount Vernon Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-mount-vernon-hospital-6340030).
