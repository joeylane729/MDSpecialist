## Overview

Dr. David W. Allen is a neurosurgeon in Reading, Pennsylvania. He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David W. Allen has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-allen-334968#expertise). Dr. David W. Allen accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-allen-334968#insurance). Dr. David W.
