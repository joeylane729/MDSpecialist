## Overview

Dr. Peter Basta is a neurosurgeon in Kansas City, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Research Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/research-medical-center-6630435) and [Menorah Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/menorah-medical-center-6670020). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Wisconsin and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter Basta has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-basta-420287#expertise). Dr. Peter Basta accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-basta-420287#insurance). Dr. Peter Basta is highly recommended by patients.
