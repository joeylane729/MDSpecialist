## Overview

Dr. James W. Campbell is a geriatrician in Cleveland, Ohio and is affiliated with [MetroHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/metro-health-medical-center-6410655). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James W. Campbell has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, anxiety disorders, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-campbell-511031#expertise). Dr. James W. Campbell accepts Medicare, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-campbell-511031#insurance). Dr. James W.
