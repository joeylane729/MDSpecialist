## Overview

Dr. Ki-Eun Chang is a neurosurgeon in San Diego, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Naval Medical Center San Diego](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/naval-medical-center-san-diego-6932840) and [Rhode Island Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/rhode-island-hospital-6150150). He received his medical degree from Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Ki-Eun Chang has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ki-eun-chang-4043442#expertise).
