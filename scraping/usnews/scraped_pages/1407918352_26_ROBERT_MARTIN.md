## Overview

Dr. Robert B. Martin is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Dallas, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Parkland Health-Dallas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/parkland-memorial-hospital-6740950) and [UT Southwestern Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/ut-southwestern-medical-center-6740343).
