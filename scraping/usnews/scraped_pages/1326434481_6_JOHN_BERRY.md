## Overview

Dr. John S. Berry is a colon and rectal surgeon in Pennington, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CaroMont Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/gaston-memorial-hospital-6360580) and [FirstHealth Moore Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/first-health-moore-regional-hospital-6361150).
