## Overview

Dr. Chong C. Lee is a neurosurgeon in Honolulu, Hawaii and is affiliated with [Straub Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/straub-clinic-and-hospital-6950405). Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/chong-lee-499896#insurance). Dr. Chong C.
