## Overview

Dr. Alvin Marcovici is a neurosurgeon in North Dartmouth, Massachusetts. He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Alvin Marcovici has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alvin-marcovici-190959#expertise). Dr. Alvin Marcovici accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alvin-marcovici-190959#insurance). Dr. Alvin Marcovici is highly recommended by patients.
