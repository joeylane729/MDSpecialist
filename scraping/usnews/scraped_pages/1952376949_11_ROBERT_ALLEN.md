## Overview

Dr. Robert D. Allen is a family medicine doctor in Visalia, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kaweah Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaweah-delta-health-care-district-6933850) and [Adventist Medical Center-Hanford](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/adventist-medical-center-hanford-6930274). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert D. Allen has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, pneumonia, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-allen-138961#expertise).
