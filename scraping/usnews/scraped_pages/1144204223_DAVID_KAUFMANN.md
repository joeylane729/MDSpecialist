## Overview

Dr. David L. Kaufmann is a neurosurgeon in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UPMC Mercy](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-mercy-6232510) and [UPMC Presbyterian Shadyside](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-university-of-pittsburgh-medical-center-6230029). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David L. Kaufmann has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-kaufmann-224453#expertise). Dr. David L. Kaufmann accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-kaufmann-224453#insurance).
