## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey M. Steinberg is a neurologist in Plantation, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hospital Miramar](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/memorial-hospital-6390111) and [Memorial Hospital Pembroke](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/memorial-hospital-pembroke-6390765).
