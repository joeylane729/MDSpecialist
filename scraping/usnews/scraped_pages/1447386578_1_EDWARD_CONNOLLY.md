## Overview

Dr. Edward A. Connolly is an orthopedist in Moline, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Genesis Medical Center-Silvis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/genesis-medical-center-6431511), [UnityPoint Health-Trinity Rock Island](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/trinity-rock-island-6430016), [Quad City Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/quad-city-ambulatory-surgery-center-A9000778) and [Crow Valley Orthopedic Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ia/crow-valley-orthopedic-surgery-center-A2068335). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Edward A. Connolly has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-connolly-240324#expertise). Dr. Edward A. Connolly accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-connolly-240324#insurance). Dr. Edward A. Connolly is highly recommended by patients.
