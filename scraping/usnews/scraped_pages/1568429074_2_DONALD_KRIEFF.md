## Overview

Dr. Donald S. Krieff is a neurosurgeon in Patchogue, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [NYU Langone Hospital-Suffolk](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/brookhaven-memorial-hospital-medical-center-6213923) and [NYU Langone Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-langone-medical-center-6212925).
