## Overview

Dr. Saksith Smithason is a neurosurgeon in Lumberton, North Carolina. He received his medical degree from Chulalongkorn University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Saksith Smithason has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/saksith-smithason-997524#expertise).
