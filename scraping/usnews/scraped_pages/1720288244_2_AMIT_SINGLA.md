## Overview

Dr. Amit Singla is a doctor in Huntington, West Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cabell Huntington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/cabell-huntington-hospital-6350345) and [St. Barnabas Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-barnabas-hospital-6210034).
