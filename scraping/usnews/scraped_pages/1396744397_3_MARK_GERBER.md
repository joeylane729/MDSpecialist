## Overview

Dr. Mark S. Gerber is a neurosurgeon in Honolulu, Hawaii and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Straub Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/straub-clinic-and-hospital-6950405) and [Pali Momi Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/pali-momi-medical-center-6950001). He received his medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark S. Gerber has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-gerber-536915#expertise). Dr. Mark S. Gerber accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-gerber-536915#insurance). Dr. Mark S. Gerber is highly recommended by patients.
