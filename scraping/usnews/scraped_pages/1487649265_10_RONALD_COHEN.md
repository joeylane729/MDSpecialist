## Overview

Dr. Ronald K. Cohen is a psychiatrist in Renton, Washington and is affiliated with [Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/valley-medical-center-6910520). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ronald K. Cohen has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ronald-cohen-788522#expertise). Dr. Ronald K. Cohen accepts Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ronald-cohen-788522#insurance). Dr. Ronald K. Cohen is highly recommended by patients.
