## Overview

Dr. Edward E. Maurin is a neurosurgeon in Grand Junction, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health St. Mary's Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/st-marys-hospital-and-medical-center-6840625) and [Animas Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/animas-surgical-hospital-6840029). He received his medical degree from University of Colorado School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Edward E. Maurin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-maurin-437895#expertise). Dr. Edward E. Maurin accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-maurin-437895#insurance).
