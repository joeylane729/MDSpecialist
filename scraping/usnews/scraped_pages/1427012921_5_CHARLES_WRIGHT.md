## Overview

Dr. Charles J. Wright is a neurosurgeon in Amarillo, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northwest Texas Healthcare System-Amarillo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/northwest-texas-healthcare-6740065) and [Thomas E. Creek VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/amarillo-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6740100). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles J. Wright has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-wright-318056#expertise). Dr. Charles J. Wright accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-wright-318056#insurance). Dr. Charles J. Wright is highly recommended by patients.
