## Overview

Dr. Gerald Oh is a neurosurgeon in Murrieta, California and is affiliated with [Loma Linda University Medical Center-Murrieta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/loma-linda-university-medical-center-murrieta-6930409). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Gerald Oh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gerald-oh-1021247#expertise). Dr. Gerald Oh accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gerald-oh-1021247#insurance). Dr. Gerald Oh is highly recommended by patients.
