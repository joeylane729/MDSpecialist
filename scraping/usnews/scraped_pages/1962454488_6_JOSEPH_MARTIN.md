## Overview

Dr. Joseph Martin is an internal medicine/pediatric specialist in Walton, Kentucky and is affiliated with [St. Elizabeth Florence Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/st-elizabeth-florence-6510120). He received his medical degree from Wright State University Boonshoft School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph Martin has expertise in treating obesity, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-martin-2787356#expertise). Dr. Joseph Martin accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-martin-2787356#insurance).
