## Overview

Dr. Joseph Hsieh is a neurosurgeon in Denver, Colorado. He received his medical degree from Harvard Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph Hsieh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-hsieh-5243141#expertise).
