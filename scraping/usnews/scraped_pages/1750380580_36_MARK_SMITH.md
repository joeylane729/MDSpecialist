## Overview

Smith is an urologist in Sheffield, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Helen Keller Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/helen-keller-hospital-6530880) and [North Alabama Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/eliza-coffee-memorial-hospital-6530440).
