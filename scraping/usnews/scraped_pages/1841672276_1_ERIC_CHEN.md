## Overview

Dr. Eric Chen is an orthopedist in Covina, California and is affiliated with [Casa Colina Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/casa-colina-hospital-for-rehabilitative-medicine-6930399), [Casa Colina Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/casa-colina-surgery-center-A1714924) and [Santa Monica Surgical Partners](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/santa-monica-surgical-partners-A9499647). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Ohio and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Eric Chen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-chen-3000151#insurance).
