## Overview

Dr. Isa S. Canavati is a neurosurgeon in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dupont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/dupont-hospital-6420033) and [Lutheran Hospital of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/lutheran-hospital-6420420). He received his medical degree from American University of Beirut Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Isa S. Canavati has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/isa-canavati-141206#expertise). Dr. Isa S. Canavati accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/isa-canavati-141206#insurance).
