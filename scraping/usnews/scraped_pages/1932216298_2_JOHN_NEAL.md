## Overview

Dr. John H. Neal is a neurosurgeon in Marshfield, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [Marshfield Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/ministry-st-josephs-hospital-6450860). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John H. Neal has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-neal-515952#expertise). Dr. John H. Neal accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-neal-515952#insurance). Dr. John H. Neal is highly recommended by patients.
