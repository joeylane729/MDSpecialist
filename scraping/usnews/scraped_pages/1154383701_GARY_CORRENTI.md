## Overview

Dr. Gary J. Correnti is a neurosurgeon in Fort Myers, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Gulf Coast Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/gulf-coast-medical-center-6390323), [Lee Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/lee-memorial-hospital-6390240) and [Adult & Childrens Surgery Center of Southwest Florida](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/adult-childrens-surgery-center-of-southwest-florida-A9696000).
