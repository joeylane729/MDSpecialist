## Overview

Dr. Martin Greenberg is a neurosurgeon in Boca Raton, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Boca Raton Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/boca-raton-regional-hospital-6390038) and [Delray Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/delray-medical-center-6390081). He received his medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Martin Greenberg has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/martin-greenberg-264969#expertise). Dr. Martin Greenberg accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/martin-greenberg-264969#insurance) below.
