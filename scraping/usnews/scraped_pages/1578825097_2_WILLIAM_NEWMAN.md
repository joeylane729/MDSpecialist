## Overview

Dr. William B. Newman is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Vacaville, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [David Grant USAF Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/david-grant-medical-center-6930735) and [Kaiser Permanente Vallejo Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-vallejo-medical-center-6933755).
