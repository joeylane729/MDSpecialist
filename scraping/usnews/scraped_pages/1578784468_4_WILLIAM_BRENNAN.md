## Overview

Dr. William F. Brennan is an orthopedist in Warwick, Rhode Island and is affiliated with [Kent Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/kent-county-memorial-hospital-6150215) and [Orthopedics Rhode Island Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ri/orthopedics-rhode-island-surgery-center-A1918736). He received his medical degree from Tufts University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William F. Brennan has expertise in treating arthritis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-brennan-28535#expertise). Dr. William F. Brennan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-brennan-28535#insurance). Dr. William F. Brennan is highly recommended by patients.
