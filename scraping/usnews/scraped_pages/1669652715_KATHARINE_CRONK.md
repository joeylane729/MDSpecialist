## Overview

Dr. Katharine M. Cronk is a neurosurgeon in Nashua, New Hampshire and is affiliated with [Southern New Hampshire Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nh/southern-new-hampshire-medical-center-6120290). She received her medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Katharine M. Cronk has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/katharine-cronk-863957#expertise). Dr. Katharine M. Cronk accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/katharine-cronk-863957#insurance).
