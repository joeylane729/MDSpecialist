## Overview

Dr. Shah-Naz Khan is a neurosurgeon in Flint, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [McLaren Flint Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/mclaren-regional-medical-center-6441038) and [Corewell Health Beaumont Troy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/beaumont-hospital-6442463).
