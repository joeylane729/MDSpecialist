## Overview

Dr. Vanessa C. Milano is a neurosurgeon in Edison, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [JFK University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/jfk-medical-center-6220247) and [Bayshore Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/bayshore-community-hospital-6220395).
