## Overview

Dr. Douglas R. Cook is a psychiatrist in Indianapolis, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Community Hospital East](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/community-hospital-east-6420605) and [Community Hospital North](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/community-hospital-north-6420023). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Douglas R. Cook has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/douglas-cook-606415#expertise). Dr. Douglas R. Cook accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/douglas-cook-606415#insurance). Dr. Douglas R. Cook is highly recommended by patients.
