## Overview

Dr. Joseph A. Traina is a neurosurgeon in South Miami, Florida. He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph A. Traina has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-traina-21016#expertise). Dr. Joseph A. Traina accepts Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-traina-21016#insurance). Dr. Joseph A. Traina is highly recommended by patients.
