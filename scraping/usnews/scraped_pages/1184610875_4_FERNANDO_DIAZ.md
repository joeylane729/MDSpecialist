## Overview

Dr. Fernando Diaz is a neurosurgeon in Royal Oak, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Henry Ford St. John Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-john-hospital-and-medical-center-6440805) and [McLaren Macomb Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/mclaren-macomb-hospital-6441847).
