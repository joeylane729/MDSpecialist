## Overview

Dr. James H. Dozier is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Longview, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Select Specialty Hospital-Longview](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/select-specialty-hospital-longview-6740307) and [Christus Good Shepherd Medical Center-Marshall](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/good-shepherd-medical-center-marshall-6742430). Dozier has expertise in treating hysterectomy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-dozier-656603#expertise). Dr. James H. Dozier accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-dozier-656603#insurance). Dr. James H.
