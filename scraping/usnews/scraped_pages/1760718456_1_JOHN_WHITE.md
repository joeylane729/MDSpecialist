## Overview

Dr. John White is a pulmonologist in Taos, New Mexico and is affiliated with [Holy Cross Hospital-Taos](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/holy-cross-hospital-6850460). He received his medical degree from University of Mississippi School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John White has expertise in treating pneumonia, sleep apnea, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-white-2493802#expertise). Dr. John White accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-white-2493802#insurance). Dr. John White is highly recommended by patients.
