## Overview

Dr. James M. Calhoun is a neurosurgeon in North Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arkansas Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-surgical-hospital-6710028), [Baptist Health Medical Center-North Little Rock](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/baptist-health-medical-center-6710497) and [Interventional Surgery Institute of Little Rock](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ar/interventional-surgery-institute-of-little-rock-A9028279). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James M. Calhoun has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-calhoun-193582#expertise). Dr. James M. Calhoun accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-calhoun-193582#insurance). Dr. James M. Calhoun is highly recommended by patients.
