## Overview

Dr. Hayden M. Boyce is a neurosurgeon in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with [Corewell Health Grand Rapids Hospitals-Blodgett and Butterworth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-6440021). He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Hayden M. Boyce has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hayden-boyce-623575#expertise). Dr. Hayden M. Boyce accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hayden-boyce-623575#insurance).
