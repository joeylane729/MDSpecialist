## Overview

Dr. Peter V. Weber is a neurologist in Fort Lewis, Washington and is affiliated with [Wilcox Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/wilcox-memorial-hospital-6950190). He received his medical degree from A. T. Still University Kirksville College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter V. Weber has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-weber-205364#expertise). Dr. Peter V. Weber accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-weber-205364#insurance).
