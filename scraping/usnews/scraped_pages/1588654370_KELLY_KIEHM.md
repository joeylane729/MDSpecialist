## Overview

Dr. Kelly J. Kiehm is a neurosurgeon in Columbus, Ohio and is affiliated with [Mount Carmel East Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/mount-carmel-east-hospital-6418083).
