## Overview

Dr. Scott D. Parker is an infectious disease specialist in Huntsville, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Crestwood Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/crestwood-medical-center-6530521) and [Huntsville Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/huntsville-hospital-6530510).
