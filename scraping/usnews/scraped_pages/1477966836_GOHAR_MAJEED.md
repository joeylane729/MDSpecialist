## Overview

Dr. Gohar Majeed is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [OHSU Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/oregon-health-and-science-university-6920570) and [HCA Florida North Florida Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/north-florida-regional-medical-center-6390225). He received his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine Bradenton Campus and has been in practice between 11-20 years.
