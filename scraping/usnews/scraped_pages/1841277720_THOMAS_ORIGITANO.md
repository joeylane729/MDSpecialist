## Overview

Dr. Thomas Origitano is a neurosurgeon in Kalispell, Montana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Logan Health-Kalispell](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mt/kalispell-regional-medical-center-6810007) and [St. Peter's Health-Helena](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mt/st-peters-hospital-6810330). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas Origitano has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-origitano-18770#expertise). Dr. Thomas Origitano accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-origitano-18770#insurance).
