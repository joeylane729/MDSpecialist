## Overview

Dr. Peter S. Kim is a cardiologist in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kaiser Permanente Sacramento Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-sacramento-medical-center-6932662) and [Goshen Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-goshen-hospital-6420505). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter S. Kim has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, aneurysm, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-618216#expertise). Dr. Peter S. Kim accepts Medicare, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-618216#insurance). Dr. Peter S. Kim is highly recommended by patients.
