## Overview

Dr. Gus G. Halamandaris is a neurosurgeon in Salinas, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Natividad Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/natividad-medical-center-6932720), [Salinas Valley Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/salinas-valley-memorial-hospital-6932745) and [Monterey Pennisula Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/monterey-pennisula-surgery-center-A9457957). He received his medical degree from University of Utah School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gus G. Halamandaris has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gus-halamandaris-486432#expertise). Dr. Gus G. Halamandaris accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gus-halamandaris-486432#insurance). Dr. Gus G. Halamandaris is highly recommended by patients.
