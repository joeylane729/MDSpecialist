## Overview

Dr. Justin Thomas is a neurosurgeon in Petoskey, Michigan and is affiliated with [McLaren Northern Michigan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/mclaren-northern-michigan-hospital-6442035). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Justin Thomas has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-thomas-913846#expertise). Dr. Justin Thomas accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-thomas-913846#insurance).
