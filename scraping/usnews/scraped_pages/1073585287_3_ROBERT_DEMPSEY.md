## Overview

Dr. Robert E. Dempsey is a psychiatrist in Paw Paw, Michigan and is affiliated with [Bronson South Haven Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/south-haven-health-system-6442360). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert E. Dempsey has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-dempsey-2673225#expertise). Dr. Robert E. Dempsey accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-dempsey-2673225#insurance).
