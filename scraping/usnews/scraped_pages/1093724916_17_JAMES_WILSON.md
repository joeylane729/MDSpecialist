## Overview

Dr. James W. Wilson is a pulmonologist in Plano, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Plano](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-6740945) and [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Allen](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-allen-6740206).
