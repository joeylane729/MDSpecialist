## Overview

Snyder is an orthopedist in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dupont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/dupont-hospital-6420033) and [Lutheran Hospital of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/lutheran-hospital-6420420).
