## Overview

Dr. William Bell is an emergency medicine physician in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Clair Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/st-clair-hospital-6232575) and [UPMC Passavant](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-passavant-6232540).
