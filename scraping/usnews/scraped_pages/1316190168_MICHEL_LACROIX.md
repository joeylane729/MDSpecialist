## Overview

Dr. Michel Lacroix is a neurosurgeon in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Geisinger Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/geisinger-medical-center-6230550) and [Geisinger Wyoming Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/geisinger-wyoming-valley-medical-center-6232725). He received his medical degree from Laval University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michel Lacroix has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michel-lacroix-810663#expertise). Dr. Michel Lacroix accepts Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michel-lacroix-810663#insurance). Dr. Michel Lacroix is highly recommended by patients.
