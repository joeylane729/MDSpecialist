## Overview

Dr. Andres Maldonado is a neurosurgeon in Peoria, Illinois and is affiliated with [OSF Healthcare St. Francis Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/osf-st-francis-medical-center-6432620). He received his medical degree from Universidad Central del Ecuador School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andres Maldonado has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andres-maldonado-2548668#expertise). Dr. Andres Maldonado accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andres-maldonado-2548668#insurance).
