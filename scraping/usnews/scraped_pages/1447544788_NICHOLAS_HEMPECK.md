## Overview

Dr. Nicholas Hempeck is a neurosurgeon in Summit, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Aurora Medical Center-Summit](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aurora-medical-center-summit-6450175) and [St. Francis-Emory Healthcare-Columbus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/st-francis-hospital-6380006). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Nicholas Hempeck has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicholas-hempeck-1206223#expertise). Dr. Nicholas Hempeck accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicholas-hempeck-1206223#insurance).
