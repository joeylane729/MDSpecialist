## Overview

Dr. Michael J. Alexander is a pulmonologist in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northwestern Medicine-Northwestern Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northwestern-memorial-hospital-6430545) and [Endeavor Health NorthShore Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northshore-evanston-hospital-6430021). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael J. Alexander has expertise in treating sleep apnea, pulmonary heart disease, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-alexander-1052341#expertise). Dr. Michael J. Alexander accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-alexander-1052341#insurance).
