## Overview

Dr. Michael J. Hartman is a doctor in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn State Health Holy Spirit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/holy-spirit-hospital-6230297) and [UPMC Harrisburg](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/pinnaclehealth-hospitals-6230039).
