## Overview

Dr. Ratnesh N. Mehra is a neurosurgeon in Southfield, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/beaumont-hospital-6442245), [Henry Ford St. John Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-john-hospital-and-medical-center-6440805) and [NOVI Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/novi-surgery-center-A3751048).
