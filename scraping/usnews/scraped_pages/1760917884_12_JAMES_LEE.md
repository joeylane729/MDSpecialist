## Overview

Dr. James M. Lee is an orthopedist in West Orange, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kessler Institute for Rehabilitation](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/kessler-institute-for-rehabilitation-6220253), [Cooperman Barnabas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/st-barnabas-medical-center-6220800) and [Harrison Endo Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nj/harrison-endo-surgical-center-A9530006). He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James M. Lee has expertise in treating arthritis, knee arthroscopy, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-lee-78648#expertise).
