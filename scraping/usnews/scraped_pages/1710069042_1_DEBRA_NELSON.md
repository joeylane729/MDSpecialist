## Overview

Dr. Debra Nelson is a neurosurgeon in Las Vegas, Nevada and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Spring Valley Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/spring-valley-hospital-medical-center-6880014) and [St. Rose Dominican Hospitals-San Martin Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/st-rose-domincian-hospitals-6880032). She received her medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Debra Nelson has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/debra-nelson-563565#expertise). Dr. Debra Nelson accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/debra-nelson-563565#insurance). Dr. Debra Nelson is highly recommended by patients.
