## Overview

Dr. Christopher L. Sneed is a neurosurgeon in Evansville, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Vincent Evansville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-marys-medical-center-6420027) and [Deaconess Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/deaconess-hospital-6420320).
