## Overview

Dr. Steve R. Klafeta is a neurosurgeon in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with [Trinity Health Grand Rapids Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-marys-health-care-6441210). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steve R. Klafeta has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steve-klafeta-432259#expertise).
