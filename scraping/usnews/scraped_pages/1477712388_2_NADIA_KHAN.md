## Overview

Dr. Nadia Khan is an oncologist in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with [Fox Chase Cancer Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/fox-chase-cancer-center-6231660). She received her medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Nadia Khan has expertise in treating lung cancer, chronic leukemia, lymphoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nadia-khan-679362#expertise). Dr. Nadia Khan accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nadia-khan-679362#insurance). Dr. Nadia Khan is highly recommended by patients.
