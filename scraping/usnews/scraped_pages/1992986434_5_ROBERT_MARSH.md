## Overview

Dr. Robert S. Marsh is an orthopedist in Tulsa, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Oklahoma Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/oklahoma-surgical-hospital-6730057) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215).
