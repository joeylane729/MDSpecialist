## Overview

Dr. Amrith Jamoona is a neurosurgeon in Modesto, California. He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Amrith Jamoona has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amrith-jamoona-709583#expertise). Dr. Amrith Jamoona accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amrith-jamoona-709583#insurance).
