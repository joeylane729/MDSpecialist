## Overview

Dr. Reza Shahim is a neurosurgeon in North Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arkansas Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-surgical-hospital-6710028) and [Arkansas Heart Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-heart-hospital-6710009). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Reza Shahim has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/reza-shahim-253982#expertise). Dr. Reza Shahim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/reza-shahim-253982#insurance).
