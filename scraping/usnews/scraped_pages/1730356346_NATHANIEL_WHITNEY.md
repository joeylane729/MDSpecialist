## Overview

Dr. Nathaniel Whitney is a neurosurgeon in Spokane, Washington and is affiliated with [Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center and Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/providence-sacred-heart-medical-center-and-childrens-hospital-6910980).
