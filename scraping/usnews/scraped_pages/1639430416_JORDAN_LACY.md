## Overview

Dr. Jordan P. Lacy is a neurosurgeon in Omaha, Nebraska and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Midwest Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/midwest-surgical-hospital-6660133) and [Cass Health-Atlantic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/cass-county-memorial-hospital-6620080).
