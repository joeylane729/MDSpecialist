## Overview

Dr. Shaheryar Ansari is a neurosurgeon in Lakewood, Colorado. He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Shaheryar Ansari has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shaheryar-ansari-5218944#expertise). Dr. Shaheryar Ansari accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shaheryar-ansari-5218944#insurance). Dr. Shaheryar Ansari is highly recommended by patients.
