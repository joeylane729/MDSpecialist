## Overview

Dr. Paul M. Schwartz is an internist in Delray Beach, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Delray Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/delray-medical-center-6390081) and [Baptist Health Boca Raton Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/boca-raton-regional-hospital-6390038). He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul M. Schwartz has expertise in treating obesity, bronchitis, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-schwartz-476187#expertise). Dr. Paul M. Schwartz accepts Medicare, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-schwartz-476187#insurance).
