## Overview

Dr. Kenneth Moore is a neurosurgeon in Indianapolis, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-academic-health-center-6420020) and [Eskenazi Health-Indianapolis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/wishard-eskenazi-hospital-6420620).
