## Overview

Dr. Jonathan Hobbs is a neurosurgeon in Chesterton, Indiana and is affiliated with [Northwest Health-Porter](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/porter-valparaiso-hospital-campus-6421390). He received his medical degree from University of Kentucky College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years.
