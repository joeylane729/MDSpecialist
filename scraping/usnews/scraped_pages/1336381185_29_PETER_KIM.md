## Overview

Dr. Peter Kim is an orthopedist in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with [Kaiser Permanente Baldwin Park Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-foundation-hospital-6930098). Dr. Peter Kim has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-1013273#expertise).
