## Overview

Dr. Amory J. Fiore is a neurosurgeon in Greenwich, Connecticut and is affiliated with [Stamford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/stamford-hospital-6160009). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Amory J. Fiore has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amory-fiore-25981#expertise).
