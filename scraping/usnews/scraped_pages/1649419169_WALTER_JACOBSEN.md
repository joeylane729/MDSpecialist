## Overview

Dr. Walter Jacobsen is a neurosurgeon in Milwaukee, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [Aurora St. Luke's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aurora-st-lukes-medical-center-6450017). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Walter Jacobsen has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/walter-jacobsen-1222573#expertise). Dr. Walter Jacobsen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/walter-jacobsen-1222573#insurance). Dr. Walter Jacobsen is highly recommended by patients.
