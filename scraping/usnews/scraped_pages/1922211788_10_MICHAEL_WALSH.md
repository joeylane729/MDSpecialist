## Overview

Dr. Michael B. Walsh is a rheumatologist in Madison, Wisconsin. He received his medical degree from Des Moines University College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael B. Walsh has expertise in treating arthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-walsh-306810#expertise). Dr. Michael B. Walsh is highly recommended by patients.
