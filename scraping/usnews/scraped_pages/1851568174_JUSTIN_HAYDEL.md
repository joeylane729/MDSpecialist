## Overview

Dr. Justin M. Haydel is a neurosurgeon in Metairie, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ochsner Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/ochsner-medical-center-6720631) and [Ochsner Medical Center-Baton Rouge](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/ochsner-medical-center-baton-rouge-6720122). He received his medical degree from LSU School of Medicine in Shreveport and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Justin M. Haydel has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-haydel-2527911#expertise). Dr. Justin M. Haydel accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-haydel-2527911#insurance). Dr. Justin M. Haydel is highly recommended by patients.
