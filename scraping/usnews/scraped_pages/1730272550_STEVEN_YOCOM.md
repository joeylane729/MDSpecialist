## Overview

Yocom is a neurosurgeon in Camden, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cooper University Health Care-Camden](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/cooper-university-hospital-6220190) and [Virtua Mount Holly Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/virtua-memorial-6220730).
