## Overview

Dr. Javed Khader Eliyas is a neurosurgeon in Hartford, Connecticut and is affiliated with [St. Francis Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/st-francis-hospital-and-medical-center-6160004). He received his medical degree from PSG Institute of Medical Sciences and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Javed Khader Eliyas has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/javed-khader-eliyas-691819#expertise). Dr. Javed Khader Eliyas accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/javed-khader-eliyas-691819#insurance). Dr. Javed Khader Eliyas is highly recommended by patients.
