## Overview

Dr. Christopher Roberts is an orthopedist in Venice, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Brooks Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/brooks-rehabilitation-hospital-6390309), [HCA Florida Sarasota Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/doctors-hospital-of-sarasota-6390956) and [Surgicare of St. Andrews](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/surgicare-of-st-andrews-A5856834).
