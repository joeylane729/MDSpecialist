## Overview

Dr. Mark W. Hawk is a neurosurgeon in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kaiser Permanente Sacramento Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-sacramento-medical-center-6932662) and [Kaiser Permanente South Sacramento Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-south-sacramento-medical-center-6932673). Hawk accepts Medicare, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-hawk-111101#insurance). Dr. Mark W. Hawk is highly recommended by patients.
