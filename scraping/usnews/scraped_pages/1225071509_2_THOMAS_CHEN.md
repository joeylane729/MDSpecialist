Dr. Thomas Chen is an infectious disease specialist in Manhasset, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [North Shore University Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/north-shore-university-hospital-6212357) and [Danbury Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/danbury-hospital-6160100).
