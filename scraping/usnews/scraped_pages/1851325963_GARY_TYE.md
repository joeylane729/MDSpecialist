## Overview

Dr. Gary Tye is a neurosurgeon in Richmond, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bon Secours St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/bon-secours-st-marys-hospital-6340921) and [Children's Hospital of Richmond at VCU](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/childrens-hospital-of-richmond-PA6340860). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gary Tye accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gary-tye-966192#insurance).
