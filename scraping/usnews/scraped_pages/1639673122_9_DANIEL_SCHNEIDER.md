## Overview

Schneider is a pathologist in Amarillo, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [BSA Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-st-anthonys-bsa-hospital-6740137) and [Northwest Texas Healthcare System-Amarillo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/northwest-texas-healthcare-6740065).
