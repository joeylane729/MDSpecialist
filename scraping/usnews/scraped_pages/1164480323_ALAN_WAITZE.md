## Overview

Dr. Alan S. Waitze is a neurosurgeon in Waterbury, Connecticut. He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Alan S. Waitze has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-waitze-1048#expertise). Dr. Alan S. Waitze accepts Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-waitze-1048#insurance).
