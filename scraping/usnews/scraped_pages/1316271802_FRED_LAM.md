## Overview

Dr. Fred C. Lam is a doctor in Stanford, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Stanford Health Care-Stanford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/stanford-hospital-and-clinics-6932330) and [Boston Medical Center-Brighton](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/st-elizabeths-medical-center-6140620). He received his medical degree from University of British Columbia Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Fred C. Lam has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fred-lam-1415067#expertise). Dr. Fred C. Lam accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fred-lam-1415067#insurance). Dr. Fred C. Lam is highly recommended by patients.
