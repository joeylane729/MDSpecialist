## Overview

Dr. Minh Nguyen is a gastroenterologist in Torrance, California and is affiliated with [Torrance Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/torrance-memorial-medical-center-6933679), [Endoscopy Center of The South Bay](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/endoscopy-center-of-the-south-bay-A5814583) and [Pacific Endo-Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/pacific-endo-surgical-center-A6225033). He received his medical degree from University of Vermont College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Minh Nguyen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/minh-nguyen-2460906#insurance).
