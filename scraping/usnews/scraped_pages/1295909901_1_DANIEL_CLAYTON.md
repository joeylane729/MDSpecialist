## Overview

Dr. Daniel B. Clayton is a pediatrician in South Jordan, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Intermountain Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/intermountain-medical-center-6870141) and [Intermountain Health Riverton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/riverton-hospital-6870124).
