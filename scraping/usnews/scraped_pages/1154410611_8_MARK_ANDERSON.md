## Overview

Dr. Mark I. Anderson is an urologist in Puyallup, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Madigan Army Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/madigan-healthcare-system-6911030) and [MultiCare Tacoma General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/tacoma-general-hospital-6911018). He received his medical degree from West Virginia University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark I. Anderson has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-anderson-266924#expertise). Dr. Mark I. Anderson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-anderson-266924#insurance). Dr. Mark I. Anderson is highly recommended by patients.
