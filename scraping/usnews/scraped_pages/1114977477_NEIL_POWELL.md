## Overview

Dr. Neil G. Powell is a neurosurgeon in Port Charlotte, Florida and is affiliated with [AdventHealth Ocala](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/munroe-regional-medical-center-6390670). He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Neil G. Powell has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neil-powell-345437#expertise). Dr. Neil G. Powell accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neil-powell-345437#insurance). Dr. Neil G. Powell is highly recommended by patients.
