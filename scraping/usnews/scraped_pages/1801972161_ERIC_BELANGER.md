## Overview

Dr. Eric Belanger is a neurosurgeon in Bismarck, North Dakota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHI St. Alexius Health Bismarck](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nd/st-alexius-medical-center-6640040) and [Sanford Medical Center Bismarck](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nd/sanford-medical-center-6640020). He received his medical degree from University of Laval Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Eric Belanger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-belanger-61688#expertise). Dr. Eric Belanger accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-belanger-61688#insurance).
