Gupta is a radiologist in Detroit, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Holy Family Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/holy-family-medical-center-6431443) and [Resurrection Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/resurrection-medical-center-6431020).
