## Overview

Dr. Mark D. Smith is an ophthalmologist in San Diego, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sharp Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sharp-memorial-hospital-6932785), [Tri-City Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/tri-city-medical-center-6932267) and [Scripps Mercy Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/scripps-mercy-ambulatory-surgery-center-A9348003).
