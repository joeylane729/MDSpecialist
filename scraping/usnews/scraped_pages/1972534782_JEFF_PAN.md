## Overview

Dr. Jeff Pan is a neurosurgeon in Edison, New Jersey and is affiliated with [JFK University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/jfk-medical-center-6220247). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeff Pan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeff-pan-432245#expertise). Dr. Jeff Pan accepts Medicare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeff-pan-432245#insurance).
