## Overview

Dr. Duncan K. Fischer is a neurosurgeon in San Angelo, Texas. He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Duncan K. Fischer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/duncan-fischer-260411#expertise). Dr. Duncan K. Fischer accepts Medicare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/duncan-fischer-260411#insurance). Dr. Duncan K. Fischer is highly recommended by patients.
