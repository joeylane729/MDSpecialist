## Overview

Brown is an anesthesiologist in Fredericksburg, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mary Washington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/mary-washington-hospital-6340290) and [UVA Health System Culpeper Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/culpeper-regional-hospital-6340195).
