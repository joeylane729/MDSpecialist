## Overview

Dr. Donald R. Smith is a neurosurgeon in Shreveport, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Health Shreveport-Bossier](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/christus-health-shreveport-bossier-6720088) and [Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport Academic Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/interim-lsu-public-hospital-6720776).
