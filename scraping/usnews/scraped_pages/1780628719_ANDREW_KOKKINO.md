## Overview

Dr. Andrew J. Kokkino is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [PeaceHealth Sacred Heart Medical Center at RiverBend](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/peacehealth-sacred-heart-medical-center-at-riverbend-6920051), [Providence Portland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-portland-medical-center-6920520) and [Riverbend Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/riverbend-ambulatory-surgery-center-A8621346).
