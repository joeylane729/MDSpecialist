## Overview

Dr. David Baker is a neurologist in Carlsbad, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [Palomar Medical Center Escondido](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/palomar-medical-center-6930660). He received his medical degree from College of Osteopathic Medicine of the Pacific and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. David Baker has expertise in treating dementia, Alzheimer's dementia, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-baker-2534867#expertise). Dr. David Baker accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-baker-2534867#insurance).
