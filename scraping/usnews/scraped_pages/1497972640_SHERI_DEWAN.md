## Overview

Dr. Sheri Dewan is a neurosurgeon in Geneva, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northwestern Medicine Delnor Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/cadence-health-delnor-hospital-6431765) and [Northwestern Medicine Central DuPage Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/cadence-health-central-dupage-hospital-6430019). She received her medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
