## Overview

Mitchell is a neurosurgeon in Norfolk, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hospital for Extended Recovery](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/hospital-for-extended-recovery-6340022) and [Sentara Norfolk General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sentara-norfolk-general-hospital-6340620).
