## Overview

Dr. James Evans is an emergency medicine physician in Fairfax, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Inova Fair Oaks Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-fair-oaks-hospital-6340231) and [Carolina Pines Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/carolina-pines-regional-medical-center-6370380).
