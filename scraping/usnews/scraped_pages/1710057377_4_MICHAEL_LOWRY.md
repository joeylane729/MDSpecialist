## Overview

Dr. Michael H. Lowry is an emergency medicine physician in Edenton, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ECU Health Chowan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/vidant-chowan-hospital-6360445) and [Carteret Health Care Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carteret-general-hospital-6360992).
