## Overview

Dr. Anthony L. Capocelli is a neurosurgeon in Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Medical Center-Little Rock](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/baptist-health-medical-center-6710335) and [Mercy Hospital Fort Smith](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/mercy-hospital-fort-smith-6710210).
