## Overview

Galgano is a neurosurgeon in Chapel Hill, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UNC Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/university-of-north-carolina-hospitals-6360260) and [Carthage Area Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/carthage-area-hospital-6211205).
