## Overview

Dr. Thomas Lee is a physiatrist in Baltimore, Maryland and is affiliated with [Sinai Hospital of Baltimore](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/sinai-hospital-of-baltimore-6320280) and [Surgicenter of Baltimore](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/surgicenter-of-baltimore-A8787413). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas Lee has expertise in treating arthritis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-lee-2391267#expertise). Dr. Thomas Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-lee-2391267#insurance).
