## Overview

Dr. Neel Patel is a dermatologist in Phoenix, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HonorHealth Scottsdale Osborn Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/scottsdale-healthcare-osborn-medical-center-6860376) and [HonorHealth Scottsdale Shea Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/scottsdale-healthcare-shea-medical-center-6860395).
