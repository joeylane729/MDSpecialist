## Overview

Dr. Robert Nudelman is a pediatric infectious disease specialist in Westlake Village, California and is affiliated with [Los Robles Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/los-robles-hospital-and-medical-center-6933672). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert Nudelman accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-nudelman-51052#insurance).
