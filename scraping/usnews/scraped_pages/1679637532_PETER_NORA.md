## Overview

Dr. Peter C. Nora is a neurosurgeon in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Swedish First Hill Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/swedish-medical-center-first-hill-6910006) and [Swedish Medical Center-Cherry Hill](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/swedish-medical-center-cherry-hill-6910680). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter C. Nora has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-nora-31426#expertise). Dr. Peter C. Nora accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-nora-31426#insurance). Dr. Peter C. Nora is highly recommended by patients.
