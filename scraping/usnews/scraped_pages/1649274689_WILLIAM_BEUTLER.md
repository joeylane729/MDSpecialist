## Overview

Dr. William J. Beutler is a neurosurgeon in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UPMC Harrisburg](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/pinnaclehealth-hospitals-6230039) and [Penn State Health Holy Spirit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/holy-spirit-hospital-6230297). He received his medical degree from University at Buffalo School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William J. Beutler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-beutler-128823#expertise). Dr. William J. Beutler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-beutler-128823#insurance). Dr. William J. Beutler is highly recommended by patients.
