## Overview

Dr. Eric B. Thompson is a gastroenterologist in Charlotte, North Carolina. He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Eric B. Thompson has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-thompson-455892#expertise).
