## Overview

Dr. Luis A. Ramos is a neurosurgeon in Oldsmar, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mease Dunedin Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/mease-hospital-6390170) and [Morton Plant Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/morton-plant-hospital-6390090).
