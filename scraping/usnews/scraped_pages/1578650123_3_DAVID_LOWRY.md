## Overview

Dr. David M. Lowry is a physiatrist in Chattanooga, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHI Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/memorial-health-care-6520019) and [Erlanger Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/erlanger-medical-center-6520065).
