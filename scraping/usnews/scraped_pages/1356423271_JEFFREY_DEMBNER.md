## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey M. Dembner is a neurosurgeon in Orange, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/hoag-memorial-hospital-presbyterian-6932115) and [St. Peter's Hospital-Albany](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-peters-hospital-6210070). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey M. Dembner has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-dembner-79524#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey M. Dembner accepts Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-dembner-79524#insurance).
