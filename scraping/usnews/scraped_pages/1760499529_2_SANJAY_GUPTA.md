## Overview

Dr. Sanjay Gupta is a psychiatrist in Cheektowaga, New York and is affiliated with [BryLin Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/brylin-hospitals-6211095). He received his medical degree from Maulana Azad Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sanjay Gupta has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-gupta-338103#expertise). Dr. Sanjay Gupta accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-gupta-338103#insurance).
