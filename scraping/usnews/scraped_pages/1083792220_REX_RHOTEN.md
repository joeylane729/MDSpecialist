## Overview

Dr. Rex L. Rhoten is a neurosurgeon in Encinitas, California and is affiliated with [Alvarado Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alvarado-hospital-medical-center-6932779). He received his medical degree from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center at Lubbock and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Rex L. Rhoten has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rex-rhoten-71697#expertise). Dr. Rex L. Rhoten accepts Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rex-rhoten-71697#insurance). Dr. Rex L. Rhoten is highly recommended by patients.
