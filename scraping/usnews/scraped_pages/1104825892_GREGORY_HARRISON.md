## Overview

Dr. Gregory S. Harrison is a neurosurgeon in Pocatello, Idaho. He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory S. Harrison has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-harrison-149956#expertise). Dr. Gregory S. Harrison accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-harrison-149956#insurance).
