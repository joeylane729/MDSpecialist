## Overview

Dr. Arthur J. Ulm is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with [TriStar Centennial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/centennial-medical-center-6520002). He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Arthur J. Ulm has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arthur-ulm-865799#expertise). Dr. Arthur J. Ulm accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arthur-ulm-865799#insurance).
