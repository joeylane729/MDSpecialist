## Overview

Dr. John C. Yu is a pulmonologist in Sioux Falls, South Dakota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Royal C. Johnson VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sd/sioux-falls-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6650425) and [Sanford USD Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sd/sanford-usd-medical-center-6650007). He received his medical degree from University of Santo Tomas and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John C. Yu has expertise in treating sleep apnea, pulmonary heart disease, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-yu-141179#expertise). Dr. John C. Yu accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-yu-141179#insurance).
