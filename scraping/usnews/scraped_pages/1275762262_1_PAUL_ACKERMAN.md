## Overview

Dr. Paul D. Ackerman is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Resurrection Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/resurrection-medical-center-6431020) and [Ascension Alexian Brothers Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/alexian-brothers-medical-center-6431613). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Paul D. Ackerman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-ackerman-714241#expertise). Dr. Paul D. Ackerman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-ackerman-714241#insurance).
