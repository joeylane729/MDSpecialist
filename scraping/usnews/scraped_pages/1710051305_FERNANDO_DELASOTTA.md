## Overview

Dr. Fernando J. Delasotta is a neurosurgeon in Linwood, New Jersey and is affiliated with [Shore Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/shore-memorial-hospital-6221330). He received his medical degree from Pontifical Catholic University Mother and Teacher and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Fernando J. Delasotta has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fernando-delasotta-71467#expertise). Dr. Fernando J. Delasotta accepts Medicare, Aetna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fernando-delasotta-71467#insurance).
