## Overview

Dr. David L. Yeh is an ophthalmologist in Darnestown, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adventist Healthcare Shady Grove Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/shady-grove-adventist-hospital-6320775), [Frederick Health Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/frederick-memorial-hospital-6320540) and [Montgomery Surgery Center-Rockville](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/montgomery-surgery-center-rockville-A7601150). He received his medical degree from University of Virginia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David L. Yeh has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, shingles, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-yeh-940#expertise). Dr. David L. Yeh accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-yeh-940#insurance).
