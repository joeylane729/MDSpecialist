## Overview

Dr. Sarah I. Woodrow is a neurosurgeon in Kansas City, Kansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Kansas Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/university-of-kansas-hospital-6670510) and [Cleveland Clinic Akron General](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/akron-general-medical-center-6410010). She received her medical degree from University of Toronto Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sarah I. Woodrow has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sarah-woodrow-716141#expertise). Dr. Sarah I. Woodrow accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sarah-woodrow-716141#insurance). Dr. Sarah I. Woodrow is highly recommended by patients.
