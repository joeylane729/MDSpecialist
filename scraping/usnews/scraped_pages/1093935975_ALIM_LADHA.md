## Overview

Dr. Alim M. Ladha is a neurosurgeon in Odessa, Texas and is affiliated with [Midland Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/midland-memorial-hospital-6740030). He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Alim M. Ladha has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alim-ladha-611302#expertise). Dr. Alim M. Ladha accepts Medicare, Aetna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alim-ladha-611302#insurance).
