## Overview

Dr. Mark Weissman is an emergency medicine physician in Marinette, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Aurora Medical Center-Bay Area](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/bay-area-medical-center-6450850) and [Froedtert South-Kenosha Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/united-hospitals-kenosha-medical-center-campus-6450610). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark Weissman accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-weissman-223136#insurance).
