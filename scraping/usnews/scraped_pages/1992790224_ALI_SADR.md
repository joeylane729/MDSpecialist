## Overview

Dr. Ali Sadr is a neurosurgeon in Brooklyn, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [NYC Health and Hospitals-Kings County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/kings-county-hospital-center-6210690) and [NYC Health and Hospitals-South Brooklyn Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/coney-island-hospital-6210570). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ali Sadr accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-sadr-180312#insurance).
