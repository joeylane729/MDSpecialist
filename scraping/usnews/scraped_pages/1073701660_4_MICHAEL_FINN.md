## Overview

Dr. Michael C. Finn is a cardiologist in Slidell, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Slidell Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/slidell-memorial-hospital-6720876) and [Ochsner Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/ochsner-medical-center-6720631). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael C. Finn has expertise in treating cardiomyopathy, coronary artery disease, heart failure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-finn-213687#expertise).
