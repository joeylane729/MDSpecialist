## Overview

Dr. Jane Ng is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Providence St. Vincent Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-st-vincent-medical-center-6920540) and [Providence Willamette Falls Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-willamette-falls-medical-center-6920350). She received her medical degree from Royal Free Hospital and University College London Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jane Ng has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jane-ng-823849#expertise). Dr. Jane Ng accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jane-ng-823849#insurance).
