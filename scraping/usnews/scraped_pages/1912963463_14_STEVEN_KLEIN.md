## Overview

Dr. Steven M. Klein is an orthopedist in La Crosse, Wisconsin. He received his medical degree from Medical College of Wisconsin Affiliated Hospitals, Inc and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven M. Klein has expertise in treating arthritis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-klein-404903#expertise). Dr. Steven M. Klein accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-klein-404903#insurance). Dr. Steven M. Klein is highly recommended by patients.
