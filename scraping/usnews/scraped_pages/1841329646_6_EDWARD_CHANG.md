## Overview

Dr. Edward J. Chang is an ophthalmologist in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [UPMC Mercy](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-mercy-6232510) and [East Side Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/pa/east-side-surgery-center-A3555707).
