## Overview

Dr. Michael Chan is a neurosurgeon in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mercy General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-general-hospital-6932670) and [Sutter Medical Center-Sacramento](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sutter-medical-center-6930073). Dr. Michael Chan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-chan-809864#expertise).
