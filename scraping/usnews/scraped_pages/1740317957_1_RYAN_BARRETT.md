## Overview

Dr. Ryan Barrett is a neurosurgeon in Southfield, Michigan and is affiliated with [McLaren Lapeer Region Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/lapeer-regional-medical-center-6441640) and [Lapeer County Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/lapeer-county-surgery-center-A6036502). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ryan Barrett has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ryan-barrett-2521439#expertise). Dr. Ryan Barrett accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ryan-barrett-2521439#insurance). Dr. Ryan Barrett is highly recommended by patients.
