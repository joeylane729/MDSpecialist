## Overview

Dr. Jean Louis Benae is a neurosurgeon in McKinney, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Medical City McKinney](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/medical-center-of-mckinney-6740025), [Methodist McKinney Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-mckinney-hospital-6740654) and [Wellness Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/wellness-ambulatory-surgery-center-A3726887).
