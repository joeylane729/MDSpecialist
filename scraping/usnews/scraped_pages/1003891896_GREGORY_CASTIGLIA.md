## Overview

Dr. Gregory J. Castiglia is a neurosurgeon in Williamsville, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Erie County Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/erie-county-medical-center-6211070) and [Kaleida Health-Buffalo General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/buffalo-general-medical-center-6211025). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory J. Castiglia has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-castiglia-230263#expertise). Dr. Gregory J. Castiglia accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-castiglia-230263#insurance).
