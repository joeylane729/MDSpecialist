## Overview

Dr. Paul C. Peterson is a neurosurgeon in Knoxville, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Covenant Health Fort Sanders Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/fort-sanders-regional-medical-center-6520510). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul C. Peterson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-peterson-181892#expertise). Dr. Paul C. Peterson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-peterson-181892#insurance).
