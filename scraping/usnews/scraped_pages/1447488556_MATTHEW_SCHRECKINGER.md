## Overview

Dr. Matthew Schreckinger is a neurosurgeon in Santa Maria, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Marian Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marian-regional-medical-center-6933440) and [French Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/french-hospital-medical-center-6933270). He received his medical degree from UMass Chan Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew Schreckinger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-schreckinger-712460#expertise). Dr. Matthew Schreckinger accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-schreckinger-712460#insurance).
