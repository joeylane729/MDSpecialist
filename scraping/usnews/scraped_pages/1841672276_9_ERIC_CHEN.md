## Overview

Dr. Eric Chen is an orthopedist in Wexford, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Independence Health System Butler Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/butler-hospital-6230270) and [UPMC Passavant](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-passavant-6232540). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Eric Chen has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-chen-1167493#expertise). Dr. Eric Chen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-chen-1167493#insurance).
