## Overview

Dr. Steven R. Abram is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Thomas Hospital for Specialty Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/center-for-spinal-surgery-6520032) and [Ascension St. Thomas Hospital-West](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/st-thomas-hospital-6521040). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven R. Abram has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-abram-252690#expertise). Dr. Steven R. Abram accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-abram-252690#insurance).
