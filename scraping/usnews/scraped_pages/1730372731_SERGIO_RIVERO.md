## Overview

Dr. Sergio Rivero is a neurosurgeon in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Banner-University Medical Center Tucson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/university-of-arizona-medical-center-6860516) and [TMC Healthcare-Tucson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/tucson-medical-center-6860020). He received his medical degree from Universidad Automoma de Guadalajara and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sergio Rivero has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sergio-rivero-648258#expertise). Dr. Sergio Rivero accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sergio-rivero-648258#insurance). Dr. Sergio Rivero is highly recommended by patients.
