## Overview

Dr. David Phillips is a resident physician in Santa Rosa, New Mexico and is affiliated with [Guadalupe County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/guadalupe-county-hospital-6850425). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Georgia and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. David Phillips has expertise in treating bursitis, anxiety disorders, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-phillips-2415474#expertise). Dr. David Phillips accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-phillips-2415474#insurance).
