## Overview

Dr. Yehuda Herschman is a neurosurgeon in Lake Worth, Florida and is affiliated with [HCA Florida JFK Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/jfk-medical-center-6390074) and [Orthopedic Surgery Center of Palm Beach County](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/orthopedic-surgery-center-of-palm-beach-county-A4358141). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Yehuda Herschman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yehuda-herschman-1058586#expertise). Dr. Yehuda Herschman accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yehuda-herschman-1058586#insurance). Dr. Yehuda Herschman is highly recommended by patients.
