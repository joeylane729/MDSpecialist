## Overview

Dr. Gregory Ward is a physiatrist in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baton Rouge General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-general-medical-center-6720042) and [Baton Rouge Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-rehabilitation-hospital-6720005).
