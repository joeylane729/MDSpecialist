## Overview

Dr. Mark A. Thompson is a cardiologist in Gastonia, North Carolina and is affiliated with [CaroMont Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/gaston-memorial-hospital-6360580). He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark A. Thompson has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-thompson-2799#expertise). Dr. Mark A. Thompson is highly recommended by patients.
