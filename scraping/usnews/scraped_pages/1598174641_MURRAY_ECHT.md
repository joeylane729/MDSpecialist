## Overview

Dr. Murray Echt is a neurosurgeon in Lebanon, New Hampshire and is affiliated with [Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nh/dartmouth-hitchcock-medical-center-6120170).
