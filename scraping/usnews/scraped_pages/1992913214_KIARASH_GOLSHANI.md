## Overview

Dr. Kiarash Golshani is a neurosurgeon in Orange, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHOC Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/childrens-hospital-of-orange-county-6932287) and [UC Irvine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/university-of-california-irvine-medical-center-6932290).
