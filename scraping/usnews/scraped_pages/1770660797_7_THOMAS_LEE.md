## Overview

Dr. Thomas Lee is a pediatric (general) surgery doctor in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Chicago Comer Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/university-of-chicago-comer-childrens-hospital-PA6431210) and [University of Chicago Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/university-of-chicago-medical-center-6431210).
