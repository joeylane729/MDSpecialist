## Overview

Dr. Thomas C. Sanchez is an anesthesiologist in Lihue, Hawaii and is affiliated with [Wilcox Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/wilcox-memorial-hospital-6950190). He received his medical degree from University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas C. Sanchez has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-sanchez-538846#expertise). Dr. Thomas C. Sanchez accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-sanchez-538846#insurance).
