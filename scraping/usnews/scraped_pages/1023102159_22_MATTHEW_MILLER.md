## Overview

Dr. Matthew Miller is an orthopedist in Tupelo, Mississippi and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [North Mississippi Medical Center-Tupelo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/north-mississippi-medical-center-6540810), [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Union County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/baptist-memorial-hospital-union-county-6540657) and [Special Orthopedic Group Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ms/special-orthopedic-group-surgery-center-A9591210). He received his medical degree from University of Mississippi School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew Miller has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-miller-1518699#expertise). Dr. Matthew Miller accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-miller-1518699#insurance).
