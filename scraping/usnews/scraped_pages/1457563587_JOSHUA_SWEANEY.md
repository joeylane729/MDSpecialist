## Overview

Dr. Joshua C. Sweaney is a neurosurgeon in Joplin, Missouri and is affiliated with [Freeman Health System-Joplin](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/freeman-hospital-6630008). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Joshua C. Sweaney has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-sweaney-864225#expertise). Dr. Joshua C. Sweaney accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-sweaney-864225#insurance).
