## Overview

Dr. Baraa Al-Hafez is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890) and [St. Luke's Health Memorial Lufkin](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-medical-center-lufkin-6742395).
