## Overview

Dr. Igor Fineman is a neurosurgeon in Pasadena, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [City of Hope Comprehensive Cancer Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/city-of-hope-6930590) and [Huntington Health Medical Center-Pasadena](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/huntington-memorial-hospital-6932350).
