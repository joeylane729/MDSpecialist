## Overview

Dr. James W. Walker is an internist in Oneonta, New York and is affiliated with [Bassett Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/bassett-medical-center-6210001). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Georgia and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James W. Walker has expertise in treating dementia, coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-walker-878891#expertise).
