## Overview

Dr. Robert H. Graham is an ophthalmologist in Scottsdale, Arizona and is affiliated with [Mayo Clinic-Arizona](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/mayo-clinic-6860019). He received his medical degree from McGovern Medical School at UTHealth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert H.
