## Overview

Dr. Bikash Bose is a neurosurgeon in Newark, Delaware and is affiliated with [St. Francis Hospital-Wilmington](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/st-francis-hospital-6310140). He received his medical degree from Maulana Azad Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bikash Bose has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bikash-bose-543700#expertise).
