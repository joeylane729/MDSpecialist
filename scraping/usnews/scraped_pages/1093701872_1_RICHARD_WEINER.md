## Overview

Dr. Richard L. Weiner is a neurosurgeon in Plano, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Plano](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baylor-regional-medical-center-6740259) and [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-6741050).
