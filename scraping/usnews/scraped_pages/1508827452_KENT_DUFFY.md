Dr. Kent Duffy is a neurosurgeon in Chappaqua, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northern Westchester Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/northern-westchester-hospital-6212500) and [Phelps Memorial Hospital Center at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/phelps-memorial-hospital-center-6213870).
