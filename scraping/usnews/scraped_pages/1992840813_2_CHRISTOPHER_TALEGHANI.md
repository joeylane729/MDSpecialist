## Overview

Dr. Christopher Taleghani is a neurosurgeon in Hermitage, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [TriStar Summit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/summit-medical-center-6520043) and [Maui Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/maui-memorial-medical-center-6950290). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher Taleghani has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-taleghani-589481#expertise).
