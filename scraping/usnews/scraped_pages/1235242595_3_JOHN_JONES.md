## Specialties

Specialty

### Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons specialize in surgeries for the face, mouth, and jaws. These physicians treat jaw, cheek, and nasal fractures, as well as temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorder and incorrect bites. Some procedures these physicians perform include wisdom teeth removal and corrective jaw surgery.
