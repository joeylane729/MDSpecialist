## Overview

Dr. Stephen Smith is a family medicine doctor in Escanaba, Michigan and is affiliated with [OSF St. Francis Hospital and Medical Group](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/osf-st-francis-hospital-6441000). He received his medical degree from American University of Integrative Sciences and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Stephen Smith has expertise in treating inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-smith-1004173#expertise). ### Family Medicine

Family medicine doctors, also known as primary care physicians or PCPs, are trained to meet the basic health needs of children and adults. They often refer patients to specialists or for further testing, and they treat common conditions such as diabetes, high cholesterol and allergies. They also perform physicals, give vaccines and may do some in-office lab testing.
