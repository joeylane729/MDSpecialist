## Overview

Dr. Stephen C. Smith is an internist in Huntington, West Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cabell Huntington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/cabell-huntington-hospital-6350345) and [St. Mary's Medical Center-Huntington](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/st-marys-medical-center-6350400).
