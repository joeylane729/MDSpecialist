## Overview

Dr. John M. Wilson is a psychiatrist in Lynchburg, Virginia and is affiliated with [Centra Specialty Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/center-for-restorative-care-and-rehabilitation-6340225). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John M. Wilson has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-wilson-159808#expertise). Dr. John M. Wilson accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-wilson-159808#insurance). Dr. John M. Wilson is highly recommended by patients.
