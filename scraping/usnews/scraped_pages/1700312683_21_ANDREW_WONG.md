## Overview

Dr. Andrew L. Wong is an internist in Bethesda, Maryland and is affiliated with [Johns Hopkins Medicine-Suburban Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/suburban-hospital-6320350). He received his medical degree from Tufts University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andrew L. Wong has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, multiple sclerosis, rheumatoid arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-wong-588735#expertise). Dr. Andrew L. Wong accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-wong-588735#insurance).
