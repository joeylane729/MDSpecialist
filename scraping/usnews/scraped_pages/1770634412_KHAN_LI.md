## Overview

Dr. Khan W. Li is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Thomas Hospital-West](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/st-thomas-hospital-6521040) and [TriStar Centennial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/centennial-medical-center-6520002).
