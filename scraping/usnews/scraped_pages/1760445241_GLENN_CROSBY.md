## Overview

Dr. Glenn A. Crosby is a neurosurgeon in Memphis, Tennessee. He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Glenn A. Crosby has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/glenn-crosby-313293#expertise). Dr. Glenn A. Crosby is highly recommended by patients.
