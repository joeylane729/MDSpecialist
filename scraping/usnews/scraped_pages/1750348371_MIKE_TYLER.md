## Overview

Dr. Mike O. Tyler is a neurosurgeon in Ladson, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Trident Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/trident-medical-center-6370232) and [Bon Secours St. Francis Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/bon-secours-st-francis-hospital-6370100). He received his medical degree from Medical University of South Carolina College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mike O. Tyler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mike-tyler-860089#expertise). Dr. Mike O. Tyler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mike-tyler-860089#insurance). Dr. Mike O. Tyler is highly recommended by patients.
