## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey J. Laurent is a neurosurgeon in Virginia Beach, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Chesapeake Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/chesapeake-regional-medical-center-6340145), [Sentara Leigh Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sentara-leigh-hospital-6340580) and [Chesapeake Regional Surgery Center at Virginia Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/va/chesapeake-regional-surgery-center-at-virginia-beach-A2589795). He received his medical degree from University of Virginia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey J. Laurent has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-laurent-315364#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey J. Laurent accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-laurent-315364#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey J. Laurent is highly recommended by patients.
