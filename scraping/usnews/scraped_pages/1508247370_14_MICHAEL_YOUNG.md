## Overview

Dr. Michael C. Young is an allergist-immunologist in South Weymouth, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [South Shore Hospital-South Weymouth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/south-shore-hospital-6141900) and [Boston Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/boston-childrens-hospital-6140270). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael C. Young has expertise in treating asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-young-196244#expertise). Dr. Michael C. Young accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-young-196244#insurance).
