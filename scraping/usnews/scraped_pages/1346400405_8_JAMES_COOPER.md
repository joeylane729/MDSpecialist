## Overview

Dr. James K. Cooper is a neurosurgeon in Temple, Texas and is affiliated with [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Temple](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/scott-and-white-healthcare-6743413). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James K. Cooper has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-cooper-2936706#expertise).
