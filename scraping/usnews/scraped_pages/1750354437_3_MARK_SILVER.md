## Overview

Dr. Mark Silver is a neurosurgeon in Sherman, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Wilson N. Jones Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-wnj-6743240), [Hunt Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hunt-regional-medical-center-6740713) and [Wellness Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/wellness-ambulatory-surgery-center-A3726887). He received his medical degree from Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark Silver has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-silver-1388441#expertise). Dr. Mark Silver accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-silver-1388441#insurance).
