## Overview

Dr. Andrew S. Zelby is a neurosurgeon in Westchester, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rush University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-university-medical-center-6430985), [Endeavor Health Elmhurst Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/elmhurst-memorial-hospital-6431620) and [Elmhurst Outpatient Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/elmhurst-outpatient-surgery-center-A6129062).
