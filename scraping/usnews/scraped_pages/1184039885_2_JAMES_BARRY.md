## Overview

Dr. James Barry is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [TriStar Skyline Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/tristar-skyline-medical-center-6521020) and [Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/baptist-medical-center-6390308). He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James Barry has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-barry-1068471#expertise). Dr. James Barry accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-barry-1068471#insurance).
