## Overview

Dr. Brandon L. Davis is an ophthalmologist in Metairie, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [East Jefferson General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/east-jefferson-general-hospital-6720418) and [Bridgepoint Continuing Care Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/louisiana-specialty-hospital-6720072).
