## Overview

Dr. Bret G. Ball is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adventist Heath Portland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/adventist-medical-center-6920510) and [Providence Portland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-portland-medical-center-6920520). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bret G. Ball has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bret-ball-50814#expertise). Dr. Bret G. Ball accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bret-ball-50814#insurance).
