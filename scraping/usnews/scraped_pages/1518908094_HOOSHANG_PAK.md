## Overview

Dr. Hooshang Pak is a neurosurgeon in Long Beach, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MemorialCare Long Beach Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/long-beach-memorial-medical-center-6931370) and [MemorialCare Miller Children's and Women's Hospital Long Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/miller-childrens-hospital-6930103).
