## Overview

Dr. Michael E. Kelly is a cardiologist in Richmond, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sheltering Arms Institute](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sheltering-arms-institute-6340435) and [VCU Health Tappahannock Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/riverside-tappahannock-hospital-6341161). He received his medical degree from Universidad de Guadalajara and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael E. Kelly has expertise in treating cardiomyopathy, heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kelly-3069725#expertise). Dr. Michael E. Kelly accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kelly-3069725#insurance). Dr. Michael E. Kelly is highly recommended by patients.
