## Overview

Dr. Howard Tung is a neurosurgeon in La Jolla, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Scripps La Jolla Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/scripps-la-jolla-hospitals-and-clinics-6931130) and [UC San Diego Health-La Jolla, Hillcrest, and East Campus Medical Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/uc-san-diego-medical-center-6932820). He received his medical degree from Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Howard Tung has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/howard-tung-7022#expertise). Dr. Howard Tung accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/howard-tung-7022#insurance).
