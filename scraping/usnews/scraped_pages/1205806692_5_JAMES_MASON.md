## Overview

Dr. James M. Mason is an internist in Fairport, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Highland Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/highland-hospital-6214240) and [Strong Memorial Hospital of the University of Rochester](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/university-of-rochester-medical-center-6214330). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James M. Mason has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-mason-879448#expertise). Dr. James M. Mason accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-mason-879448#insurance).
