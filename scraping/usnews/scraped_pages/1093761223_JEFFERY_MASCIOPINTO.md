## Overview

Dr. Jeffery E. Masciopinto is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [SSM Health St. Mary's Hospital-Madison](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-marys-hospital-6450810) and [SSM Health St. Clare Hospital-Baraboo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-clare-hospital-and-health-services-6450110). He received his medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffery E. Masciopinto has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffery-masciopinto-370363#expertise). Dr. Jeffery E. Masciopinto accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffery-masciopinto-370363#insurance). Dr. Jeffery E. Masciopinto is highly recommended by patients.
