## Overview

Dr. Richard Y. Chung is a neurosurgeon in Santa Barbara, California and is affiliated with [Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/santa-barbara-cottage-hospital-6933375) and [Santa Barbara Outpatient Surgery Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/santa-barbara-outpatient-surgery-centers-A6896406).
