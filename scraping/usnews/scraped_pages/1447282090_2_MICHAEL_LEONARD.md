## Overview

Dr. Michael A. Leonard is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Santa Rosa Health System-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/santa-rosa-hospital-6743120) and [Foundation Surgical Hospital of San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/foundation-surgical-hospital-of-san-antonio-6740395). He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael A. Leonard has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-leonard-432687#expertise). Dr. Michael A. Leonard accepts Medicare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-leonard-432687#insurance). Dr. Michael A. Leonard is highly recommended by patients.
