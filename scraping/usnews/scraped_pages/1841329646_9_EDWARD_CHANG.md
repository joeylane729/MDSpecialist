## Overview

Dr. Edward Chang is an internist in Cincinnati, Ohio. He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Edward Chang has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-chang-159729#expertise).
