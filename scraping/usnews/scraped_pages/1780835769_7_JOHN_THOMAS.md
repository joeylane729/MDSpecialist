## Overview

Dr. John Thomas is an orthopedist in Sarasota, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA Florida Sarasota Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/doctors-hospital-of-sarasota-6390956), [Lakewood Ranch Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/lakewood-ranch-medical-center-6390109), [Intercoastal Medical Group Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/intercoastal-medical-group-ambulatory-surgery-center-A4872357) and [Lincoln Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/co/lincoln-surgery-center-A6892456). He received his medical degree from Florida State University College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John Thomas has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-thomas-914110#expertise). Dr. John Thomas accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-thomas-914110#insurance). Dr. John Thomas is highly recommended by patients.
