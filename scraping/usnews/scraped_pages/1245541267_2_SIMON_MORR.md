## Overview

Dr. Simon Morr is a neurosurgeon in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westchester Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/westchester-medical-center-6215150) and [Pascack Valley Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/hackensack-university-medical-center-at-pascack-valley-6221618). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Simon Morr accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/simon-morr-3964863#insurance). Dr. Simon Morr is highly recommended by patients.
