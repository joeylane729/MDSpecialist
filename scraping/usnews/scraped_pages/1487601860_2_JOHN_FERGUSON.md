## Overview

Dr. John H. Ferguson is a pulmonologist in Wheat Ridge, Colorado and is affiliated with [Intermountain Health Lutheran Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/exempla-lutheran-medical-center-6840012).
