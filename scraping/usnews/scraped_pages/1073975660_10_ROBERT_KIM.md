## Overview

Dr. Robert Y. Kim is an interventional radiology doctor in Walnut Creek, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [John Muir Health-Walnut Creek Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/john-muir-medical-center-6933853) and [Alameda Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alameda-hospital-6930030). Kim accepts Medicare, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-kim-408289#insurance).
