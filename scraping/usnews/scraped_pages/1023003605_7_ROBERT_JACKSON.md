## Overview

Dr. Robert Jackson is a radiologist in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-medical-center-6740415) and [University Health-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/university-hospital-6743105).
