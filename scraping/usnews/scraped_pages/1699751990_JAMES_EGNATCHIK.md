## Overview

Dr. James Egnatchik is a neurosurgeon in West Seneca, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kaleida Health-Buffalo General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/buffalo-general-medical-center-6211025) and [Mercy Hospital-Buffalo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mercy-hospital-6211100).
