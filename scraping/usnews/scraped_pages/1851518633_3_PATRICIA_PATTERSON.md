## Overview

Dr. Patricia E. Patterson is a pulmonologist in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Alabama at Birmingham Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/university-of-alabama-hospital-at-birmingham-6530304) and [UAB St. Vincent's Birmingham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/st-vincents-birmingham-6530260). Patterson has expertise in treating sleep apnea, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patricia-patterson-391879#expertise). Dr. Patricia E.
