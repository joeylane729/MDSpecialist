## Overview

Dr. Jack Stern is a neurosurgeon in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westchester Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/westchester-medical-center-6215150) and [Northern Westchester Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/northern-westchester-hospital-6212500).
