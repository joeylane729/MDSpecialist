## Overview

Dr. Anthony J. Hall is a neurosurgeon in Hollywood, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Broward Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/broward-general-medical-center-6390210) and [Broward Health Imperial Point](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/broward-health-imperial-point-6390215).
