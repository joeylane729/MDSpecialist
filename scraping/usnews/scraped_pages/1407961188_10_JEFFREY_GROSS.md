## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey M. Gross is a physiatrist in New York, New York and is affiliated with [NYU Langone Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-langone-medical-center-6212925). He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey M. Gross has expertise in treating arthritis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-gross-162568#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey M. Gross accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-gross-162568#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey M. Gross is highly recommended by patients.
