Patel is a pulmonologist in Marietta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [WellStar Kennestone Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/kennestone-hospital-6380775) and [WellStar Windy Hill Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/wellstar-windy-hill-hospital-6380215).
