## Overview

Dr. Achilles K. Papavasiliou is a neurosurgeon in Hyannis, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Cape Cod Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/cape-cod-hospital-6141130). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Achilles K. Papavasiliou has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/achilles-papavasiliou-159768#expertise). Dr. Achilles K. Papavasiliou accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/achilles-papavasiliou-159768#insurance). Dr. Achilles K. Papavasiliou is highly recommended by patients.
