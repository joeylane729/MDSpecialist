## Overview

Dr. Brent P. Peterson is a neurosurgeon in Fayetteville, Arkansas and is affiliated with [Washington Regional Medical Center-Fayetteville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/washington-regional-medical-center-6710195). Peterson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brent-peterson-848314#expertise). Dr. Brent P.
