## Overview

Dr. Tuan V. Nguyen is a neurosurgeon in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with [Baptist Health Brookwood Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/brookwood-medical-center-6530175) and [Surgical Clinic Solutions](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/al/surgical-clinic-solutions-A9051301).
