## Overview

Dr. Timothy A. Lucas is a pulmonologist in Altoona, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [James E. Van Zandt Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/james-e-van-zandt-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6230075) and [UPMC Altoona](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/altoona-regional-hospital-6230086). He received his medical degree from Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy A. Lucas has expertise in treating pneumonia, sleep apnea, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-lucas-333349#expertise). Dr. Timothy A. Lucas accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-lucas-333349#insurance).
