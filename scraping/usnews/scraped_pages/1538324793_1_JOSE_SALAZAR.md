## Overview

Dr. Jose A. Salazar is a pulmonologist in Miami, Florida. He received his medical degree from Universidad de Zaragoza Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jose A. Salazar has expertise in treating bronchitis, pneumonia, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jose-salazar-187394#expertise). Dr. Jose A. Salazar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jose-salazar-187394#insurance). Dr. Jose A.
