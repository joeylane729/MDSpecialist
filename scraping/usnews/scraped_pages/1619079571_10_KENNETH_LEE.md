## Overview

Dr. Kenneth C. Lee is an ophthalmologist in Aiea, Hawaii. He received his medical degree from Harvard Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kenneth C. Lee has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kenneth-lee-563438#expertise). Dr. Kenneth C.
