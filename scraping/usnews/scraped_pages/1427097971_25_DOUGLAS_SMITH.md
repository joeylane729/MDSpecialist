Smith is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Long Beach, California and is affiliated with [MemorialCare Long Beach Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/long-beach-memorial-medical-center-6931370) and [Davita Medical ASC](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/davita-medical-asc-A7942955).
