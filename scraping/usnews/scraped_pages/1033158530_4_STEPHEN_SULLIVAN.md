## Overview

Dr. Stephen Sullivan is a neurosurgeon in Ann Arbor, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Michigan Health-Ann Arbor](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/university-of-michigan-hospitals-and-health-centers-6440110) and [Veterans Affairs Ann Arbor Healthcare System](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/va-ann-arbor-hospitals-6440115). He received his medical degree from University of Chicago Division of the Biological Sciences The Pritzker School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stephen Sullivan accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-sullivan-27485#insurance). Dr. Stephen Sullivan is highly recommended by patients.
