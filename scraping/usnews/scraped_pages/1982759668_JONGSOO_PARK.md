## Overview

Dr. Jongsoo Park is a neurosurgeon in Stanford, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Stanford Health Care-Stanford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/stanford-hospital-and-clinics-6932330) and [Lucile Packard Children's Hospital Stanford](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/lucile-salter-packard-childrens-hospital-at-stanford-6933605).
