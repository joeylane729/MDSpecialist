## Overview

Dr. Chanland Roonprapunt is a neurosurgeon in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mount Sinai Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mount-sinai-medical-center-6213140) and [Mount Sinai Morningside and Mount Sinai West Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-lukes-roosevelt-hospital-center-6213335).
