## Overview

Dr. Alexander Spiessberger is a doctor in Cleveland, Ohio and is affiliated with [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670). He received his medical degree from Leopold Franzen University Innsbruck Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Alexander Spiessberger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alexander-spiessberger-1498824#expertise). Dr. Alexander Spiessberger accepts Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alexander-spiessberger-1498824#insurance). Dr. Alexander Spiessberger is highly recommended by patients.
