## Overview

Dr. Kevin L. Stevenson is a neurosurgeon in Macon, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Piedmont Macon Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/coliseum-medical-center-6380715) and [Northside Hospital Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northside-hospital-6380171). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kevin L. Stevenson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kevin-stevenson-363504#expertise). Dr. Kevin L. Stevenson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kevin-stevenson-363504#insurance).
