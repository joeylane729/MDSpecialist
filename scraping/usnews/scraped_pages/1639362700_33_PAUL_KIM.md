## Overview

Dr. Paul H. Kim is an internist in White Plains, New York and is affiliated with [White Plains Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/white-plains-hospital-6215330). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul H. Kim has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, aneurysm, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-kim-84426#expertise).
