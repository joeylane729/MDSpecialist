## Overview

Dr. Paula Eboli is a neurosurgeon in Torrance, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Torrance Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/torrance-memorial-medical-center-6933679) and [Cedars-Sinai Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/cedars-sinai-medical-center-6930444). She received her medical degree from University of Buenos Aires and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paula Eboli has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paula-eboli-632073#expertise). Dr. Paula Eboli accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paula-eboli-632073#insurance).
