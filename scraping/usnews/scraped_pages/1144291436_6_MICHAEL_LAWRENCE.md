## Overview

Dr. Michael Lawrence is a geriatrician in Snow Hill, North Carolina. He received his medical degree from Instituto Superior de Ciencias Medicinas de Villa Clara and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael Lawrence has expertise in treating dementia, hypertension & high blood pressure, depression, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lawrence-282320#expertise). Dr. Michael Lawrence accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lawrence-282320#insurance).
