## Overview

Dr. Thomas F. Rapacki is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma. He received his medical degree from Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences F. Edward Hebert School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas F. Rapacki has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-rapacki-394242#expertise). Dr. Thomas F. Rapacki accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-rapacki-394242#insurance).
