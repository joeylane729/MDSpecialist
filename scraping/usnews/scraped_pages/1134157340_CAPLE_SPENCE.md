## Overview

Dr. Caple A. Spence is a neurosurgeon in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Oklahoma Heart Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/oklahoma-heart-hospital-6730054) and [Oklahoma Heart Hospital South Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/oklahoma-heart-hospital-south-campus-6730203).
