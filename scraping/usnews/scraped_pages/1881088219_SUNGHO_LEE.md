## Overview

Dr. Sungho Lee is a neurosurgeon in Shreveport, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Willis-Knighton Medical Center DBA Willis Knighton Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/willis-knighton-medical-center-6720877) and [North Caddo Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/north-caddo-medical-center-6720882). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Sungho Lee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sungho-lee-1384580#expertise). Dr. Sungho Lee accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sungho-lee-1384580#insurance).
