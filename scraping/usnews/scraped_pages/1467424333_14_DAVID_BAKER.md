## Overview

Dr. David Baker is a pediatrician in Lewiston, Maine and is affiliated with [Central Maine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/me/central-maine-medical-center-6110370). He received his medical degree from Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Baker accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-baker-885265#insurance).
