## Overview

Dr. George F. Gade is a neurosurgeon in Pensacola, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/baptist-hospital-6390792) and [Gulf Breeze Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/gulf-breeze-hospital-6390248).
