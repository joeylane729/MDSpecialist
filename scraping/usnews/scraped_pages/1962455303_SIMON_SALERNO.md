## Overview

Salerno is a neurosurgeon in Neptune, New Jersey and is affiliated with [Jersey Shore University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/jersey-shore-university-medical-center-6220740).
