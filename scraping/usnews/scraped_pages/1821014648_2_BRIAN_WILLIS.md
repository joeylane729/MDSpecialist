## Overview

Dr. Brian K. Willis is a neurosurgeon in Edina, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport Academic Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/interim-lsu-public-hospital-6720776) and [Willis-Knighton Medical Center DBA Willis Knighton Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/willis-knighton-medical-center-6720877). He received his medical degree from LSU School of Medicine in Shreveport and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Brian K. Willis has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-willis-450964#expertise). Dr. Brian K. Willis accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-willis-450964#insurance).
