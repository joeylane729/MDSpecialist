## Overview

Dr. Jack M. Klem is a neurosurgeon in Colorado Springs, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Baptist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/baptist-hospital-of-miami-6390578) and [Cleveland Clinic Martin Health-Stuart](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/martin-medical-center-6391000). He received his medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jack M. Klem has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jack-klem-608805#expertise). Dr. Jack M. Klem accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jack-klem-608805#insurance). Dr. Jack M. Klem is highly recommended by patients.
