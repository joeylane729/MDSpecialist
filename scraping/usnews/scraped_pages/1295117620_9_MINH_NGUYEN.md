## Overview

Dr. Minh Nguyen is a family medicine doctor in Grants Pass, Oregon and is affiliated with [Asante Three Rivers Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/three-rivers-community-hospital-6920005). She received her medical degree from Ross University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Minh Nguyen has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/minh-nguyen-838761#expertise). Dr. Minh Nguyen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/minh-nguyen-838761#insurance). Dr. Minh Nguyen is highly recommended by patients.
