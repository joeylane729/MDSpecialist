## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey Tomlin is a neurosurgeon in Portsmouth, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Palomar Medical Center Escondido](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/palomar-medical-center-6930660) and [UC San Diego Health-La Jolla, Hillcrest, and East Campus Medical Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/uc-san-diego-medical-center-6932820). He received his medical degree from Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences F. Edward Hebert School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey Tomlin has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-tomlin-3376064#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey Tomlin accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-tomlin-3376064#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey Tomlin is highly recommended by patients.
