## Overview

Dr. Richard V. Williamson is an orthopedist in Mount Vernon, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Skagit Regional Health-Mount Vernon](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/skagit-valley-hospital-6910420) and [Island Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/island-hospital-6910035).
