## Overview

Dr. Daniel H. Donovan is a neurologist in Cookeville, Tennessee. He received his medical degree from Emory University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Daniel H. Donovan has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-donovan-202836#expertise). Donovan is highly recommended by patients.
