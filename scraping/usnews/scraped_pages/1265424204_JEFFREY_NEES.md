## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey P. Nees is a neurosurgeon in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. He received his medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey P. Nees has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-nees-166268#expertise).
