## Overview

Russell is a pediatrician in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Alabama at Birmingham Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/university-of-alabama-hospital-at-birmingham-6530304) and [USA Health University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/university-of-south-alabama-medical-center-6530600).
