## Overview

Dr. Kurtus Dafford is a neurosurgeon in Boston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Brigham and Women's Faulkner Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/brigham-and-womens-faulkner-hospital-6140310) and [Brigham and Women's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/brigham-and-womens-hospital-6140215).
