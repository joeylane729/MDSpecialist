## Overview

Dr. Daniel S. Robertson is an orthopedist in Rockville, Maryland and is affiliated with [Johns Hopkins Medicine-Suburban Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/suburban-hospital-6320350) and [Largo ASC](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/largo-asc-A9364300). He received his medical degree from University of Nebraska College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Daniel S. Robertson has expertise in treating arthritis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-robertson-713061#expertise). Dr. Daniel S. Robertson accepts Medicare, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-robertson-713061#insurance). Dr. Daniel S. Robertson is highly recommended by patients.
