## Overview

Dr. Todd Ridenour is a neurosurgeon in Davenport, Iowa and is affiliated with [Genesis Medical Center-Davenport-West Central Park](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/genesis-medical-center-davenport-6620004). He received his medical degree from University of South Dakota School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Todd Ridenour has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/todd-ridenour-206202#expertise).
