## Overview

Dr. Michail Vasilakis is a neurosurgeon in Marietta, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Marietta Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/marietta-memorial-hospital-6411630) and [Selby General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/selby-general-hospital-6419165). He received his medical degree from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michail Vasilakis has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michail-vasilakis-714411#expertise). Dr. Michail Vasilakis accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michail-vasilakis-714411#insurance).
