## Overview

Dr. David L. Chen is a dermatologist in Norwich, Connecticut and is affiliated with [William W. Backus Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/william-w-backus-hospital-6160560). He received his medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. David L. Chen has expertise in treating acne, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-chen-1228617#expertise). Dr. David L.
