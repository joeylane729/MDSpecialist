## Overview

Dr. William J. Smith is an orthopedist in Lake Placid, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adirondack Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/adirondack-medical-center-6210007) and [Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/claxton-hepburn-medical-center-6213740).
