## Overview

Dr. Deus Cielo is a neurosurgeon in Providence, Rhode Island and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Miriam Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/miriam-hospital-6150130) and [Providence Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/providence-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6150185). He received his medical degree from Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Deus Cielo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/deus-cielo-349779#expertise). Dr. Deus Cielo accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/deus-cielo-349779#insurance). Dr. Deus Cielo is highly recommended by patients.
