## Overview

Dr. Joshua E. Loewenstein is a neurosurgeon in Chapel Hill, North Carolina and is affiliated with [UNC Rex Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/rex-healthcare-6361210). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Joshua E. Loewenstein has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-loewenstein-1364820#expertise). Dr. Joshua E. Loewenstein accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-loewenstein-1364820#insurance). Dr. Joshua E. Loewenstein is highly recommended by patients.
