Wong is a pediatrician in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hassenfeld Children’s Hospital at NYU Langone](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-childrens-hospital-PA6212925) and [NYU Langone Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-langone-medical-center-6212925).
