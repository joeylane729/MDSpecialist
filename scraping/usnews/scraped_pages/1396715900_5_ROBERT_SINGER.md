## Overview

Dr. Robert Singer is a plastic surgeon in La Jolla, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Scripps La Jolla Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/scripps-la-jolla-hospitals-and-clinics-6931130) and [UC San Diego Health-La Jolla, Hillcrest, and East Campus Medical Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/uc-san-diego-medical-center-6932820).
