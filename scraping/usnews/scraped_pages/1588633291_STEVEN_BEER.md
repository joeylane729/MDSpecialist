## Overview

Dr. Steven J. Beer is a neurosurgeon in Cheyenne, Wyoming and is affiliated with [Memorial Hospital of Converse County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/memorial-hospital-of-converse-county-6830080) and [High Plains Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wy/high-plains-surgery-center-A9534988). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven J. Beer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-beer-293500#expertise). Dr. Steven J. Beer accepts Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-beer-293500#insurance).
