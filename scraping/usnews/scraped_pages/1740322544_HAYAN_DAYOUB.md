## Overview

Dr. Hayan Dayoub is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HSHS St. John's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/st-johns-hospital-6432980) and [Springfield Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/memorial-medical-center-6432950).
