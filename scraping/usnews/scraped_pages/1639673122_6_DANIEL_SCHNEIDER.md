## Overview

Dr. Daniel S. Schneider is an ENT-otolaryngologist in Saint Paul, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [M Health Fairview Southdale Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/fairview-southdale-hospital-6610918), [Allina Health United Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/united-hospital-of-st-paul-6611585) and [Brainerd Lakes Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mn/brainerd-lakes-surgery-center-A8940531).
