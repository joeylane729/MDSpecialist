## Overview

Dr. Michael Cohen is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Albany, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Albany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/albany-medical-center-6210030) and [Saratoga Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/saratoga-hospital-6214520). He received his medical degree from UMass Chan Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Cohen has expertise in treating hysterectomy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-1385081#expertise). Dr. Michael Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-1385081#insurance).
