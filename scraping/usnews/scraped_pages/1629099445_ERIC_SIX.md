## Overview

Dr. Eric G. Six is a neurosurgeon in Harlingen, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Harlingen Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/harlingen-medical-center-6740224) and [Valley Baptist Medical Center-Harlingen](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/valley-baptist-medical-center-6741790).
