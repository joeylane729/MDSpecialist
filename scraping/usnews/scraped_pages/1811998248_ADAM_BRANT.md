## Overview

Dr. Adam Brant is a neurosurgeon in Fresno, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Community Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/community-regional-medical-center-6930790) and [St. Agnes Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-agnes-medical-center-6930810).
