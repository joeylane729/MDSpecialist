## Overview

Dr. Thomas R. Whisenhunt is a neurosurgeon in Auburn, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Central Alabama VA Medical Center-Montgomery](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/central-alabama-veterans-health-care-system-6530008) and [Jackson Hospital and Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/jackson-hospital-6530695). He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Thomas R. Whisenhunt has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-whisenhunt-632950#expertise). Dr. Thomas R. Whisenhunt accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-whisenhunt-632950#insurance).
