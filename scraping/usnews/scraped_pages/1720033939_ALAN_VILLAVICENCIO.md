## Overview

Dr. Alan T. Villavicencio is a neurosurgeon in Boulder, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/exempla-good-samaritan-medical-center-6840021), [UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/university-of-colorado-hospital-6840430) and [Foothills Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/co/foothills-surgery-center-A4761782).
