## Overview

Dr. Kaveh Barami is a neurosurgeon in Hartford, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Francis Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/st-francis-hospital-and-medical-center-6160004) and [Mercy General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-general-hospital-6932670). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kaveh Barami has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kaveh-barami-396825#expertise). Dr. Kaveh Barami accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kaveh-barami-396825#insurance). Dr. Kaveh Barami is highly recommended by patients.
