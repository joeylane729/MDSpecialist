## Overview

Dr. Benjamin T. White is a neurosurgeon in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. He received his medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Benjamin T. White has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/benjamin-white-283977#expertise). White is highly recommended by patients.
