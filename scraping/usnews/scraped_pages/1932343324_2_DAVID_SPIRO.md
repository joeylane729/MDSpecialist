## Overview

Dr. David Spiro is a pediatric emergency medicine physician in Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arkansas Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-childrens-hospital-6710340) and [UAMS Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/university-of-arkansas-for-medical-sciences-6710440).
