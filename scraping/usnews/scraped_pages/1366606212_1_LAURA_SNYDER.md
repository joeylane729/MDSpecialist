## Overview

Dr. Laura Snyder is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Macon, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Piedmont Columbus Regional Midtown Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/medical-center-6380430) and [St. Francis-Emory Healthcare-Columbus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/st-francis-hospital-6380006). She received her medical degree from Medical University of South Carolina College of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Laura Snyder accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/laura-snyder-1184990#insurance).
