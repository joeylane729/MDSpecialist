## Overview

Dr. Saul Wilson is a neurosurgeon in Iowa City, Iowa and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Iowa Health Care Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/university-of-iowa-hospitals-and-clinics-6620765) and [University of Iowa Stead Family Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/university-of-iowa-childrens-hospital-PA6620765).
