## Overview

Dr. Viren S. Vasudeva is a neurosurgeon in Athens, Georgia and is affiliated with [Piedmont Athens Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/athens-regional-medical-center-6380040) and [Georgia Spine Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ga/georgia-spine-surgery-center-A3184538).
