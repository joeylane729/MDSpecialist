## Overview

Dr. Gary P. Colon is a neurosurgeon in Naples, Florida and is affiliated with [NCH Baker Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/naples-community-hospital-6390669). He received his medical degree from Weill Cornell Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gary P. Colon has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gary-colon-20575#expertise). Dr. Gary P. Colon accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gary-colon-20575#insurance).
