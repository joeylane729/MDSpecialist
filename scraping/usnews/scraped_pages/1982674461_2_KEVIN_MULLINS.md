## Overview

Dr. Kevin Mullins is a neurosurgeon in West Islip, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Good Samaritan University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/good-samaritan-hospital-medical-center-6215295) and [St. Catherine of Siena Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-catherine-of-siena-medical-center-6214616). He received his medical degree from University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kevin Mullins has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kevin-mullins-254680#expertise). Dr. Kevin Mullins accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kevin-mullins-254680#insurance) below. Dr. Kevin Mullins is highly recommended by patients.
