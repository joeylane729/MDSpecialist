## Overview

Dr. Dionysios Klironomos is a neurosurgeon in Richland, Washington and is affiliated with [Kadlec Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/kadlec-regional-medical-center-6910530). He received his medical degree from University of Western Ontario and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Dionysios Klironomos has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/dionysios-klironomos-806091#expertise). Dr. Dionysios Klironomos accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/dionysios-klironomos-806091#insurance). Dr. Dionysios Klironomos is highly recommended by patients.
