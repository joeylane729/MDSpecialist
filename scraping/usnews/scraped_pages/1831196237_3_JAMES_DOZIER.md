## Overview

Dr. James C. Dozier is a neurosurgeon in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dupont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/dupont-hospital-6420033) and [Lutheran Downtown Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-joseph-hospital-6420450). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James C. Dozier has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-dozier-142117#expertise). Dr. James C. Dozier accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-dozier-142117#insurance).
