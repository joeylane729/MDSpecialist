## Overview

Dr. Frank L. Genovese is a neurosurgeon in Schenectady, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ellis Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/ellis-hospital-6214560) and [Albany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/albany-medical-center-6210030). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Frank L. Genovese has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/frank-genovese-337115#expertise).
