## Overview

Dr. Amit Schwartz is a neurosurgeon in Brooklyn, New York and is affiliated with [Maimonides Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/maimonides-medical-center-6210747). He received his medical degree from Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Amit Schwartz has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amit-schwartz-2392685#expertise). Dr. Amit Schwartz accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amit-schwartz-2392685#insurance). Dr. Amit Schwartz is highly recommended by patients.
