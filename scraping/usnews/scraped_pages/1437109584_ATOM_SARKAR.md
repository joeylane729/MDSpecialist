## Overview

Dr. Atom Sarkar is a neurosurgeon in Chester, Pennsylvania. He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Atom Sarkar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/atom-sarkar-347314#expertise).
