## Overview

Dr. James D. Evans is a pediatrician in Orinda, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Alta Bates Summit Medical Center-Berkeley](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alta-bates-summit-medical-center-6930290) and [John Muir Health-Concord Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/john-muir-medical-center-6930480).
