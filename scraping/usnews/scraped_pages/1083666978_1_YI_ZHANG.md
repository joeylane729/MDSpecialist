## Overview

Dr. Yi Zhang is an ophthalmologist in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Temple University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/temple-university-hospital-6232300). He received his medical degree from Zhejiang Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Yi Zhang has expertise in treating retinal detachment, cataract, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yi-zhang-794630#expertise). Dr. Yi Zhang accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yi-zhang-794630#insurance). Dr. Yi Zhang is highly recommended by patients.
