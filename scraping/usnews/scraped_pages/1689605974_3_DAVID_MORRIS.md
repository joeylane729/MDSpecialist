## Overview

Dr. David Morris is a plastic surgeon in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Shriners Hospitals for Children-Chicago](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/shriners-hospitals-for-children-chicago-6431140) and [University of Illinois Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/university-of-illinois-hospital-chicago-6430553). He received his medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Morris accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-morris-115357#insurance). Dr. David Morris is highly recommended by patients.
