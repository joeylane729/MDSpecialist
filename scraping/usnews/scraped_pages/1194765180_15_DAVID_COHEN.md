## Overview

Dr. David E. Cohen is a pediatrician in Plattsburgh, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Vermont Health Network-Champlain Valley Physicians Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/cvph-medical-center-6213990) and [Adirondack Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/adirondack-medical-center-6210007).
