## Overview

Dr. Paul W. Detwiler is a neurosurgeon in Tyler, Texas. He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul W. Detwiler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-detwiler-123991#expertise).
