## Overview

Dr. Stephen R. Parker is a neurosurgeon in Jacksonville, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Vincent's Riverside Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/st-vincents-medical-center-6390390) and [Ascension St. Vincent's Southside Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/st-lukes-hospital-6390145). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stephen R. Parker has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-parker-665981#expertise). Dr. Stephen R. Parker accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-parker-665981#insurance).
