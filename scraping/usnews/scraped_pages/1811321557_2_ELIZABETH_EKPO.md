## Overview

Dr. Elizabeth I. Ekpo is a neurologist in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with [UC Davis Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/university-of-california-davis-medical-center-6932680). She received her medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Elizabeth I. Ekpo has expertise in treating dementia, sleep apnea, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/elizabeth-ekpo-877649#expertise). Dr. Elizabeth I. Ekpo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/elizabeth-ekpo-877649#insurance).
