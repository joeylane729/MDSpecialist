## Overview

Dr. Syed A. Naqvi is a psychiatrist in Prince Frederick, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CalvertHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/calvert-memorial-hospital-6320730) and [St. Elizabeths Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/dc/st-elizabeths-hospital-6330200).
