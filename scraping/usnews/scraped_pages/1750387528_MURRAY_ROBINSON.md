## Overview

Dr. Murray D. Robinson is a neurosurgeon in Decatur, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory Decatur Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/dekalb-medical-6380488) and [Piedmont Eastside Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-eastside-medical-center-6381075). He received his medical degree from Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Murray D. Robinson has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/murray-robinson-3430105#expertise). Dr. Murray D. Robinson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/murray-robinson-3430105#insurance).
