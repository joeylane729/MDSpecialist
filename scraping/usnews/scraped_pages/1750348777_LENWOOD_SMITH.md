## Overview

Dr. Lenwood P. Smith is a neurosurgeon in Durham, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carteret Health Care Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carteret-general-hospital-6360992) and [ECU Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/vidant-medical-center-6360690). Smith accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lenwood-smith-329825#insurance). Dr. Lenwood P.
