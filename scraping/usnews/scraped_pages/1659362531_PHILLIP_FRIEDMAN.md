## Overview

Dr. Phillip Friedman is a neurosurgeon in Bingham Farms, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health Dearborn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/oakwood-hospital-and-medical-center-6440405) and [DMC Sinai-Grace Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/sinai-grace-hospital-6440630).
