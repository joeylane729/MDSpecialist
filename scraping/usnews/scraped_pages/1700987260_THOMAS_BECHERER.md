Dr. Thomas Becherer is a neurosurgeon in Louisville, Kentucky and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Louisville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/baptist-health-louisville-6510365) and [University of Louisville Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/university-of-louisville-hospital-6510550).
