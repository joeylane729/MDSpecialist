## Overview

Dr. Onechang W. Lee is a neurosurgeon in Fountain Valley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Fountain Valley Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/fountain-valley-regional-hospital-and-medical-center-6930765) and [MemorialCare Orange Coast Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/orange-coast-memorial-medical-center-6930775). He received his medical degree from Albany Medical College and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Onechang W. Lee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/onechang-lee-1149366#expertise). Dr. Onechang W. Lee accepts Medicare, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/onechang-lee-1149366#insurance).
