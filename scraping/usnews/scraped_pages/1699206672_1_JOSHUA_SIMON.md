## Overview

Dr. Joshua Simon is a psychiatrist in Marana, Arizona. He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joshua Simon has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-simon-51714#expertise).
