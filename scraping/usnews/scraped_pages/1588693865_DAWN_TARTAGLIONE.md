## Overview

Dr. Dawn R. Tartaglione is a neurosurgeon in Dover, Delaware and is affiliated with [Bayhealth Hospitals-Sussex and Kent](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/bayhealth-medical-center-6310007).
