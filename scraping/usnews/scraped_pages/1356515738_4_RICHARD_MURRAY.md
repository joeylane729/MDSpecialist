## Overview

Dr. Richard Murray is a pulmonologist in York, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UPMC Memorial](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/memorial-hospital-6233549) and [WellSpan York Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/wellspan-york-hospital-6233550). He received his medical degree from University of Virginia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard Murray accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-murray-170341#insurance) below. Dr. Richard Murray is highly recommended by patients.
