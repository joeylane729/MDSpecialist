## Overview

Dr. Roberto Rey Dios is a neurosurgeon in Vero Beach, Florida and is affiliated with [Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/indian-river-medical-center-6391120). He received his medical degree from University of Santiago de Compostela and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Roberto Rey Dios has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/roberto-rey-dios-747548#expertise). Dr. Roberto Rey Dios accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/roberto-rey-dios-747548#insurance). Dr. Roberto Rey Dios is highly recommended by patients.
