Dr. Keyvan Abtin is a neurosurgeon in Hillsboro, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Legacy Emanuel Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-emanuel-hospital-and-health-center-6920003) and [Legacy Good Samaritan Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-good-samaritan-hospital-and-health-center-6920418).
