## Overview

Dr. Sujit Bhimireddy is a neurosurgeon in Dayton, Ohio and is affiliated with [Miami Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/miami-valley-hospital-6411190). He received his medical degree from Saint Louis University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Sujit Bhimireddy has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sujit-bhimireddy-3318720#expertise). Dr. Sujit Bhimireddy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sujit-bhimireddy-3318720#insurance). Dr. Sujit Bhimireddy is highly recommended by patients.
