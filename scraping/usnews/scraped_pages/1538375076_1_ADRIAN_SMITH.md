## Overview

Dr. Adrian M. Smith is a neurosurgeon in Owensboro, Kentucky and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MyMichigan Medical Center Midland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/midmichigan-medical-center-midland-6441785) and [Vibra Hospital of Southeastern Michigan](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/vibra-hospital-of-southeastern-michigan-6440044). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Adrian M. Smith has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/adrian-smith-780252#expertise). Dr. Adrian M. Smith accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/adrian-smith-780252#insurance). Dr. Adrian M. Smith is highly recommended by patients.
