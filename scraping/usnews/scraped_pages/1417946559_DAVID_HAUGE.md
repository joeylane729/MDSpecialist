## Overview

Dr. David H. Hauge is a neurosurgeon in Knoxville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Covenant Health Fort Sanders Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/fort-sanders-regional-medical-center-6520510) and [Covenant Health Parkwest](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/parkwest-medical-center-6520530).
