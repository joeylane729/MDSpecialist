## Overview

Dr. Bernardo J. Ordonez is a neurosurgeon in Chesapeake, Virginia and is affiliated with [Sentara Norfolk General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sentara-norfolk-general-hospital-6340620). He received his medical degree from University of Maryland School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bernardo J. Ordonez has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bernardo-ordonez-860549#expertise). Dr. Bernardo J. Ordonez accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bernardo-ordonez-860549#insurance).
