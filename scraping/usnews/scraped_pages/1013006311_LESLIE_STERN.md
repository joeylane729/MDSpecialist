Dr. Leslie Stern is a neurosurgeon in Fall River, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Southcoast Hospitals-Fall River, New Bedford and Wareham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/southcoast-hospitals-group-6140016) and [St. Anne's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/st-annes-hospital-6140900).
