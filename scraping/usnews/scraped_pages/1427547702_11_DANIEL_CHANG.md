## Overview

Dr. Daniel W. Chang is a neurologist in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hollywood Presbyterian Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/hollywood-presbyterian-medical-center-6931670) and [PIH Health Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/good-samaritan-hospital-6931540).
