## Overview

Dr. Paul E. Kim is an anesthesiologist in San Diego, California and is affiliated with [Jennifer Moreno VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/va-san-diego-hospital-6932825). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul E. Kim has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-kim-477513#expertise). Dr. Paul E.
