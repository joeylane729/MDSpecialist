## Overview

Dr. Paul L. Moots is a neurologist in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Vanderbilt University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/vanderbilt-university-medical-center-6521060). He received his medical degree from Ohio State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul L. Moots has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-moots-1522733#expertise). Dr. Paul L. Moots accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-moots-1522733#insurance). Dr. Paul L. Moots is highly recommended by patients.
