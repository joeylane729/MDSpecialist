## Overview

Dr. Michaela Lee is a neurosurgeon in Scottsdale, Arizona and is affiliated with [Banner-University Medical Center Phoenix](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/banner-good-samaritan-medical-center-6860250).
