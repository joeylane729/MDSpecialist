## Overview

Dr. Joseph B. Schnittker is a neurosurgeon in Elkhart, Indiana and is affiliated with [Elkhart General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/elkhart-general-hospital-6420270). He received his medical degree from Queen's University at Kingston and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph B. Schnittker has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-schnittker-867396#expertise). Dr. Joseph B. Schnittker accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-schnittker-867396#insurance).
