## Overview

Dr. Gregory R. Trost is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UW Health University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/university-of-wisconsin-hospital-and-clinics-6450820) and [UnityPoint Health Meriter](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/meriter-hospital-6450775). He received his medical degree from University of Wisconsin School of Medicine and Public Health and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory R. Trost has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-trost-282901#expertise). Dr. Gregory R. Trost accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-trost-282901#insurance).
