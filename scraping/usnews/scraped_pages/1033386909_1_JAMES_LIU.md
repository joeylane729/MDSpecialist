## Overview

Dr. James Liu is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Aurora, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/exempla-good-samaritan-medical-center-6840021) and [Intermountain Health Lutheran Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/exempla-lutheran-medical-center-6840012). He received his medical degree from The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. James Liu accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-liu-1054047#insurance).
