## Overview

Dr. Sudesh Ebenezer is a neurosurgeon in Flint, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hurley Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/hurley-medical-center-childrens-services-PA6441030) and [Hurley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/hurley-medical-center-6441030).
