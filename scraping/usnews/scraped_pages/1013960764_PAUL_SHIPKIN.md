## Overview

Dr. Paul Shipkin is a neurologist in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Hospitals of the University of Pennsylvania-Penn Presbyterian](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/hospital-of-the-university-of-pennsylvania-6231900). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
