## Overview

Dr. James B. Blankenship is a neurosurgeon in Fayetteville, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Physicians' Specialty Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/physicians-specialty-hospital-6710239) and [Washington Regional Medical Center-Fayetteville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/washington-regional-medical-center-6710195). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James B. Blankenship has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-blankenship-803101#expertise). Dr. James B. Blankenship accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-blankenship-803101#insurance).
