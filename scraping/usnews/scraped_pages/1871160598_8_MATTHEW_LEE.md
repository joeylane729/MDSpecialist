## Overview

Dr. Matthew Lee is a radiologist in Bemidji, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sanford Bemidji Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/north-country-regional-hospital-6610120) and [CHI St. Joseph's Health-Park Rapids](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/st-josephs-area-health-services-6611315). He received his medical degree from Univ of North Dakota School of Medicine and Health Sciences and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Matthew Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-lee-715510#insurance).
