## Overview

Dr. Anant Patel is an emergency medicine physician in Fort Worth, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [JPS Health Network-Fort Worth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/jps-health-network-6741483) and [Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital Southwest](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-harris-methodist-hospital-southwest-6741445).
