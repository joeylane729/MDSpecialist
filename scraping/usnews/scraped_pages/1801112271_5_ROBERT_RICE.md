## Overview

Dr. Robert S. Rice is an orthopedist in Layton, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Layton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/layton-hospital-6870379), [Intermountain Health McKay-Dee Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/intermountain-mckay-dee-hospital-center-6870160), [Mountain West Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ut/mountain-west-surgery-center-A3546725) and [South Ogden Specialty Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ut/south-ogden-specialty-surgical-center-A6847619).
