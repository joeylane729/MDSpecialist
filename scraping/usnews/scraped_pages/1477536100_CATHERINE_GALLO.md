## Overview

Dr. Catherine J. Gallo is a neurosurgeon in Eugene, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [PeaceHealth Sacred Heart Medical Center University District](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/sacred-heart-medical-center-6920160), [McKenzie-Willamette Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/mckenzie-willamette-medical-center-6920741) and [Two Rivers Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/two-rivers-surgical-center-A2134411). She received her medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Catherine J. Gallo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/catherine-gallo-220750#expertise). Dr. Catherine J. Gallo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/catherine-gallo-220750#insurance).
