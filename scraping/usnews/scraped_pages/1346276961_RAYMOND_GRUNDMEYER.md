## Overview

Dr. Raymond W. Grundmeyer is a neurosurgeon in Wichita, Kansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kansas Spine and Specialty Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/kansas-spine-hospital-6670036) and [Wesley Healthcare Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/wesley-medical-center-6671170). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Raymond W. Grundmeyer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raymond-grundmeyer-413640#expertise). Dr. Raymond W. Grundmeyer accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raymond-grundmeyer-413640#insurance). Dr. Raymond W. Grundmeyer is highly recommended by patients.
