## Overview

Dr. Richard D. Fessler is a neurosurgeon in Southfield, Michigan and is affiliated with [Henry Ford St. John Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-john-hospital-and-medical-center-6440805) and [NOVI Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/novi-surgery-center-A3751048). He received his medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard D. Fessler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-fessler-2557315#expertise). Dr. Richard D. Fessler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-fessler-2557315#insurance).
