## Overview

Dr. Michael S. Park is a neurosurgeon in Bradenton, Florida and is affiliated with [HCA Florida Blake Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/blake-medical-center-6390055). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael S. Park has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-635333#expertise). Dr. Michael S. Park accepts Medicare, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-635333#insurance).
