## Overview

Dr. Thomas Ostergard is a neurosurgeon in Huntsville, Alabama and is affiliated with [Moses H. Cone Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/moses-h-cone-memorial-hospital-6360002) and [Greensboro Specialty Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/greensboro-specialty-surgery-center-A8413483). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Thomas Ostergard has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-ostergard-1086510#expertise). Dr. Thomas Ostergard accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-ostergard-1086510#insurance). Dr. Thomas Ostergard is highly recommended by patients.
