## Overview

Dr. Victor T. Freund is a neurosurgeon in Greenville, North Carolina and is affiliated with [ECU Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/vidant-medical-center-6360690). He received his medical degree from Brody School of Medicine at East Carolina University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Victor T. Freund has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-freund-284929#expertise). Dr. Victor T. Freund accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-freund-284929#insurance).
