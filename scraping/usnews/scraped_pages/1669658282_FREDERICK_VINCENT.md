## Overview

Dr. Frederick J. Vincent is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Legacy Emanuel Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-emanuel-hospital-and-health-center-6920003) and [Providence Portland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-portland-medical-center-6920520). He received his medical degree from Laval University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Frederick J. Vincent has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/frederick-vincent-805881#expertise). Dr. Frederick J. Vincent accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/frederick-vincent-805881#insurance). Dr. Frederick J. Vincent is highly recommended by patients.
