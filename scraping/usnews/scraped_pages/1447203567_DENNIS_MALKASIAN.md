## Overview

Dr. Dennis R. Malkasian is a neurosurgeon in Newport Beach, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/hoag-memorial-hospital-presbyterian-6932115) and [Tibor Rubin VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/va-long-beach-hospitals-6931390).
