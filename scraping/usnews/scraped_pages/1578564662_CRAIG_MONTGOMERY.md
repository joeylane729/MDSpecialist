Montgomery is a neurosurgeon in Syracuse, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Crouse Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/crouse-hospital-6214890) and [St. Joseph's Health Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-josephs-hospital-health-center-6214870).
