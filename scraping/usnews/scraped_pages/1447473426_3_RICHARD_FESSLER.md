## Overview

Dr. Richard Fessler is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with [Rush University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-university-medical-center-6430985). He received his medical degree from University of Chicago Division of the Biological Sciences The Pritzker School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard Fessler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-fessler-3374053#expertise). Dr. Richard Fessler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-fessler-3374053#insurance).
