## Overview

Dr. Bryan A. Bertoglio is a neurosurgeon in Elk Grove Village, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rush University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-university-medical-center-6430985) and [Ascension Alexian Brothers Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/alexian-brothers-medical-center-6431613).
