## Overview

Dr. Michael J. Martin is an internist in Charlotte, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-6360280) and [Novant Health Presbyterian Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/novant-health-presbyterian-medical-center-6360320). Martin has expertise in treating obesity, coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-martin-214929#expertise). Martin is highly recommended by patients.
