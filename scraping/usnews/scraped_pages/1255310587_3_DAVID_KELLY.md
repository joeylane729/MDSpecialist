## Overview

Dr. David Kelly is a pediatrician in Livingston, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cooperman Barnabas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/st-barnabas-medical-center-6220800) and [McMullen Children’s Center at Saint Barnabas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/saint-barnabas-childrens-center-PA6220800).
