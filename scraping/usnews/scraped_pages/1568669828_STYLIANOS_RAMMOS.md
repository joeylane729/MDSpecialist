## Overview

Dr. Stylianos K. Rammos is a neurosurgeon in Sherwood, Arkansas and is affiliated with [CHI St. Vincent Infirmary](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/st-vincent-infirmary-6710400). He received his medical degree from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stylianos K. Rammos has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stylianos-rammos-639069#expertise). Dr. Stylianos K. Rammos accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stylianos-rammos-639069#insurance). Dr. Stylianos K. Rammos is highly recommended by patients.
