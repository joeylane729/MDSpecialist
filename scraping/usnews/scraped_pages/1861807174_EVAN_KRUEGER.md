## Overview

Dr. Evan Krueger is a neurosurgeon in Neenah, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ThedaCare Regional Medical Center-Neenah](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/theda-clark-medical-center-6451290) and [Ascension NE Wisconsin-St. Elizabeth Hospital and Mercy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-elizabeth-hospital-6450060). He received his medical degree from A.T. Still University, School of Osteopathic Medicine in Arizona and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Evan Krueger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/evan-krueger-1115945#expertise). Dr. Evan Krueger accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/evan-krueger-1115945#insurance).
