## Overview

Dr. William R. Taylor is a neurosurgeon in San Diego, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Alvarado Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alvarado-hospital-medical-center-6932779) and [UC San Diego Health-La Jolla, Hillcrest, and East Campus Medical Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/uc-san-diego-medical-center-6932820).
