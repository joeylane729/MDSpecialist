## Overview

Dr. Lee Sandquist is a neurosurgeon in Spokane Valley, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MultiCare Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/valley-hospital-6910867) and [Confluence Health-Central Washington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/central-washington-hospital-6911195). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Lee Sandquist has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lee-sandquist-823123#expertise). Dr. Lee Sandquist accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lee-sandquist-823123#insurance).
