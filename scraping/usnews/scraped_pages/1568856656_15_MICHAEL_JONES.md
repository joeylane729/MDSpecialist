## Overview

Dr. Michael Jones is a neurosurgeon in Waukesha, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [ProHealth Waukesha Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/waukesha-memorial-hospital-6452020). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Jones has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-jones-1217920#expertise). Dr. Michael Jones accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-jones-1217920#insurance).
