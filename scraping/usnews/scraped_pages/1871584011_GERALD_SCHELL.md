## Overview

Dr. Gerald R. Schell is a neurosurgeon in Saginaw, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Aleda E. Lutz VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/aleda-e-lutz-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6442295), [Covenant Healthcare-Saginaw](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/covenant-medical-center-harrison-6440023) and [Flint Region ASC](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/flint-region-asc-A9733770). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gerald R. Schell has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gerald-schell-208249#expertise). Dr. Gerald R. Schell accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gerald-schell-208249#insurance).
