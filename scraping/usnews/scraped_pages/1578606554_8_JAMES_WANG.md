## Overview

Dr. James J. Wang is an internist in Avon Lake, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [Cleveland Clinic Avon Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-avon-hospital-6410566).
