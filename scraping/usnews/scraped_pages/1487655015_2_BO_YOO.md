## Overview

Dr. Bo H. Yoo is a neurosurgeon in Janesville, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [Mercyhealth Hospital and Trauma Center-Janesville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/mercy-health-hospital-6450015). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bo H. Yoo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bo-yoo-3409763#expertise). Dr. Bo H. Yoo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bo-yoo-3409763#insurance).
