## Overview

Dr. Daniel J. Schneider is a pulmonologist in Ann Arbor, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Michigan Health-Ann Arbor](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/university-of-michigan-hospitals-and-health-centers-6440110) and [Veterans Affairs Ann Arbor Healthcare System](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/va-ann-arbor-hospitals-6440115).
