## Overview

Dr. Robert A. Martin is a neurologist in Springfield, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Baystate Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/baystate-medical-center-6141955). He received his medical degree from UMass Chan Medical School and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Robert A. Martin has expertise in treating dementia, Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-martin-1185700#expertise).
