## Overview

Dr. Marco T. Silva is a neurosurgeon in Beaumont, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Dubuis Hospital of Beaumont](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/dubuis-hospital-of-beaumont-6740198) and [Christus Southeast Texas Hospital-St. Elizabeth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/christus-hospital-st-elizabeth-6740258). He received his medical degree from University of California San Diego School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Marco T. Silva has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marco-silva-153740#expertise). Dr. Marco T. Silva accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marco-silva-153740#insurance).
