## Overview

Dr. Samuel M. Taylon is a neurosurgeon in Merriam, Kansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Overland Park Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/overland-park-regional-medical-center-6670775), [Research Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/research-medical-center-6630435) and [Johnson County Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ks/johnson-county-surgery-center-A4824565). Taylon has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/samuel-taylon-712642#expertise).
