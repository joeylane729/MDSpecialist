## Overview

Dr. David Morris is an internist in Sandusky, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Firelands Regional Health System-Sandusky](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/firelands-regional-medical-center-6410015) and [Magruder Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/h-b-magruder-memorial-hospital-6411900). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Morris has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, sleep apnea, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-morris-144026#expertise).
