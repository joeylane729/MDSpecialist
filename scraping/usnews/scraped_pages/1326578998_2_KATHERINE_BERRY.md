## Overview

Dr. Katherine A. Berry is a dermatologist in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-milton-s-hershey-medical-center-6230705).
