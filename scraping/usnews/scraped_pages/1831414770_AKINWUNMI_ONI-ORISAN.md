## Overview

Dr. Akinwunmi A. Oni-Orisan is a neurosurgeon in Oxnard, California and is affiliated with [St. John's Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-johns-regional-medical-center-6932310). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Akinwunmi A. Oni-Orisan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/akinwunmi-oni-orisan-1218937#expertise). Dr. Akinwunmi A. Oni-Orisan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/akinwunmi-oni-orisan-1218937#insurance). Dr. Akinwunmi A. Oni-Orisan is highly recommended by patients.
