## Overview

Dr. Paul M. Davis is a plastic surgeon in Shreveport, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Health Shreveport-Bossier](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/christus-health-shreveport-bossier-6720088) and [Willis-Knighton Medical Center DBA Willis Knighton Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/willis-knighton-medical-center-6720877). Davis has expertise in treating breast reconstruction, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-davis-165297#expertise). Dr. Paul M. Davis accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-davis-165297#insurance). Dr. Paul M. Davis is highly recommended by patients.
