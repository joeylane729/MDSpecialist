## Overview

Dr. Robert Solomon is a geriatrician in Roselle Park, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Overlook Medical Center Atlantic Health System](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/overlook-medical-center-6221370) and [Trinitas Regional Medical Center RWJBH](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/trinitas-regional-medical-center-6220001). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert Solomon has expertise in treating dementia, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-solomon-14492#expertise). Dr. Robert Solomon accepts Medicare, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-solomon-14492#insurance). Dr. Robert Solomon is highly recommended by patients.
