## Overview

Schumacher is a rheumatologist in Chicopee, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baystate Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/baystate-medical-center-6141955) and [Holyoke Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/holyoke-medical-center-6141110).
