## Overview

Dr. Omar Qahwash is a neurosurgeon in Lansing, Michigan and is affiliated with [University of Michigan Health-Ann Arbor](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/university-of-michigan-hospitals-and-health-centers-6440110).
