## Overview

Dr. Pal Randhawa is a neurosurgeon in Clackamas, Oregon and is affiliated with [Integris Canadian Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/integris-canadian-valley-hospital-6730038).
