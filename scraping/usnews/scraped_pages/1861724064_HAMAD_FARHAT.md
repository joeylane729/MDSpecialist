## Overview

Dr. Hamad I. Farhat is a neurosurgeon in Oak Lawn, Illinois and is affiliated with [Advocate Christ Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-christ-medical-center-6432413). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Hamad I. Farhat has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hamad-farhat-2473758#expertise). Dr. Hamad I. Farhat accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hamad-farhat-2473758#insurance). Dr. Hamad I. Farhat is highly recommended by patients.
