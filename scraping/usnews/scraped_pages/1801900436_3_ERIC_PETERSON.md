## Overview

Dr. Eric Peterson is an ophthalmologist in Salem, Virginia and is affiliated with [Carilion Roanoke Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/carilion-roanoke-memorial-hospital-6341040) and [Roanoke Valley Center for Sight](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/va/roanoke-valley-center-for-sight-A5653139). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Eric Peterson has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, shingles, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-1419805#expertise). Dr. Eric Peterson accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-1419805#insurance).
