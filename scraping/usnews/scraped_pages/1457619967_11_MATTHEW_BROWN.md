## Overview

Dr. Matthew Brown is an orthopedist in La Jolla, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Scripps Green Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/scripps-green-hospital-6931140) and [Cooper University Health Care-Camden](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/cooper-university-hospital-6220190). He received his medical degree from University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew Brown has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-brown-1011723#expertise). Dr. Matthew Brown accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-brown-1011723#insurance). Dr. Matthew Brown is highly recommended by patients.
