## Overview

Dr. William J. Jeong is a neurosurgeon in Lynchburg, Virginia and is affiliated with [St. Rose Dominican Hospitals-San Martin Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/st-rose-domincian-hospitals-6880032) and [San Martin Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nv/san-martin-surgery-center-A3354219).
