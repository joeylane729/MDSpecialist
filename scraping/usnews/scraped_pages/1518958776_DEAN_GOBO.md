Dr. Dean J. Gobo is a neurosurgeon in Clearwater, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Morton Plant Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/morton-plant-hospital-6390090) and [Mease Countryside Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/mease-countryside-hospital-6390160).
