## Overview

Dr. Brian Lee is an orthopedist in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with [Adventist Health-White Memorial](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/white-memorial-medical-center-6931770) and [HealthSouth Kerlan-Jobe Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/healthsouth-kerlan-jobe-surgery-center-A3799629). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Brian Lee has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-lee-1222387#expertise). Dr. Brian Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-lee-1222387#insurance). Dr. Brian Lee is highly recommended by patients.
