## Overview

Dr. Fredrick S. Junn is a neurosurgeon in Dearborn, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health Dearborn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/oakwood-hospital-and-medical-center-6440405) and [Corewell Health Taylor Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/oakwood-heritage-hospital-6442420). He received his medical degree from University of Western Ontario and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Fredrick S. Junn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fredrick-junn-796947#expertise). Dr. Fredrick S. Junn accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fredrick-junn-796947#insurance).
