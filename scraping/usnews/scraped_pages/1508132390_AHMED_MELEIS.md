## Overview

Dr. Ahmed Meleis is a neurosurgeon in Albany, New York and is affiliated with [Albany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/albany-medical-center-6210030). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Ahmed Meleis has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ahmed-meleis-979145#expertise). Dr. Ahmed Meleis accepts Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ahmed-meleis-979145#insurance). Dr. Ahmed Meleis is highly recommended by patients.
