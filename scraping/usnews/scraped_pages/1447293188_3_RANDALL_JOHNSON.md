## Overview

Dr. Randall R. Johnson is a neurosurgeon in Neenah, Wisconsin. He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Randall R. Johnson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/randall-johnson-397333#expertise). Dr. Randall R. Johnson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/randall-johnson-397333#insurance). Dr. Randall R. Johnson is highly recommended by patients.
