## Overview

Dr. Ellen Air is a neurosurgeon in Detroit, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Henry Ford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/henry-ford-hospital-6440650) and [Henry Ford West Bloomfield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/henry-ford-west-bloomfield-hospital-6440049).
