Anson is a neurosurgeon in Las Vegas, Nevada and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MountainView Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/mountain-view-hospital-6880007), [Southern Hills Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/southern-hills-hospital-and-medical-center-6880013) and [San Martin Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nv/san-martin-surgery-center-A3354219).
