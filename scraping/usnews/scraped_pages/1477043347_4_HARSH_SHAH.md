## Overview

Dr. Harsh Shah is an internist in Kirksville, Missouri and is affiliated with [University of Utah Health, University of Utah Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/university-of-utah-health-care-6870315). One of every four physicians in the U.S. is an internist, many of whom are certified in one of 19 subspecialties, including cardiology, infectious disease and medical oncology.
