## Overview

Dr. Kenji Muro is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois. He received his medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kenji Muro has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kenji-muro-465327#expertise).
