## Overview

Dr. John Harris is an emergency medicine physician in Thomasville, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Alleghany Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/alleghany-memorial-hospital-6361403) and [UNC Health Blue Ridge](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/blue-ridge-healthcare-hospitals-6360427). He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Harris accepts Medicare, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-harris-862460#insurance). Dr. John Harris is highly recommended by patients.
