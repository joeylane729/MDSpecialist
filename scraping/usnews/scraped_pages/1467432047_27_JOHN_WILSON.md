## Overview

Dr. John R. Wilson is a neurologist in Oakbrook Terrace, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rush Oak Park Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-oak-park-hospital-6432420) and [Endeavor Health Elmhurst Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/elmhurst-memorial-hospital-6431620).
