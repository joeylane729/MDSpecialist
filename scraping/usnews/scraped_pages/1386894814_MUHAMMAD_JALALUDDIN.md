## Overview

Dr. Muhammad Jalaluddin is a neurosurgeon in Bath, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bath Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/bath-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6210260) and [St. James Mercy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-james-mercy-hospital-6211860).
