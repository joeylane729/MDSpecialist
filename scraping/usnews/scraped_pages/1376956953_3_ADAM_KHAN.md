## Overview

Dr. Adam A. Khan is a neurosurgeon in Dearborn, Michigan and is affiliated with [Corewell Health Trenton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/oakwood-southshore-medical-center-6442455).
