## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey B. English is a neurologist in Atlanta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Piedmont Atlanta Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/piedmont-hospital-6380180) and [Tanner Medical Center-Villa Rica](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/tanner-medical-center-villa-rica-6381215). He received his medical degree from Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey B. English has expertise in treating Alzheimer's dementia, multiple sclerosis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-english-203674#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey B. English accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-english-203674#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey B. English is highly recommended by patients.
