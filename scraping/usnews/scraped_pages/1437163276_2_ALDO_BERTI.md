## Overview

Dr. Aldo F. Berti is a neurosurgeon in South Miami, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health South Miami Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/south-miami-hospital-6390994), [HCA Florida Mercy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/hca-florida-mercy-hospital-6390572) and [Coral Gables Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/coral-gables-surgery-center-A1794092).
