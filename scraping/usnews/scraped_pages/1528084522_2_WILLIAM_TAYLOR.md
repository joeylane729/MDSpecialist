## Overview

Dr. William K. Taylor is an interventional radiology doctor in Akron, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Summa Health-Akron Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/summa-akron-city-and-st-thomas-hospitals-6410012) and [VA Northeast Ohio Healthcare System-Cleveland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/louis-stokes-cleveland-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6410205).
