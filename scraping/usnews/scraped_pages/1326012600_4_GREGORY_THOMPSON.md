## Overview

Dr. Gregory E. Thompson is a neurosurgeon in Reading, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn State Health St. Joseph](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/st-joseph-medical-center-6230051) and [Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-milton-s-hershey-medical-center-6230705). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory E. Thompson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-thompson-271185#expertise). Dr. Gregory E. Thompson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-thompson-271185#insurance). Dr. Gregory E. Thompson is highly recommended by patients.
