## Overview

Dr. Brent Morgan is a neurosurgeon in McKinney, Texas and is affiliated with [Baylor Scott and White Institute for Rehabilitation-Dallas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baylor-institute-for-rehabilitation-6740915). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Brent Morgan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brent-morgan-2438550#expertise). Dr. Brent Morgan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brent-morgan-2438550#insurance).
