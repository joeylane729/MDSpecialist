## Overview

Dr. George Al Shamy is a neurosurgeon in Shenandoah, Texas and is affiliated with [Memorial Hermann Northeast Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-northeast-hospital-6742059) and [Memorial Hermann Surgery Center Pinecroft](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/memorial-hermann-surgery-center-pinecroft-A2874798). He received his medical degree from American University of Beirut Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. George Al Shamy has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-al-shamy-2337373#expertise). Dr. George Al Shamy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-al-shamy-2337373#insurance).
