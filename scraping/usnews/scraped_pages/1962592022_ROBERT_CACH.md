## Overview

Dr. Robert L. Cach is a neurosurgeon in Pocatello, Idaho and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Portneuf Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/portneuf-medical-center-6820005) and [Eastern Idaho Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/eastern-idaho-regional-medical-center-6820165). He received his medical degree from University of Colorado School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert L. Cach has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-cach-115416#expertise). Dr. Robert L. Cach accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-cach-115416#insurance). Dr. Robert L. Cach is highly recommended by patients.
