## Overview

Dr. George J. Martin is a neurosurgeon in El Paso, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hospitals of Providence Sierra Campus-Tenet Healthcare](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/sierra-medical-center-6741285) and [Kindred Hospital El Paso](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/kindred-hospital-el-paso-6740302). He received his medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. George J. Martin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-martin-760974#expertise). Dr. George J. Martin accepts Medicare, Humana - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-martin-760974#insurance).
