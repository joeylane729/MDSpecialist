## Overview

Dr. David K. Hong is a pediatric infectious disease specialist in Menlo Park, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [John Muir Health-Concord Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/john-muir-medical-center-6930480) and [Lucile Packard Children's Hospital Stanford](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/lucile-salter-packard-childrens-hospital-at-stanford-6933605).
