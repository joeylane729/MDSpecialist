## Overview

Dr. Bassam A. Hadi is a neurosurgeon in Saint Louis, Missouri and is affiliated with [Mercy Hospital South](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/st-anthonys-medical-center-6631160). He received his medical degree from Saint Louis University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bassam A. Hadi has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bassam-hadi-444053#expertise). Dr. Bassam A.
