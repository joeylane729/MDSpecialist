## Overview

Dr. Phillip S. Dickey is a neurosurgeon in Guilford, Connecticut and is affiliated with [Yale New Haven Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/yale-new-haven-hospital-6160400). He received his medical degree from University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Phillip S. Dickey has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-dickey-473035#expertise). Dr. Phillip S. Dickey accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-dickey-473035#insurance). Dr. Phillip S. Dickey is highly recommended by patients.
