## Overview

Dr. Houman H. Khosrovi is a neurosurgeon in Belpre, Ohio. He received his medical degree from West Virginia University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Houman H. Khosrovi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/houman-khosrovi-284555#expertise). Khosrovi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/houman-khosrovi-284555#insurance).
