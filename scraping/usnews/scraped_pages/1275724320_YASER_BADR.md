## Overview

Dr. Yaser Badr is a neurosurgeon in Glendale, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adventist Health Glendale](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/glendale-adventist-medical-center-6930831) and [Adventist Health-White Memorial](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/white-memorial-medical-center-6931770).
