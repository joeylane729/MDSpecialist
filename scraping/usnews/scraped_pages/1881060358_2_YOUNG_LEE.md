## Overview

Dr. Young H. Lee is a pulmonologist in Corona, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Riverside Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/riverside-community-hospital-6932585) and [Corona Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/corona-regional-medical-center-6930045). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Young H. Lee has expertise in treating pneumonia, sleep apnea, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/young-lee-573531#expertise). Dr. Young H. Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/young-lee-573531#insurance). Dr. Young H. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
