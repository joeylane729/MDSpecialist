## Overview

Dr. Charles O. Wood is a neurosurgeon in Lima, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/exempla-good-samaritan-medical-center-6840021) and [Fargo VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nd/fargo-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6640190). He received his medical degree from Emory University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles O. Wood has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-wood-140793#expertise). Dr. Charles O. Wood accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-wood-140793#insurance).
