## Overview

Dr. James M. Arthur is a neurosurgeon in Hot Springs, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHI St. Vincent Hot Springs Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/st-josephs-mercy-health-center-6710300) and [National Park Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/national-park-medical-center-6710290).
