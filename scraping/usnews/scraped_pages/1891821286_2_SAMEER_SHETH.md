## Overview

Dr. Sameer Sheth is a cardiologist in Boston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/massachusetts-general-hospital-6140430) and [Beverly Hospital-Montebello](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/beverly-hospital-6932010).
