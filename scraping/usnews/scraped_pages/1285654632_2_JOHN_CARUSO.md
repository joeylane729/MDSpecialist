## Overview

Dr. John Caruso is an internist in Binghamton, New York and is affiliated with [Our Lady of Lourdes Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/our-lady-of-lourdes-memorial-hospital-6210370). He received his medical degree from Nova Southeastern University - College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Caruso has expertise in treating dementia, coronary artery disease, bronchitis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-caruso-900097#expertise). Dr. John Caruso accepts Aetna, Humana, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-caruso-900097#insurance).
