## Overview

Dr. Lawrence G. Rapp is a neurosurgeon in Clarkston, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Doctors' Hospital of Michigan](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/doctors-hospital-of-michigan-6442120) and [Henry Ford Macomb Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/henry-ford-macomb-hospital-6441830). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Lawrence G. Rapp has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lawrence-rapp-249551#expertise).
