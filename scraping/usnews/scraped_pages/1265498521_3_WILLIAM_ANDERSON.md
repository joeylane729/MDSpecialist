## Overview

Dr. William D. Anderson is an emergency medicine physician in Gilmer, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Crescent Medical Center Lancaster](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/crescent-medical-center-lancaster-6740984) and [Hill Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hill-regional-hospital-6741840). He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William D. Anderson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-anderson-380453#insurance). Dr. William D. Anderson is highly recommended by patients.
