## Overview

Dr. Joseph Georges is a doctor in Phoenix, Arizona and is affiliated with [Banner Desert Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/banner-desert-medical-center-6860016). He received his medical degree from Arizona College of Osteopathic Medicine of Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Joseph Georges has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-georges-3273304#expertise). Dr. Joseph Georges accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-georges-3273304#insurance). Dr. Joseph Georges is highly recommended by patients.
