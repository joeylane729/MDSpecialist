## Overview

Dr. Randolph C. Bishop is a neurosurgeon in Savannah, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Health University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/memorial-university-medical-center-6381027) and [Candler Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/st-josephs-candler-6381080). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Randolph C. Bishop has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/randolph-bishop-199543#expertise). Dr. Randolph C. Bishop accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/randolph-bishop-199543#insurance).
