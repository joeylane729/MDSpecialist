## Overview

Dr. David A. Lewis is an internist in Mountain View, California and is affiliated with [El Camino Health-Mountain View](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/el-camino-hospital-6931392). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David A. Lewis has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-lewis-23778#expertise). Dr. David A. Lewis accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-lewis-23778#insurance). Dr. David A. Lewis is highly recommended by patients.
