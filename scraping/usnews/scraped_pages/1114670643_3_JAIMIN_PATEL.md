## Overview

Dr. Jaimin Patel is an internist in Mechanicsville, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bon Secours Memorial Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/memorial-regional-medical-center-6340896) and [Luminis Health Doctors Community Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/doctors-community-hospital-6320645).
