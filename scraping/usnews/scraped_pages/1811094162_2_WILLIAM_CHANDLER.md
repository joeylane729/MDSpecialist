## Overview

Dr. William Chandler is a radiologist in Winston Salem, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Medical City Arlington](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/medical-center-arlington-6740115) and [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Plano](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-6740945). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. William Chandler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-chandler-1119591#insurance).
