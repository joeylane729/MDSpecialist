## Overview

Dr. Brooks R. Osburn is a neurosurgeon in Temple Terrace, Florida and is affiliated with [Brandon Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/brandon-regional-hospital-6390070). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Brooks R. Osburn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brooks-osburn-1361762#expertise). Dr. Brooks R. Osburn accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brooks-osburn-1361762#insurance). Dr. Brooks R. Osburn is highly recommended by patients.
