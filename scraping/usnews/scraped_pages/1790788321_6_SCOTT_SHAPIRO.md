## Overview

Dr. Scott Shapiro is a cardiologist in Horsham, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Jefferson Abington Hospital-Jefferson Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/abington-memorial-hospital-6230010). He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Scott Shapiro has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/scott-shapiro-975189#expertise). Dr. Scott Shapiro accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/scott-shapiro-975189#insurance). Dr. Scott Shapiro is highly recommended by patients.
