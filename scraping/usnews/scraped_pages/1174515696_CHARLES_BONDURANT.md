## Overview

Dr. Charles P. Bondurant is a neurosurgeon in Columbia, Missouri. He received his medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles P. Bondurant has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-bondurant-168564#expertise). Dr. Charles P. Bondurant accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-bondurant-168564#insurance). Dr. Charles P. Bondurant is highly recommended by patients.
