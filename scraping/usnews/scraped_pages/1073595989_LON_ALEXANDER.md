## Overview

Dr. Lon F. Alexander is a neurosurgeon in Flower Mound, Texas and is affiliated with [North Oaks Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/north-oaks-medical-center-6720254). He received his medical degree from Louisiana State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Lon F. Alexander has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lon-alexander-215165#expertise). Dr. Lon F. Alexander accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lon-alexander-215165#insurance).
