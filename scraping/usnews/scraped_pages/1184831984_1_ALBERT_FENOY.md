## Overview

Dr. Albert Fenoy is a neurosurgeon in Lake Success, New York and is affiliated with [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890). He received his medical degree from Stony Brook University Health Sciences Center School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Albert Fenoy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/albert-fenoy-3838591#insurance).
