## Overview

Dr. Mohamed S. Saleh is a neurosurgeon in Wesley Chapel, Florida and is affiliated with [AdventHealth Tampa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/university-community-hospital-6391103). He received his medical degree from New York Medical College and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Mohamed S. Saleh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mohamed-saleh-997820#expertise).
