## Overview

Dr. Charles G. Miller is an internist in Erwin, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Unicoi County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/unicoi-county-memorial-hospital-6520270). He received his medical degree from University of Pikeville Kentucky College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles G. Miller has expertise in treating arthritis, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-miller-345503#expertise).
