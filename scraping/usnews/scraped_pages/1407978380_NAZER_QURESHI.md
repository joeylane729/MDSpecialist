## Overview

Dr. Nazer Qureshi is a neurosurgeon in New Britain, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hospital of Central Connecticut](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/hospital-of-central-connecticut-6160013) and [CentraState Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/centrastate-medical-center-6220322).
