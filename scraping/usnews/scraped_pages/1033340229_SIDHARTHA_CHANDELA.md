## Overview

Dr. Sidhartha Chandela is a neurosurgeon in Falls Church, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Inova Fairfax Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-fairfax-hospital-6340490) and [Holy Cross Hospital-Silver Spring](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/holy-cross-hospital-6320813).
