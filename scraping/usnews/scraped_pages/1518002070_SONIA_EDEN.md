## Overview

Dr. Sonia V. Eden is a neurosurgeon in Oxford, Mississippi and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Memorial Hospital-North Mississippi](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/baptist-memorial-hospital-north-mississippi-6540693) and [Ascension Borgess Allegan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/allegan-general-hospital-6440030). She received her medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sonia V. Eden has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sonia-eden-590220#expertise). Dr. Sonia V. Eden accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sonia-eden-590220#insurance).
