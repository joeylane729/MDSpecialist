## Overview

Dr. Steven Bailey is an ophthalmologist in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with [OHSU Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/oregon-health-and-science-university-6920570). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven Bailey has expertise in treating visual impairment and blindness, retinal detachment, cataract, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-bailey-943788#expertise). Dr. Steven Bailey accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-bailey-943788#insurance). Dr. Steven Bailey is highly recommended by patients.
