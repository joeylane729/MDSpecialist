## Overview

Dr. Glenn E. Harper is a neurosurgeon in Round Rock, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Seton Williamson Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-williamson-6740412), [Ascension Seton Medical Center Austin](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-6740200) and [Advanced Surgical Center-Round Rock](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/advanced-surgical-center-round-rock-A5886645). He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Glenn E. Harper has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/glenn-harper-32689#expertise). Dr. Glenn E. Harper accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/glenn-harper-32689#insurance).
