## Overview

Dr. Kevin Kelly is a neurosurgeon in Aurora, Illinois and is affiliated with [Rush University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-university-medical-center-6430985). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
