Dr. Francesca D. Tekula is a neurosurgeon in Anderson, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Vincent Anderson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-vincent-anderson-regional-hospital-6420050), [Community Hospital of Anderson and Madison County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/community-hospital-of-anderson-6420008) and [Orthoalliance of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/in/orthoalliance-of-indiana-A8097415).
