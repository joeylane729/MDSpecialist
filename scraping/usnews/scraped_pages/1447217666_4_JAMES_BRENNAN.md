## Overview

Dr. James F. Brennan is an urologist in San Andreas, California. He received his medical degree from Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James F. Brennan has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-brennan-420642#expertise). Dr. James F. Brennan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-brennan-420642#insurance).
