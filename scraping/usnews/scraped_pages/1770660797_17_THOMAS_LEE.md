## Overview

Dr. Thomas D. Lee is a psychiatrist in Arlington Heights, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Endeavor Health Northwest Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northwest-community-healthcare-6432485) and [Ascension St. Alexius Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/st-alexius-medical-center-6431926).
