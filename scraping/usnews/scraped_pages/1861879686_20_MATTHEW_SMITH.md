## Overview

Dr. Matthew Smith is an urologist in Centerville, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Miami Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/miami-valley-hospital-6411190) and [Reid Health-Richmond](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/reid-hospital-and-health-care-services-6421190).
