## Overview

Dr. George S. Ibrahim is an orthopedist in Lansdowne, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [George Washington University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/dc/george-washington-university-hospital-6330140), [Fauquier Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/fauquier-hospital-6341165) and [Stonesprings Surgicenter](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/va/stonesprings-surgicenter-A2508808). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. George S. Ibrahim has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-ibrahim-915921#expertise).
