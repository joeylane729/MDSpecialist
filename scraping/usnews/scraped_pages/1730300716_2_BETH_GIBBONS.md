## Overview

Dr. Beth A. Gibbons (Narasimhan) is a neurosurgeon in Greeley, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Banner Fort Collins Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/banner-fort-collins-medical-center-6840445) and [McKee Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/mckee-medical-center-6840782). She received her medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Beth A. Gibbons (Narasimhan) has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/beth-gibbons-2384389#expertise). Gibbons (Narasimhan) accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/beth-gibbons-2384389#insurance).
