## Overview

Dr. Oliver N. Dold is a neurosurgeon in Decatur, Illinois and is affiliated with [Decatur Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/decatur-memorial-hospital-6431370). He received his medical degree from University of Calgary Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Oliver N. Dold has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/oliver-dold-1014493#expertise). Dr. Oliver N. Dold accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/oliver-dold-1014493#insurance). Dr. Oliver N. Dold is highly recommended by patients.
