## Overview

Dr. Julius A. Silvidi is a neurosurgeon in Fargo, North Dakota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sanford Medical Center Fargo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nd/sanford-fargo-medical-center-6640180) and [HSHS St. Vincent Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-vincent-hospital-6450450). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Julius A. Silvidi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/julius-silvidi-1368615#expertise). Dr. Julius A. Silvidi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/julius-silvidi-1368615#insurance). Dr. Julius A. Silvidi is highly recommended by patients.
