## Overview

Weiner is an anesthesiologist in Temecula, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Loma Linda University Medical Center-Murrieta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/loma-linda-university-medical-center-murrieta-6930409) and [Palomar Medical Center Escondido](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/palomar-medical-center-6930660).
