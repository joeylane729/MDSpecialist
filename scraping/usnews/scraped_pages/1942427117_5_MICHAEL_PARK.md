## Overview

Dr. Michael Park is an internist in Austin, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Seton Medical Center Austin](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-6740200) and [Central Texas Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/central-texas-rehabilitation-hospital-6740392). He received his medical degree from American University of Antigua College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Park has expertise in treating heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-930094#expertise). Dr. Michael Park accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-930094#insurance).
