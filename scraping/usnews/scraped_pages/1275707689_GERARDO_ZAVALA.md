## Overview

Dr. Gerardo Zavala is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-medical-center-6740415) and [South Texas Spine and Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/south-texas-spine-and-surgical-hospital-6740245).
