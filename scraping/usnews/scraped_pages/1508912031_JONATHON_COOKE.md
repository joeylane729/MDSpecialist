## Overview

Dr. Jonathon Cooke is a neurosurgeon in La Mesa, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sharp Grossmont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sharp-grossmont-hospital-6930887) and [Avera McKennan Hospital and University Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sd/avera-mckennan-hospital-and-university-health-center-6650420).
