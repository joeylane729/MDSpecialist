## Overview

Dr. Amy C. Lee is a neurologist in Mountain View, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Good Samaritan Hospital-San Jose](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/good-samaritan-hospital-6930076) and [El Camino Health-Mountain View](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/el-camino-hospital-6931392).
