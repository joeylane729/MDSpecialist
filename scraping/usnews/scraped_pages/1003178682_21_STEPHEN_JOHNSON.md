## Overview

Dr. Stephen C. Johnson is an urologist in Chillicothe, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adena Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/adena-regional-medical-center-6410340) and [Chillicothe Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/chillicothe-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6410370). Johnson has expertise in treating kidney disease, kidney removal surgery, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-johnson-580084#expertise).
