## Overview

Dr. Jason Duong is a neurosurgeon in Duarte, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [City of Hope Comprehensive Cancer Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/city-of-hope-6930590) and [Arrowhead Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/arrowhead-regional-medical-center-6932770).
