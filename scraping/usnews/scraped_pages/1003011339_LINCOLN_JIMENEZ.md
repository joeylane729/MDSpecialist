## Overview

Dr. Lincoln Jimenez is a neurosurgeon in Pensacola, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Sacred Heart Pensacola Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/sacred-heart-hospital-of-pensacola-6390830) and [HCA Florida West Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/west-florida-hospital-6390793).
