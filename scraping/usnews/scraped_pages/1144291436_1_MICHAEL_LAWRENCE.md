## Overview

Dr. Michael Lawrence is an allergist-immunologist in Brockton, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Signature Healthcare Brockton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/signature-healthcare-brockton-hospital-6140630) and [Morton Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/morton-hospital-6142000). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael Lawrence has expertise in treating asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lawrence-359217#expertise). Dr. Michael Lawrence accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lawrence-359217#insurance). Dr. Michael Lawrence is highly recommended by patients.
