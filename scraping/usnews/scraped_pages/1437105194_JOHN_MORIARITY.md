## Overview

Dr. John L. Moriarity is a neurosurgeon in Flowood, Mississippi and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Merit Health River Oaks](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/river-oaks-hospital-6540385), [St. Dominic-Jackson Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/st-dominic-hospital-6540390) and [Advanced Surgical Care](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ms/advanced-surgical-care-A9530476).
