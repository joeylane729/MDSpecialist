## Overview

Dr. Robert J. Bohinski is a neurosurgeon in Cincinnati, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bethesda North Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/bethesda-north-hospital-6410382), [Jewish Hospital-Mercy Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/jewish-hospital-mercy-6419060) and [Mayfield Spine Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/oh/mayfield-spine-surgery-center-A6223510).
