## Overview

Dr. Phillip Kissel is a neurosurgeon in San Luis Obispo, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [French Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/french-hospital-medical-center-6933270) and [Sierra Vista Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sierra-vista-regional-medical-center-6933295). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Phillip Kissel has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-kissel-431458#expertise). Dr. Phillip Kissel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-kissel-431458#insurance).
