## Overview

Dr. Shane A. Hawksworth is a neurosurgeon in El Paso, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Las Palmas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/las-palmas-medical-center-6741315) and [William Beaumont Army Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/william-beaumont-army-medical-center-6741320). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Shane A. Hawksworth has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shane-hawksworth-1208112#expertise). Dr. Shane A. Hawksworth accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shane-hawksworth-1208112#insurance).
