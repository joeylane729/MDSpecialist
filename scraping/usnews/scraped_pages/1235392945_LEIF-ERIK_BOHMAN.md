## Overview

Dr. Leif-Erik Bohman is a neurosurgeon in Newark, Delaware and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ChristianaCare Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/christiana-care-hospital-6310120), [Beebe Healthcare-Lewes Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/beebe-medical-center-6310030), [Delaware Outpatient Center for Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/delaware-outpatient-center-for-surgery-A7531820) and [Delaware Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/delaware-surgery-center-A9330076). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Leif-Erik Bohman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/leif-erik-bohman-685218#expertise). Dr. Leif-Erik Bohman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/leif-erik-bohman-685218#insurance). Dr. Leif-Erik Bohman is highly recommended by patients.
