## Overview

Dr. Matthew S. Erwood is a neurosurgeon in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with [UAB St. Vincent's Birmingham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/st-vincents-birmingham-6530260). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Georgia and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew S. Erwood has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-erwood-3265584#expertise). Dr. Matthew S. Erwood accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-erwood-3265584#insurance).
