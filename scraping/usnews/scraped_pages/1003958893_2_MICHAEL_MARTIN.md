## Overview

Dr. Michael J. Martin is an orthopedist in Tacoma, Washington. He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael J. Martin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-martin-392198#expertise).
