## Overview

Dr. Niteen S. Andalkar is a neurosurgeon in Mesa, Arizona and is affiliated with [Banner-University Medical Center Phoenix](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/banner-good-samaritan-medical-center-6860250) and [Aloe Surgical Center Scottsdale](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/az/aloe-surgical-center-scottsdale-A8259827). He received his medical degree from University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Niteen S. Andalkar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/niteen-andalkar-424856#expertise). Dr. Niteen S. Andalkar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/niteen-andalkar-424856#insurance).
