## Overview

Dr. James C. Mills is an orthopedist in Seneca, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Prisma Health Oconee Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/oconee-medical-center-6370590), [Prisma Health Baptist Easley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/baptist-easley-hospital-6370244) and [Blue Ridge Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/sc/blue-ridge-surgery-center-A9847423). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James C. Mills has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, bursitis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-mills-301475#expertise). Dr. James C. Mills accepts Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-mills-301475#insurance). Dr. James C. Mills is highly recommended by patients.
