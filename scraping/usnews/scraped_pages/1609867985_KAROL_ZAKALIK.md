## Overview

Dr. Karol Zakalik is a neurosurgeon in Royal Oak, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health Beaumont Troy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/beaumont-hospital-6442463) and [Henry Ford Providence Southfield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/providence-hospital-6440780).
