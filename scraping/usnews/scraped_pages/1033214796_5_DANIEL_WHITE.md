## Overview

White is an orthopedist in Casper, Wyoming and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Summit Medical Center-Casper](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/summit-medical-center-6830384), [Wyoming Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/wyoming-medical-center-6830040) and [Wyoming Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wy/wyoming-surgical-center-A7816569).
