## Overview

Dr. Terrence F. Holekamp is a neurosurgeon in Saint Louis, Missouri and is affiliated with [Mercy Hospital St. Louis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/mercy-hospital-6631165). He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Terrence F. Holekamp has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/terrence-holekamp-691198#expertise). Dr. Terrence F. Holekamp accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/terrence-holekamp-691198#insurance). Dr. Terrence F. Holekamp is highly recommended by patients.
