## Overview

Dr. Srinivasan S. Purighalla is a neurosurgeon in Moline, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Genesis Medical Center-Silvis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/genesis-medical-center-6431511) and [UnityPoint Health-Trinity Bettendorf](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/trinity-bettendorf-6620377). He received his medical degree from Rangaraya Medical College NTR and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Srinivasan S. Purighalla has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/srinivasan-purighalla-247415#expertise). Dr. Srinivasan S. Purighalla accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/srinivasan-purighalla-247415#insurance).
