## Overview

Dr. Jonathan P. Mannas is a neurosurgeon in Lubbock, Texas and is affiliated with [Covenant Medical Center-Lubbock](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/covenant-hospital-6742360) and [Covenant High Plains Surgery Center-22nd Street](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/covenant-high-plains-surgery-center-22nd-street-A7835038).
