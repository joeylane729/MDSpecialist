## Overview

Dr. Timothy J. Miller is an anesthesiologist in Davenport, Iowa and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Jackson County Regional Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/jackson-county-regional-health-center-6620895), [Ottumwa Regional Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/ottumwa-regional-health-center-6621105) and [Crow Valley Orthopedic Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ia/crow-valley-orthopedic-surgery-center-A2068335). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy J. Miller has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-miller-144294#expertise). Dr. Timothy J. Miller accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-miller-144294#insurance).
