## Overview

Dr. Joseph F. Emrich is a neurosurgeon in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Berkshire Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/berkshire-medical-center-6141705) and [Geisinger Wyoming Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/geisinger-wyoming-valley-medical-center-6232725). He received his medical degree from McGill University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph F. Emrich has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-emrich-166692#expertise). Dr. Joseph F. Emrich accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-emrich-166692#insurance).
