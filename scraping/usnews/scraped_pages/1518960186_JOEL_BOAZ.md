Boaz is a neurosurgeon in Carmel, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-academic-health-center-6420020) and [Ascension St. Vincent Carmel](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-vincent-carmel-hospital-6420175).
