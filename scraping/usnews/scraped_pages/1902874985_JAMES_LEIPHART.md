## Overview

Dr. James W. Leiphart is a neurosurgeon in Fairfax, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Inova Fair Oaks Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-fair-oaks-hospital-6340231) and [Inova Alexandria Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-alexandria-hospital-6340020). Leiphart has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-leiphart-287324#expertise).
