## Overview

Dr. Brian P. Callahan is a neurosurgeon in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carondelet St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/carondelet-st-marys-hospital-6860480) and [TMC Healthcare-Tucson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/tucson-medical-center-6860020).
