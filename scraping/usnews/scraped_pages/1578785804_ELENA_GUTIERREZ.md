## Overview

Dr. Elena Gutierrez is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [SSM Health St. Mary's Hospital-Madison](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-marys-hospital-6450810). She received her medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Elena Gutierrez has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/elena-gutierrez-121721#expertise). Dr. Elena Gutierrez accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/elena-gutierrez-121721#insurance). Dr. Elena Gutierrez is highly recommended by patients.
