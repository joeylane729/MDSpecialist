## Overview

Dr. Kurtis Birch is a neurosurgeon in West Hollywood, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cedars-Sinai Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/cedars-sinai-medical-center-6930444) and [Providence Santa Rosa Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/santa-rosa-memorial-hospital-6930109).
