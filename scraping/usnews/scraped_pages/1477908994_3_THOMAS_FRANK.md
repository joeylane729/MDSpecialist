## Overview

Dr. Thomas Frank is a neurosurgeon in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carondelet St. Joseph's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/carondelet-st-josephs-hospital-6860460) and [Mt.
