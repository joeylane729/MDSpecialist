## Overview

Dr. Somnath N. Nair is a neurosurgeon in Boynton Beach, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Bethesda Hospital East & West](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/bethesda-memorial-hospital-6390050) and [Delray Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/delray-medical-center-6390081).
