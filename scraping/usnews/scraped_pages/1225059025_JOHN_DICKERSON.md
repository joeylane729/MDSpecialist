## Overview

Dr. John R. Dickerson is a neurosurgeon in Wichita, Kansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Via Christi Hospital Wichita](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/via-christi-regional-medical-center-6670017) and [Kansas Spine and Specialty Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/kansas-spine-hospital-6670036).
