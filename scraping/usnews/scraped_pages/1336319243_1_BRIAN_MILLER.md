## Overview

Dr. Brian R. Miller is an internist in Plymouth, Minnesota and is affiliated with [Allina Health Abbott Northwestern Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/allina-abbott-northwestern-hospital-6610815).
