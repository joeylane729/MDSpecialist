## Overview

Dr. Perry J. Argires is a neurosurgeon in Lancaster, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn Medicine Lancaster General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/lancaster-general-hospital-6231120), [WellSpan Ephrata Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/ephrata-community-hospital-6230745), [Advanced Surgery Center of Lancaster](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/pa/advanced-surgery-center-of-lancaster-A8678819) and [Wellspan Ephrata Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/pa/wellspan-ephrata-community-hospital-A5431448). Argires accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/perry-argires-191407#insurance). Dr. Perry J.
