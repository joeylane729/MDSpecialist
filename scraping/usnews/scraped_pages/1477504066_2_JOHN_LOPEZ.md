## Overview

Dr. John A. Lopez is a neurosurgeon in Fairbanks, Alaska and is affiliated with [Fairbanks Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ak/fairbanks-memorial-hospital-6940058). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John A.
