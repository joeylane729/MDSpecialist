## Overview

Dr. Eric E. Potts is a neurologist in Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Froedtert Menomonee Falls Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/community-memorial-hospital-6450905) and [Aurora St. Luke's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aurora-st-lukes-medical-center-6450017). Potts has expertise in treating dementia, Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-potts-500046#expertise).
