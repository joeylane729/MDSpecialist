## Overview

Dr. John A. Jenkins is a neurosurgeon in Orlando, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Orlando Health-Orlando Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/orlando-regional-medical-center-6390695) and [AdventHealth Orlando](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/florida-hospital-6390690). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John A. Jenkins has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-jenkins-401848#expertise).
