## Overview

Dr. John F. Keller is a neurosurgeon in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health Grand Rapids Hospitals-Blodgett and Butterworth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-6440021), [Trinity Health Grand Rapids Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-marys-health-care-6441210) and [Grand Valley Surgical Center-Grand Rapids](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/grand-valley-surgical-center-grand-rapids-A8116196). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John F. Keller has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-keller-76980#expertise). Dr. John F. Keller accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-keller-76980#insurance).
