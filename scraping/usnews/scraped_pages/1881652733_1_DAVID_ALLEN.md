## Overview

Dr. David R. Allen is a pulmonologist in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [SSM Health St. Anthony Hospital-Oklahoma City](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-anthony-hospital-6730830) and [McAlester Regional Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/mcalester-regional-health-center-6730590). Allen has expertise in treating sleep apnea, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-allen-286149#expertise). Dr. David R.
