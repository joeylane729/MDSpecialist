## Overview

Dr. Neda Jafari is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with [HCA Houston Healthcare Clear Lake](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/clear-lake-regional-medical-center-6740149). She received her medical degree from Des Moines University College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Neda Jafari has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neda-jafari-801719#expertise). Dr. Neda Jafari is highly recommended by patients.
