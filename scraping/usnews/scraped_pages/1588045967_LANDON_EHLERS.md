## Overview

Dr. Landon D. Ehlers is a neurosurgeon in Omaha, Nebraska and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Midwest Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/midwest-surgical-hospital-6660133) and [Nebraska Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/methodist-hospital-6660710).
