## Overview

Dr. Peter M. Nguyen is a preventive medicine physician in Victoria, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Citizens Medical Center-Victoria](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/citizens-medical-center-6743625) and [DeTar Healthcare System-Victoria](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/detar-hospital-navarro-6743630).
