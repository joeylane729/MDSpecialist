## Overview

Dr. Arthur G. Arand is a neurosurgeon in Hamilton, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Jewish Hospital-Mercy Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/jewish-hospital-mercy-6419060), [Mercy Health-Fairfield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/mercy-hospital-6410855) and [Mayfield Spine Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/oh/mayfield-spine-surgery-center-A6223510). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Arthur G. Arand has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arthur-arand-489102#expertise). Dr. Arthur G. Arand accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arthur-arand-489102#insurance) below. Dr. Arthur G. Arand is highly recommended by patients.
