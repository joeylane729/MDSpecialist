## Overview

Dr. Siddharth U. Shetgeri is a neurosurgeon in Westerville, Ohio and is affiliated with [Kettering Health Dayton](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/grandview-medical-center-6411182) and [Whitehall Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/oh/whitehall-surgery-center-A4727582). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Siddharth U.
