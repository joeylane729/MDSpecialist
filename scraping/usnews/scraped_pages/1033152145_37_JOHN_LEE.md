## Overview

Dr. John Lee is an internist in Arcadia, California and is affiliated with [San Gabriel Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/san-gabriel-valley-medical-center-6933162). He received his medical degree from Universidad Autonoma de Ciudad Juarez and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Lee has expertise in treating bronchitis, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1363985#expertise). Dr. John Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1363985#insurance).
