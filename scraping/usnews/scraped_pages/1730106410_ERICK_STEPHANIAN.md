## Overview

Dr. Erick Stephanian is a neurosurgeon in Lafayette, Indiana and is affiliated with [Union Hospital-Terre Haute](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/union-hospital-6421366). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Erick Stephanian has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/erick-stephanian-454085#expertise). Dr. Erick Stephanian accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/erick-stephanian-454085#insurance). Dr. Erick Stephanian is highly recommended by patients.
