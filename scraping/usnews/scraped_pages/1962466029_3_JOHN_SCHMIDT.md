## Overview

Dr. John G. Schmidt is a neurologist in Oroville, California and is affiliated with [Oroville Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/oroville-hospital-6932304). He received his medical degree from University of Minnesota Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John G. Schmidt has expertise in treating dementia, Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-schmidt-349105#expertise). Dr. John G. Schmidt accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-schmidt-349105#insurance). Dr. John G. Schmidt is highly recommended by patients.
