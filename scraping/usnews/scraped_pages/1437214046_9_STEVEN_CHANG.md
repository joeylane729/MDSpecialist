## Overview

Chang is a general surgeon in Laguna Beach, California and is affiliated with [MemorialCare Saddleback Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/saddleback-memorial-medical-center-6930272), [Laguna Niguel Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/laguna-niguel-surgery-center-A6193242) and [Specialty Surgical Center of Irvine](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/specialty-surgical-center-of-irvine-A4163634).
