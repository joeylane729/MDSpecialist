## Overview

Dr. Mark Corriveau is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [SSM Health St. Mary's Hospital-Madison](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-marys-hospital-6450810). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Mark Corriveau has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-corriveau-1060558#expertise).
