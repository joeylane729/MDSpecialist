## Overview

Dr. Michael E. Tobias is a neurosurgeon in White Plains, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Maria Fareri Children's Hospital at Westchester Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/maria-fareri-childrens-hospital-at-westchester-medical-center-PA6215150) and [Westchester Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/westchester-medical-center-6215150). He received his medical degree from The Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael E. Tobias has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-tobias-669866#expertise). Dr. Michael E. Tobias accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-tobias-669866#insurance). Dr. Michael E. Tobias is highly recommended by patients.
