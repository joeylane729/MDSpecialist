## Overview

Dr. Peter Kim is a pulmonologist in Miramar, Florida and is affiliated with [Memorial Hospital Miramar](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/memorial-hospital-6390111). He received his medical degree from Stony Brook University Health Sciences Center School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter Kim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-643700#insurance). Dr. Peter Kim is highly recommended by patients.
