## Overview

Dr. James Kalyvas is a neurosurgeon in New Orleans, Louisiana and is affiliated with [Ochsner Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/ochsner-medical-center-6720631). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James Kalyvas has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-kalyvas-936073#expertise). Dr. James Kalyvas accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-kalyvas-936073#insurance). Dr. James Kalyvas is highly recommended by patients.
