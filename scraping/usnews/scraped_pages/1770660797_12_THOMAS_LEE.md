## Overview

Dr. Thomas T. Lee is an obstetrician-gynecologist in City Of Industry, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Chino Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/chino-valley-medical-center-6930401) and [Montclair Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/montclair-hospital-medical-center-6932000).
