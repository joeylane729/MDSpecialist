## Overview

Dr. Mark Weiner is a neurosurgeon in Canton, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Aultman Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/aultman-hospital-6410280) and [Cleveland Clinic Union Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/union-hospital-6411260). He received his medical degree from University of Wisconsin School of Medicine and Public Health and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark Weiner has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-weiner-2557150#expertise). Dr. Mark Weiner accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-weiner-2557150#insurance). Dr. Mark Weiner is highly recommended by patients.
