## Overview

Dr. Marc D. Moisi is a neurosurgeon in Detroit, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rehabilitation Institute of Michigan at Detroit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/dmc-rehabilitation-institute-of-michigan-6440803) and [Hurley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/hurley-medical-center-6441030).
