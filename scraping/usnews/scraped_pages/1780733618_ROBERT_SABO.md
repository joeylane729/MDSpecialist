## Overview

Dr. Robert A. Sabo is a neurosurgeon in Somers Point, New Jersey and is affiliated with [Shore Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/shore-memorial-hospital-6221330). He received his medical degree from Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert A. Sabo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-sabo-64115#expertise). Dr. Robert A. Sabo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-sabo-64115#insurance).
