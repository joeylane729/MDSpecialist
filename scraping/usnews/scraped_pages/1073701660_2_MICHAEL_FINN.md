## Overview

Dr. Michael Finn is a neurosurgeon in Edina, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/university-of-colorado-hospital-6840430) and [Denver Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/denver-health-medical-center-6840290).
