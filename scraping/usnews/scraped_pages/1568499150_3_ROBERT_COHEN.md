## Overview

Dr. Robert H. Cohen is an internist in Lincoln, Rhode Island and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Miriam Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/miriam-hospital-6150130) and [Rhode Island Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/rhode-island-hospital-6150150). He received his medical degree from New York Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert H. Cohen has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-cohen-139802#expertise). Dr. Robert H. Cohen accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-cohen-139802#insurance) below.
