## Overview

Dr. John D. Edwards is a vascular surgeon in Arlington Heights, Illinois and is affiliated with [Endeavor Health Northwest Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northwest-community-healthcare-6432485) and [Northwest Community Day Surgery Center II](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/northwest-community-day-surgery-center-ii-A4321537). He received his medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John D. Edwards accepts Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-edwards-350887#insurance). Dr. John D. Edwards is highly recommended by patients.
