## Overview

Dr. Robert D. Lang is a pediatrician in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Corewell Health Grand Rapids Hospitals-Blodgett and Butterworth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-6440021) and [Corewell Health Helen DeVos Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-helen-devos-childrens-hospital-PA6440021). He received his medical degree from University of Minnesota Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert D. Lang has expertise in treating heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-lang-130883#expertise). Dr. Robert D. Lang accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-lang-130883#insurance). Dr. Robert D. Lang is highly recommended by patients.
