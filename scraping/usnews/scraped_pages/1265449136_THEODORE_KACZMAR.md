## Overview

Dr. Theodore Kaczmar is a neurosurgeon in Salinas, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Salinas Valley Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/salinas-valley-memorial-hospital-6932745) and [Community Hospital of the Monterey Peninsula](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/community-hospital-monterey-peninsula-6930385). He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Theodore Kaczmar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/theodore-kaczmar-486430#expertise). Dr. Theodore Kaczmar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/theodore-kaczmar-486430#insurance).
