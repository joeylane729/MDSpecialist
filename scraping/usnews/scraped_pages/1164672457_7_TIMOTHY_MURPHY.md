## Overview

Dr. Timothy P. Murphy is an internist in Weston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Newton-Wellesley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/newton-wellesley-hospital-6141530). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy P. Murphy has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, aneurysm, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-murphy-140899#expertise). Dr. Timothy P. Murphy accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-murphy-140899#insurance).
