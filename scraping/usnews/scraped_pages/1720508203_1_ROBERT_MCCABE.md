## Overview

Dr. Robert McCabe is an infectious disease specialist in Castro Valley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Eden Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/eden-medical-center-6930133) and [Highland Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alameda-county-medical-center-6930275).
