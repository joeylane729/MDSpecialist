## Overview

Dr. Drew A. Spencer is a neurosurgeon in Palos Heights, Illinois and is affiliated with [Endeavor Health Edward Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/edward-hospital-6432350). He received his medical degree from Southern Illinois University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Drew A. Spencer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/drew-spencer-1221233#expertise). Dr. Drew A. Spencer accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/drew-spencer-1221233#insurance). Dr. Drew A. Spencer is highly recommended by patients.
