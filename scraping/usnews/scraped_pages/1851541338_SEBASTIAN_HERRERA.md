## Overview

Dr. Sebastian R. Herrera is a neurosurgeon in Shenandoah, Texas. He received his medical degree from Pontifical Xavierian University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sebastian R. Herrera has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sebastian-herrera-694621#expertise).
