## Overview

Dr. Timothy W. Vogel is a neurosurgeon in Ocala, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Boston Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/boston-childrens-hospital-6140270), [Hackensack Meridian Health JM Sanzari and K Hovnanian Children’s Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/joseph-m-sanzari-childrens-hospital-at-hackensack-university-medical-center-PA6220340) and [Hudson Crossing Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nj/hudson-crossing-surgery-center-A2736575). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy W. Vogel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-vogel-3345797#insurance).
