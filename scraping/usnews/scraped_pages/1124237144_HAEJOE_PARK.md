## Overview

Dr. Haejoe Park is a neurosurgeon in Hershey, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-milton-s-hershey-medical-center-6230705) and [Regional Hospital of Scranton](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/regional-hospital-of-scranton-6233000). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Haejoe Park has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/haejoe-park-624521#expertise). Dr. Haejoe Park accepts Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/haejoe-park-624521#insurance).
