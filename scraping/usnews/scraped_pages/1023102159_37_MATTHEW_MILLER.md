## Overview

Dr. Matthew E. Miller is a pulmonologist in Lakewood, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Anthony Hospital-Lakewood](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/st-anthony-hospital-6840385) and [OHSU Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/oregon-health-and-science-university-6920570). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew E. Miller has expertise in treating pneumonia, pulmonary heart disease, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-miller-1159670#expertise). Dr. Matthew E. Miller accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-miller-1159670#insurance).
