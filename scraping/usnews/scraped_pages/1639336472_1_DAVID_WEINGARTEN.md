## Overview

Dr. David Weingarten is an internist in Beaverton, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Legacy Good Samaritan Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-good-samaritan-hospital-and-health-center-6920418) and [Providence St. Vincent Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-st-vincent-medical-center-6920540). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Weingarten has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-weingarten-2531821#expertise). Dr. David Weingarten accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-weingarten-2531821#insurance).
