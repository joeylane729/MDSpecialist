## Overview

Dr. Patrick Kim is a neurosurgeon in Tampa, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Tampa General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/tampa-general-hospital-6391060) and [UW Medicine-Harborview Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/harborview-medical-center-6910610). He received his medical degree from Louisiana State University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Patrick Kim has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-kim-1122891#expertise). Dr. Patrick Kim accepts Medicare, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-kim-1122891#insurance).
