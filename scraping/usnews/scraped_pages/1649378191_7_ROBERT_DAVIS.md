## Overview

Dr. Robert F. Davis is an orthopedist in Nashua, New Hampshire and is affiliated with [Southern New Hampshire Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nh/southern-new-hampshire-medical-center-6120290). He received his medical degree from West Virginia University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert F. Davis has expertise in treating arthritis, rotator cuff injury, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-davis-308663#expertise).
