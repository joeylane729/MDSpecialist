## Overview

Dr. Phillip V. Parry is a neurosurgeon in Marietta, Georgia and is affiliated with [WellStar Kennestone Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/kennestone-hospital-6380775). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Phillip V. Parry has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-parry-3840868#expertise). Dr. Phillip V. Parry accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/phillip-parry-3840868#insurance).
