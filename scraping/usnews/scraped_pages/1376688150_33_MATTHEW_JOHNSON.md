## Overview

Dr. Matthew T. Johnson is an urologist in Chico, California and is affiliated with [Enloe Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/enloe-medical-center-6930096) and [Center for Urologic Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mo/center-for-urologic-surgery-A8289065). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew T. Johnson has expertise in treating surgical removal of the bladder, kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-johnson-1390133#expertise). Dr. Matthew T. Johnson accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-johnson-1390133#insurance).
