## Overview

Dr. John C. Lee is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Honolulu, Hawaii and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kapiolani Medical Center for Women and Children](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/kapiolani-medical-center-for-women-6950335) and [Queen's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/queens-medical-center-6950380). He received his medical degree from University of Hawaii John A Burns School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John C. Lee accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-674294#insurance). Dr. John C. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
