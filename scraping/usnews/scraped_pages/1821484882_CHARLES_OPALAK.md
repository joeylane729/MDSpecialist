## Overview

Dr. Charles Opalak is a neurosurgeon in Greenville, South Carolina and is affiliated with [Prisma Health Greenville Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/greenville-memorial-hospital-6370323).
