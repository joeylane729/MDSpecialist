## Overview

Dr. Michael S. Muhlbauer is a neurosurgeon in Memphis, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Le Bonheur Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/le-bonheur-childrens-hospital-PA6520785), [Baptist Memorial Hospital for Women](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-for-women-6520022) and [Semmes Murphey Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tn/semmes-murphey-surgery-center-A7182751).
