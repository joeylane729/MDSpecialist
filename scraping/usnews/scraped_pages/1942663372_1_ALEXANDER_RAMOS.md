## Overview

Dr. Alexander Ramos is a neurologist in Bethesda, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Johns Hopkins Medicine-Suburban Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/suburban-hospital-6320350) and [Holy Cross Hospital-Silver Spring](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/holy-cross-hospital-6320813). He received his medical degree from Louisiana State University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Alexander Ramos has expertise in treating Alzheimer's dementia, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alexander-ramos-1152942#expertise). Dr. Alexander Ramos accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alexander-ramos-1152942#insurance).
