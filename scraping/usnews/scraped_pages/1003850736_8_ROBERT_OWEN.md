## Overview

Dr. Robert J. Owen is an orthopedist in Cumming, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northside Hospital Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northside-hospital-6380171) and [WellStar Windy Hill Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/wellstar-windy-hill-hospital-6380215). He received his medical degree from Emory University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Robert J.
