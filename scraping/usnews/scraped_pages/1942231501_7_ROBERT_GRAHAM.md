## Overview

Dr. Robert M. Graham is an oncologist in Chattanooga, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Erlanger Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/erlanger-medical-center-6520065). He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Robert M. Graham has expertise in treating lung cancer, breast cancer, small intestine/colorectal cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-graham-3424767#expertise). Dr. Robert M. Graham is highly recommended by patients.
