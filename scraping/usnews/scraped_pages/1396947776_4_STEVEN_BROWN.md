## Overview

Brown is a family medicine doctor in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AHN Allegheny General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/allegheny-general-hospital-6230041) and [AHN West Penn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/western-pennsylvania-hospital-6232690).
