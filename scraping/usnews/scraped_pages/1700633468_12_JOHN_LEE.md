## Overview

Dr. John Lee is a psychiatrist in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cedars-Sinai Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/cedars-sinai-medical-center-6930444) and [Stewart and Lynda Resnick Neuropsychiatric Hospital at UCLA](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/resnick-neuropsychiatric-hospital-at-ucla-6931647). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John Lee has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1223518#expertise). Dr. John Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1223518#insurance).
