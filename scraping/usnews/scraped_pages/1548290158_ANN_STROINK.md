## Overview

Dr. Ann R. Stroink is a neurosurgeon in Bloomington, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carle BroMenn Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-bromenn-medical-center-6430245) and [Carle Eureka Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-eureka-hospital-6431623).
