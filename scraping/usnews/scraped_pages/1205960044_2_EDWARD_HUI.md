## Overview

Dr. Edward K. Hui is a geriatrician in Santa Monica, California and is affiliated with [UCLA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ucla-medical-center-6931755). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Edward K. Hui has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, heart failure, sleep apnea, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-hui-438443#expertise).
