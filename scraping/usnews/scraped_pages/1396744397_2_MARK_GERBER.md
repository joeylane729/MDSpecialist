## Overview

Dr. Mark B. Gerber is a neurosurgeon in Naples, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [NCH Baker Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/naples-community-hospital-6390669), [Physicians Regional-Pine Ridge Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/physicians-regional-medical-center-pine-ridge-6390103) and [Surgery Center of Naples](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/surgery-center-of-naples-A3568584).
