## Overview

Dr. Jay V. Howington is a neurosurgeon in Savannah, Georgia and is affiliated with [Memorial Health University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/memorial-university-medical-center-6381027) and [Neurological Institute Ambulatory Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ga/neurological-institute-ambulatory-surgical-center-A1924458). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Georgia and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jay V. Howington has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jay-howington-4052100#expertise). Dr. Jay V. Howington accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jay-howington-4052100#insurance). Dr. Jay V. Howington is highly recommended by patients.
