## Overview

Dr. Christopher Chittum is a neurosurgeon in Spartanburg, South Carolina and is affiliated with [Spartanburg Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/spartanburg-regional-medical-center-6370610) and [Spartanburg Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/sc/spartanburg-surgery-center-A3249530).
