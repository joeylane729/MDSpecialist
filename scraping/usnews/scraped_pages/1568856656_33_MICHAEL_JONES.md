## Overview

Dr. Michael C. Jones is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Harrisburg, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-6360280) and [Atrium Health-Cabarrus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-northeast-6360360).
