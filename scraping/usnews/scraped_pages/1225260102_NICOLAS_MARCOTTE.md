## Overview

Dr. Nicolas Marcotte is a neurosurgeon in Boston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [New England Baptist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/new-england-baptist-hospital-6140460) and [Northridge Surgical Suites](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nh/northridge-surgical-suites-A5339870). He received his medical degree from McGill University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Nicolas Marcotte has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicolas-marcotte-716676#expertise). Dr. Nicolas Marcotte accepts Aetna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicolas-marcotte-716676#insurance).
