## Overview

Dr. John C. Shin is an ophthalmologist in San Rafael, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MarinHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marin-general-hospital-6933370), [Novato Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/novato-community-hospital-6932123) and [Marin Ophthalmic Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/marin-ophthalmic-surgery-center-A8741729). He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John C. Shin has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, shingles, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-shin-126851#expertise). Dr. John C. Shin accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-shin-126851#insurance). Dr. John C. Shin is highly recommended by patients.
