## Overview

Dr. Margaret A. Carmody is a neurosurgeon in Sioux Falls, South Dakota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Avera McKennan Hospital and University Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sd/avera-mckennan-hospital-and-university-health-center-6650420) and [Royal C. Johnson VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sd/sioux-falls-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6650425). She received her medical degree from Medical College of Wisconsin and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Margaret A. Carmody has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/margaret-carmody-702443#expertise). Dr. Margaret A. Carmody accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/margaret-carmody-702443#insurance). Dr. Margaret A. Carmody is highly recommended by patients.
