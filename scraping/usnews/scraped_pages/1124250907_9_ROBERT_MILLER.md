## Overview

Dr. Robert A. Miller is an ENT-otolaryngologist in Tempe, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HonorHealth Tempe Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/tempe-st-lukes-hospital-6860390) and [Banner Desert Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/banner-desert-medical-center-6860016).
