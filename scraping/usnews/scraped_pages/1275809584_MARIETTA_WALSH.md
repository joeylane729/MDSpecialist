## Overview

Dr. Marietta E. Walsh is a neurosurgeon in Waterloo, Iowa and is affiliated with [MercyOne Waterloo Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/covenant-medical-center-6621375). She received her medical degree from Touro College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Marietta E. Walsh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marietta-walsh-1179256#expertise). Dr. Marietta E. Walsh accepts Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marietta-walsh-1179256#insurance). Dr. Marietta E. Walsh is highly recommended by patients.
