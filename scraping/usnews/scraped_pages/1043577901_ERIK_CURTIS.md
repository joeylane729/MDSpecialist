## Overview

Dr. Erik I. Curtis is a neurosurgeon in Scottsdale, Arizona and is affiliated with [HonorHealth John C. Lincoln Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/john-c-lincoln-north-mountain-hospital-6860253). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Erik I. Curtis has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/erik-curtis-766274#expertise). Dr. Erik I. Curtis accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/erik-curtis-766274#insurance). Dr. Erik I. Curtis is highly recommended by patients.
