## Overview

Dr. Gordon T. Sakamoto is a neurosurgeon in Burlingame, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mills-Peninsula Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mills-peninsula-health-services-6930315) and [Sequoia Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sequoia-hospital-6932555). He received his medical degree from Stanford University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gordon T. Sakamoto has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gordon-sakamoto-654849#expertise). Dr. Gordon T. Sakamoto accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gordon-sakamoto-654849#insurance).
