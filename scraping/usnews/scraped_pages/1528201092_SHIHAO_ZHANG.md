## Overview

Dr. Shihao Zhang is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma. He received his medical degree from LSU School of Medicine in Shreveport and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Shihao Zhang has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shihao-zhang-812023#expertise). Dr. Shihao Zhang is highly recommended by patients.
