## Overview

Dr. Magdy S. Shady is a neurosurgeon in Stony Brook, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Stony Brook University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/stony-brook-university-hospital-6214775) and [Good Samaritan University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/good-samaritan-hospital-medical-center-6215295).
