## Overview

Dr. Michael Park is an internist in Atlanta, Georgia and is affiliated with [Piedmont Atlanta Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/piedmont-hospital-6380180). He received his medical degree from Touro University Nevada College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Park has expertise in treating kidney disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-1082498#expertise). Dr. Michael Park accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-park-1082498#insurance). Dr. Michael Park is highly recommended by patients.
