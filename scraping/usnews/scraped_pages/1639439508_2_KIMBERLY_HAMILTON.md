## Overview

Dr. Kimberly Hamilton is a neurosurgeon in Chapel Hill, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [North Carolina Children's Hospital at UNC](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/north-carolina-childrens-hospital-at-unc-PA6360260) and [UNC Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/university-of-north-carolina-hospitals-6360260). She received her medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Kimberly Hamilton accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kimberly-hamilton-895345#insurance).
