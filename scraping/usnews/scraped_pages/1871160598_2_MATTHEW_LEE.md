## Overview

Dr. Matthew C. Lee is an orthopedist in Tallahassee, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA Florida Capital Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/capital-regional-medical-center-6391033) and [Tallahassee Memorial Healthcare](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/tallahassee-memorial-hospital-6391030). He received his medical degree from Florida State University College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew C. Lee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-lee-110861#expertise). Dr. Matthew C. Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-lee-110861#insurance). Dr. Matthew C. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
