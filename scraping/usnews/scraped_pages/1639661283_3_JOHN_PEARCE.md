Pearce is an orthopedist in Austin, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arise Austin Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/austin-surgical-hospital-6740242), [Ascension Seton Medical Center Austin](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-6740200), [CP Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/cp-surgery-center-A1823789) and [Orthopaedic Associates of Central Texas Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/orthopaedic-associates-of-central-texas-surgery-center-A3253075).
