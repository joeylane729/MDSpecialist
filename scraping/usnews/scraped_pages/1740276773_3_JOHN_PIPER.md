## Overview

Piper is an infectious disease specialist in Newark, Delaware and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ChristianaCare Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/christiana-care-hospital-6310120) and [St. Francis Hospital-Wilmington](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/st-francis-hospital-6310140).
