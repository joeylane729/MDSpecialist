## Overview

Dr. Marc A. Goldman is a gastroenterologist in Springfield, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baystate Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/baystate-medical-center-6141955) and [Mercy Medical Center-Springfield](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/mercy-medical-center-6141940).
