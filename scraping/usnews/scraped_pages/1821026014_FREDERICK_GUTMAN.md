Dr. Frederick Gutman is a neurosurgeon in Stony Brook, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Stony Brook University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/stony-brook-university-hospital-6214775) and [St. Charles Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-charles-hospital-6214055).
