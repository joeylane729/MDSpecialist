## Overview

Dr. Philip S. Lee is a neurosurgeon in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Geisinger Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/geisinger-medical-center-6230550) and [Geisinger Wyoming Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/geisinger-wyoming-valley-medical-center-6232725). He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Philip S. Lee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/philip-lee-1070385#expertise). Dr. Philip S. Lee accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/philip-lee-1070385#insurance). Dr. Philip S. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
