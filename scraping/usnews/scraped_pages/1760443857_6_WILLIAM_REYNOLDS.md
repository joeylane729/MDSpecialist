## Overview

Dr. William D. Reynolds is an ophthalmologist in Tampa, Florida and is affiliated with [St. Joseph's Hospital-Tampa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/st-josephs-hospital-6390053) and [Columbia Eye and Specialty Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/columbia-eye-and-specialty-surgery-center-A7213675).
