## Overview

Dr. Clark H. Allen is a neurosurgeon in Idaho Falls, Idaho. He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Clark H. Allen has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/clark-allen-179675#expertise).
