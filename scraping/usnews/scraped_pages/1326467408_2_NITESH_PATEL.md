## Overview

Dr. Nitesh M. Patel is a nephrologist in Voorhees, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Inspira Medical Center-Vineland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/south-jersey-healthcare-regional-medical-center-6220125) and [Jefferson Washington Township, Cherry Hill and Stratford Hospitals-Jefferson Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/kennedy-university-hospital-6221345).
