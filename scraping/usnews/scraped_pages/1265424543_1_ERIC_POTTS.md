## Overview

Dr. Eric A. Potts is a neurosurgeon in Carmel, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-academic-health-center-6420020) and [Indiana Spine Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/indiana-spine-hospital-6429162).
