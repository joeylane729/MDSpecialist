## Overview

Dr. Frank J. Coufal is a neurosurgeon in La Jolla, California and is affiliated with [Scripps La Jolla Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/scripps-la-jolla-hospitals-and-clinics-6931130).
