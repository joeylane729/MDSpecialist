## Overview

Dr. Frank La Marca is a neurosurgeon in Ann Arbor, Michigan and is affiliated with [Henry Ford Jackson Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/allegiance-health-6441500) and [Grass Lake Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/grass-lake-surgery-center-A9515777).
