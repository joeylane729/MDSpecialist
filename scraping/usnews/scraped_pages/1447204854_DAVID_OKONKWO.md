## Overview

Dr. David O. Okonkwo is a neurosurgeon in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/childrens-hospital-of-pittsburgh-of-upmc-6232430) and [UPMC Mercy](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/upmc-mercy-6232510). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David O. Okonkwo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-okonkwo-362918#expertise).
