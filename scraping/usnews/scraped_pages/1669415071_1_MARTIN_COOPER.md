## Overview

Dr. Martin M. Cooper is a psychiatrist in Farmington, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UConn John Dempsey Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/university-of-connecticut-health-center-john-dempsey-hospital-6160215) and [Hartford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/hartford-hospital-6160003). He received his medical degree from University of Connecticut and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Martin M. Cooper has expertise in treating dementia, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/martin-cooper-359024#expertise). Dr. Martin M. Cooper accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/martin-cooper-359024#insurance). Dr. Martin M. Cooper is highly recommended by patients.
