## Overview

Dr. Mark J. Kotapka is a neurosurgeon in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Jefferson Einstein Philadelphia Hospital-Jefferson Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/einstein-medical-center-6231634) and [Jefferson Einstein Montgomery Hospital-Jefferson Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/einstein-medical-center-montgomery-6230515).
