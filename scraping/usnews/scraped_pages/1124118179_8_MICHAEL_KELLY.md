## Overview

Dr. Michael A. Kelly is an emergency medicine physician in Welch, West Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Welch Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/welch-community-hospital-6350780) and [Clinch Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/clinch-valley-medical-center-6340800). He received his medical degree from Dalhousie University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael A. Kelly has expertise in treating dementia, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kelly-883044#expertise). Dr. Michael A. Kelly accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kelly-883044#insurance). Dr. Michael A. Kelly is highly recommended by patients.
