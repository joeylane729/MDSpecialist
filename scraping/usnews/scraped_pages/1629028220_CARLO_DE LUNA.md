## Overview

Dr. Carlo M. De Luna is a neurosurgeon in Hershey, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Wilkes-Barre General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/wilkes-barre-general-hospital-6230026). He received his medical degree from University at Buffalo School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Carlo M. De Luna has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carlo-de-luna-3041062#expertise). Dr. Carlo M. De Luna accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carlo-de-luna-3041062#insurance).
