## Overview

Dr. Alan Siu is a neurosurgeon in Ventura, California and is affiliated with [Ventura County Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ventura-county-medical-center-6933820). He received his medical degree from Cleveland Clinic Lerner College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Alan Siu has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-siu-710448#expertise). Dr. Alan Siu accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-siu-710448#insurance).
