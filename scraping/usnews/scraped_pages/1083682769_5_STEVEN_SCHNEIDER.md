## Overview

Dr. Steven Schneider is a psychiatrist in Indianapolis, Indiana and is affiliated with [Adams Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/adams-memorial-hospital-6420240). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven Schneider has expertise in treating dementia, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-schneider-326582#expertise). Dr. Steven Schneider accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-schneider-326582#insurance).
