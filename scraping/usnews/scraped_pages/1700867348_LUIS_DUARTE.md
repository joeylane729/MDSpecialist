## Overview

Dr. Luis E. Duarte is a neurosurgeon in San Angelo, Texas and is affiliated with [Shannon Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/shannon-medical-center-6740129). He received his medical degree from Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Luis E. Duarte has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/luis-duarte-211754#expertise). Dr. Luis E. Duarte accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/luis-duarte-211754#insurance). Dr. Luis E. Duarte is highly recommended by patients.
