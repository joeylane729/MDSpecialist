## Overview

Dr. Emad S. Farag is a neurologist in Riverside, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bakersfield Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/bakersfield-memorial-hospital-6930079) and [Emanate Health Queen of The Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/citrus-valley-medical-center-6930689). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Emad S. Farag has expertise in treating Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/emad-farag-447539#expertise). Dr. Emad S. Farag accepts Medicare, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/emad-farag-447539#insurance).
