## Overview

Dr. Achal Patel is a neurosurgeon in Edinburg, Texas. He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Achal Patel has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/achal-patel-1055438#expertise). Dr. Achal Patel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/achal-patel-1055438#insurance). Dr. Achal Patel is highly recommended by patients.
