## Overview

Dr. Tibor Boco is a neurosurgeon in Arlington Heights, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rush University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/rush-university-medical-center-6430985), [Endeavor Health Elmhurst Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/elmhurst-memorial-hospital-6431620) and [Elmhurst Outpatient Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/elmhurst-outpatient-surgery-center-A6129062). He received his medical degree from University of Chicago Division of the Biological Sciences The Pritzker School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Tibor Boco has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/tibor-boco-78007#expertise). Dr. Tibor Boco accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/tibor-boco-78007#insurance).
