## Overview

Dr. Gregory M. Bailey is an orthopedist in State College, Pennsylvania. He received his medical degree from Ohio University Heritage College of Osteopathic Medicine in Athens and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory M. Bailey has expertise in treating spinal fusion, bursitis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-bailey-862709#expertise). Dr. Gregory M. Bailey accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-bailey-862709#insurance).
