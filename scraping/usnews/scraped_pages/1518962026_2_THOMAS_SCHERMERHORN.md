## Overview

Dr. Thomas C. Schermerhorn is a neurosurgeon in Traverse City, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Munson Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/munson-medical-center-6440013) and [Aleda E. Lutz VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/aleda-e-lutz-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6442295). Schermerhorn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-schermerhorn-136494#expertise).
