## Overview

Dr. Andrea J. Chamczuk is a neurosurgeon in Youngstown, Ohio and is affiliated with [Mercy Health-St. Elizabeth Youngstown Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/st-elizabeth-health-center-6412440). She received her medical degree from Saba University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andrea J. Chamczuk has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrea-chamczuk-834808#expertise). Dr. Andrea J. Chamczuk accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrea-chamczuk-834808#insurance).
