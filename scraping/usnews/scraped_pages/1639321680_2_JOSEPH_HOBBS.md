## Overview

Dr. Joseph K. Hobbs is a neurosurgeon in San Angelo, Texas and is affiliated with [Shannon Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/shannon-medical-center-6740129). He received his medical degree from Ohio State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph K. Hobbs has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-hobbs-696369#expertise). Dr. Joseph K. Hobbs accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-hobbs-696369#insurance). Dr. Joseph K. Hobbs is highly recommended by patients.
