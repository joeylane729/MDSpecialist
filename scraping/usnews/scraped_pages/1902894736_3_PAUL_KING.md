## Overview

Dr. Paul D. King is a gastroenterologist in Saint Louis, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Desoto](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/baptist-memorial-hospital-desoto-6540950) and [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Memphis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-memphis-6520681). King has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-king-138457#expertise). Dr. Paul D.
