## Overview

Dr. Joseph Walker is a physiatrist in Farmington, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UConn John Dempsey Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/university-of-connecticut-health-center-john-dempsey-hospital-6160215) and [Hartford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/hartford-hospital-6160003). He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph Walker has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-walker-651075#expertise). Dr. Joseph Walker accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-walker-651075#insurance).
