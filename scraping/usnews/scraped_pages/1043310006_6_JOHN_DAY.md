## Overview

Dr. John Day is an orthopedist in Marietta, Georgia. He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Day has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-day-122502#expertise). Dr. John Day is highly recommended by patients.
