## Overview

Dr. Sun Lee is a neurosurgeon in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hollywood Presbyterian Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/hollywood-presbyterian-medical-center-6931670) and [Providence St. Joseph Medical Center-Burbank](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-st-joseph-medical-center-6930340). He received his medical degree from Seoul National University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sun Lee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sun-lee-964513#expertise). Dr. Sun Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sun-lee-964513#insurance).
