## Overview

Dr. Robert T. Elliott is an internist in San Diego, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Scripps Mercy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/scripps-mercy-hospital-6932810) and [Eisenhower Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/eisenhower-medical-center-6932324).
