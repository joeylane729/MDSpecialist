## Overview

Dr. Paul L. Oboynick is a neurosurgeon in Shawnee Mission, Kansas. He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul L. Oboynick has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-oboynick-7491#expertise). Dr. Paul L. Oboynick accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-oboynick-7491#insurance). Dr. Paul L. Oboynick is highly recommended by patients.
