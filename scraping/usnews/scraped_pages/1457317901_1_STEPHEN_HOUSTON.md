## Overview

Dr. Stephen C. Houston is a neurosurgeon in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Swedish Medical Center-Cherry Hill](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/swedish-medical-center-cherry-hill-6910680) and [UW Medicine-University of Washington Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/university-of-washington-medical-center-6910750). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stephen C. Houston has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-houston-27203#expertise). Dr. Stephen C. Houston accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-houston-27203#insurance). Dr. Stephen C. Houston is highly recommended by patients.
