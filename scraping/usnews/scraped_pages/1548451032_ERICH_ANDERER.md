## Overview

Dr. Erich G. Anderer is a neurosurgeon in Brooklyn, New York and is affiliated with [NYU Langone Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-langone-medical-center-6212925). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Erich G.
