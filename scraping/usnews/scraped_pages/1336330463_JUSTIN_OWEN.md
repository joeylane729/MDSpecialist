## Overview

Dr. Justin L. Owen is a neurosurgeon in Marrero, Louisiana. He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Justin L. Owen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-owen-63129#expertise). Dr. Justin L. Owen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/justin-owen-63129#insurance).
