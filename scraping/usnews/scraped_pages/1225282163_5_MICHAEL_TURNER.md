Turner is a dermatologist in Colorado Springs, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UCHealth Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/memorial-hospital-6840190) and [Common Spirit-Penrose Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/penrose-st-francis-health-services-6840002).
