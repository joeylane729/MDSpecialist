## Overview

Dr. Shamsuddin Khwaja is a neurologist in Killeen, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AdventHealth Central Texas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/metroplex-adventist-hospital-6742145) and [Seton Medical Center Harker Heights](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-harker-heights-6740668). He received his medical degree from Liaquat Medical College Sind and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Shamsuddin Khwaja has expertise in treating Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shamsuddin-khwaja-321561#expertise). Dr. Shamsuddin Khwaja accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shamsuddin-khwaja-321561#insurance).
