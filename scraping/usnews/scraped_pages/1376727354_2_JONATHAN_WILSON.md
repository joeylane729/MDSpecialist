## Overview

Dr. Jonathan Wilson is a neurosurgeon in Winston Salem, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/wake-forest-baptist-medical-center-6361700). He received his medical degree from West Virginia University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Jonathan Wilson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-wilson-1444578#expertise). Dr. Jonathan Wilson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-wilson-1444578#insurance).
