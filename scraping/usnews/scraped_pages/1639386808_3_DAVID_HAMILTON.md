## Overview

Hamilton is an orthopedist in Detroit, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rehabilitation Institute of Michigan at Detroit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/dmc-rehabilitation-institute-of-michigan-6440803) and [DMC Huron Valley-Sinai Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/huron-valley-sinai-hospital-6442200).
