## Overview

Dr. Ali Shirzadi is a neurosurgeon in Murfreesboro, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Ascension Saint Thomas Rutherford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/middle-tennessee-medical-center-6520920). He received his medical degree from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center at Lubbock and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ali Shirzadi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-shirzadi-2767743#expertise). Dr. Ali Shirzadi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-shirzadi-2767743#insurance). Dr. Ali Shirzadi is highly recommended by patients.
