## Overview

Dr. Keith B. Quattrocchi is a neurosurgeon in Greenbrae, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MarinHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marin-general-hospital-6933370) and [Kaweah Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaweah-delta-health-care-district-6933850). He received his medical degree from George Washington University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Keith B.
