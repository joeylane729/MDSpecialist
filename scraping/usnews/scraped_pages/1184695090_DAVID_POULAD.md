## Overview

Dr. David Poulad is a neurosurgeon in Union, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bayshore Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/bayshore-community-hospital-6220395) and [Clara Maass Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/clara-maass-medical-center-6220758).
