## Overview

Dr. Eric Peterson is a pulmonologist in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dupont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/dupont-hospital-6420033) and [Lutheran Hospital of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/lutheran-hospital-6420420). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Eric Peterson has expertise in treating pulmonary heart disease, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-90560#expertise). Dr. Eric Peterson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-90560#insurance). Dr. Eric Peterson is highly recommended by patients.
