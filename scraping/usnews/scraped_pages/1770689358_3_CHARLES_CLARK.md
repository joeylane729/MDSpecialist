## Overview

Dr. Charles Clark is an orthopedist in Nampa, Idaho and is affiliated with [St. Luke's Nampa Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/st-lukes-nampa-6820411). He received his medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years.
