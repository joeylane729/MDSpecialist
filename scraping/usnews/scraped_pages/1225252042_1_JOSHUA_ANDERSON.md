## Overview

Dr. Joshua R. Anderson is a neurosurgeon in Grand Island, Nebraska. He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joshua R. Anderson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-anderson-606297#expertise). Dr. Joshua R. Anderson accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joshua-anderson-606297#insurance). Dr. Joshua R. Anderson is highly recommended by patients.
