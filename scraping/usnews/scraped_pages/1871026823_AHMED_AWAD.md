## Overview

Dr. Ahmed M. Awad is a nephrologist in Kansas City, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Saint Luke's Hospital of Kansas City](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/st-lukes-hospital-6630470) and [University Health-Truman Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/truman-medical-center-hospital-hill-6630380). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ahmed M. Awad has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, kidney disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ahmed-awad-323274#expertise).
