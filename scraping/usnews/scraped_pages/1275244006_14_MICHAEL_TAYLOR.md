## Overview

Dr. Michael Taylor is an interventional radiology doctor in San Francisco, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Francis Memorial Hospital-San Francisco](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-francis-memorial-hospital-6933020) and [UCSF Health St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-marys-hospital-and-medical-center-6933050). He received his medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael Taylor accepts Medicare, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-taylor-38720#insurance).
