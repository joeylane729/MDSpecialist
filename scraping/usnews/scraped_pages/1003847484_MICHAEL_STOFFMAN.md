Stoffman is a neurosurgeon in Niagara Falls, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/niagara-falls-memorial-medical-center-6213670), [Kaleida Health-Buffalo General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/buffalo-general-medical-center-6211025) and [Atlas Surgery Center-Williamsville](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ny/atlas-surgery-center-williamsville-A6626122).
