## Overview

Dr. Jerry Noel is a neurosurgeon in Santa Monica, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arrowhead Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/arrowhead-regional-medical-center-6932770) and [Mercy General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-general-hospital-6932670). He received his medical degree from College of Osteopathic Medicine of the Pacific and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jerry Noel has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jerry-noel-857149#expertise). Dr. Jerry Noel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jerry-noel-857149#insurance). Dr. Jerry Noel is highly recommended by patients.
