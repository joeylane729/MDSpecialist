Ammar is a neurosurgeon in Garden City, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Health University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/memorial-university-medical-center-6381027), [Candler Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/st-josephs-candler-6381080) and [Neurological Institute Ambulatory Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ga/neurological-institute-ambulatory-surgical-center-A1924458).
