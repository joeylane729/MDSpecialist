## Overview

Dr. Anthony M. Martino is a neurosurgeon in Mobile, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mobile Infirmary Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/mobile-infirmary-medical-center-6530006) and [Springhill Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/springhill-medical-center-6530640).
