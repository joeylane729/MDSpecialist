## Overview

Dr. Richard Chung is an internist in Dedham, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [South Shore Hospital-South Weymouth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/south-shore-hospital-6141900). He received his medical degree from Stony Brook University Health Sciences Center School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Richard Chung has expertise in treating dementia, stroke, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-chung-806822#expertise).
