## Overview

Dr. Michael P. Verdon is a neurosurgeon in Dayton, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dayton Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/dayton-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6411220) and [Miami Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/miami-valley-hospital-6411190).
