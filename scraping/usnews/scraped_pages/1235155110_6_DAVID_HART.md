## Overview

Dr. David E. Hart is a neurologist in Albany, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Albany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/albany-medical-center-6210030) and [St. Peter's Hospital-Albany](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-peters-hospital-6210070).
