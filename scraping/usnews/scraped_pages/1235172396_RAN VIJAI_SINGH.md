## Overview

Dr. Ran Vijai P. Singh is a neurosurgeon in Norfolk, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hospital for Extended Recovery](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/hospital-for-extended-recovery-6340022) and [Sentara Norfolk General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sentara-norfolk-general-hospital-6340620). He received his medical degree from Moti Lal Nehru Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ran Vijai P. Singh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ran-vijai-singh-1042212#expertise). Dr. Ran Vijai P. Singh accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ran-vijai-singh-1042212#insurance). Dr. Ran Vijai P. Singh is highly recommended by patients.
