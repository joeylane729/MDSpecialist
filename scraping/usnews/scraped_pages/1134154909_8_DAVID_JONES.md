## Overview

Dr. David I. Jones is a pulmonologist in Tyler, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Mother Frances Hospital-Tyler](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/trinity-mother-frances-hospitals-and-clinics-6743570) and [UT Health Tyler](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/east-texas-medical-center-6740092).
