## Overview

Dr. Christopher D. Baggott is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [SSM Health St. Mary's Hospital-Madison](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/st-marys-hospital-6450810).
