## Overview

Dr. Mohammed A. Rahman is a neurosurgeon in Warsaw, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HSHS Good Shepherd Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/shelby-memorial-hospital-6432930) and [HSHS St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/st-marys-hospital-6431390). He received his medical degree from Rowan University School of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mohammed A. Rahman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mohammed-rahman-770816#expertise). Dr. Mohammed A. Rahman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mohammed-rahman-770816#insurance).
