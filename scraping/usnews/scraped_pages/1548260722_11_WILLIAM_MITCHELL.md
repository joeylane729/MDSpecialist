Dr. William A. Mitchell is an orthopedist in Wellesley, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [New England Baptist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/new-england-baptist-hospital-6140460) and [Newton-Wellesley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/newton-wellesley-hospital-6141530).
