## Overview

Dr. Robert A. Martin is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Princeton, New Jersey and is affiliated with [Penn Medicine Princeton Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/university-medical-center-of-princeton-at-plainsboro-6221190).
