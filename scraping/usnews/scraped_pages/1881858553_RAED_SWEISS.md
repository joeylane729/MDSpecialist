## Overview

Dr. Raed B. Sweiss is a neurosurgeon in Moreno Valley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Riverside University Health System-Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/riverside-county-regional-medical-center-6930110) and [Arrowhead Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/arrowhead-regional-medical-center-6932770). He received his medical degree from Edward Via Virginia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Raed B. Sweiss has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raed-sweiss-858524#expertise). Dr. Raed B. Sweiss accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raed-sweiss-858524#insurance).
