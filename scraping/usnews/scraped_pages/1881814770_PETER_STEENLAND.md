## Overview

Dr. Peter R. Steenland is a neurosurgeon in Asheville, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Charles George Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/charles-george-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6361110) and [Angel Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/angel-medical-center-6360550). He received his medical degree from University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Peter R. Steenland has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-steenland-779666#expertise). Dr. Peter R. Steenland accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-steenland-779666#insurance).
