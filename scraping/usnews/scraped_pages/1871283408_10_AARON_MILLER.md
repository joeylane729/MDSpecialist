## Overview

Dr. Aaron M. Miller is a neurologist in Geneva, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Northwestern Medicine Delnor Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/cadence-health-delnor-hospital-6431765) and [Advocate Sherman Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/sherman-hospital-6431610). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Aaron M. Miller has expertise in treating Alzheimer's dementia, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/aaron-miller-96632#expertise). Dr. Aaron M. Miller accepts Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/aaron-miller-96632#insurance).
