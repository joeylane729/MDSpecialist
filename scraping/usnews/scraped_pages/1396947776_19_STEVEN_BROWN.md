## Overview

Brown is an orthopedist in Cedar Rapids, Iowa and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Children's Hospital at Saint Francis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/childrens-hospital-at-saint-francis-PA6731215) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215).
