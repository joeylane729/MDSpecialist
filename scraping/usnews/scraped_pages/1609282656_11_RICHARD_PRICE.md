## Overview

Dr. Richard L. Price is a psychiatrist in Monsey, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [New York-Presbyterian Hospital-Columbia and Cornell](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/new-york-presbyterian-university-hospital-of-columbia-and-cornell-6210024) and [Montefiore Mount Vernon Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mount-vernon-hospital-6212530). He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard L. Price has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-price-276331#expertise). Dr. Richard L. Price accepts Medicare, Aetna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-price-276331#insurance). Dr. Richard L. Price is highly recommended by patients.
