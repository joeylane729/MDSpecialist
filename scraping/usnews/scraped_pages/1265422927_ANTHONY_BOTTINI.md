## Overview

Dr. Anthony G. Bottini is a neurosurgeon in Edina, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [M Health Fairview Southdale Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/fairview-southdale-hospital-6610918) and [M Health Fairview University of Minnesota Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/university-of-minnesota-medical-center-6610004).
