## Overview

Dr. Hitesh Patel is an oncologist in Clearwater, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Morton Plant Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/morton-plant-hospital-6390090) and [Largo Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/hca-medical-center-hospital-largo-6390489).
