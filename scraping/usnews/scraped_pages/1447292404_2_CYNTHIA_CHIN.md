## Overview

Dr. Cynthia M. Chin is an internist in Union City, California and is affiliated with [UCSF Health-UCSF Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ucsf-medical-center-6930043). She received her medical degree from Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Cynthia M. Chin has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, multiple sclerosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/cynthia-chin-108251#expertise). Dr. Cynthia M. Chin accepts Medicare, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/cynthia-chin-108251#insurance).
