## Overview

Dr. Tyler Atkins is a neurosurgeon in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with [Parkview Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/parkview-regional-medical-center-6429153). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Tyler Atkins has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/tyler-atkins-1241911#expertise). Dr. Tyler Atkins accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/tyler-atkins-1241911#insurance).
