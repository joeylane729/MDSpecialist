## Overview

Dr. Lee Hwang is a neurosurgeon in Fountain Hill, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [St. Luke's University Hospital-Bethlehem Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/st-lukes-hospital-and-health-network-6230049).
