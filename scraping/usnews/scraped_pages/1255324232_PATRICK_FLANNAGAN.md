## Overview

Dr. Patrick P. Flannagan is a neurosurgeon in Pickerington, Ohio and is affiliated with [OhioHealth Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/doctors-hospital-6410952). He received his medical degree from MCP-Hahnemann College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Patrick P. Flannagan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-flannagan-172254#expertise). Dr. Patrick P. Flannagan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-flannagan-172254#insurance).
