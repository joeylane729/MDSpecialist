## Overview

Dr. Peter K. Kim is a neurologist in Simi Valley, California. He received his medical degree from Meharry Medical College School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter K. Kim has expertise in treating Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-101454#expertise). Dr. Peter K. Kim accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-kim-101454#insurance).
