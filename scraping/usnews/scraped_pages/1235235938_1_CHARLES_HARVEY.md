## Overview

Dr. Charles J. Harvey is an orthopedist in Altoona, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [James E. Van Zandt Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/james-e-van-zandt-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6230075) and [UPMC Altoona](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/altoona-regional-hospital-6230086). He received his medical degree from Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles J. Harvey has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-harvey-861171#expertise). Dr. Charles J. Harvey accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-harvey-861171#insurance).
