## Overview

Dr. James J. Wang is a neurologist in Memphis, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Memorial Hospital for Women](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-for-women-6520022) and [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Memphis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-memphis-6520681). He received his medical degree from Weifang Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James J. Wang has expertise in treating Alzheimer's dementia, Parkinson's disease, sleep apnea, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-wang-288370#expertise). Dr. James J. Wang accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-wang-288370#insurance). Dr. James J. Wang is highly recommended by patients.
