## Overview

Dr. James A. Botros is a neurosurgeon in Albuquerque, New Mexico and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of New Mexico Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/university-of-new-mexico-childrens-hospital-PA6850037) and [University of New Mexico Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/university-hospital-6850037).
