## Overview

Dr. Michael Cohen is a neurosurgeon in Bangor, Maine and is affiliated with [Eastern Maine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/me/eastern-maine-medical-center-6110050). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Cohen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-873447#expertise). Dr. Michael Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-873447#insurance). Dr. Michael Cohen is highly recommended by patients.
