## Overview

Dr. Walter H. Hall is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Morganton, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UNC Health Blue Ridge](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/blue-ridge-healthcare-hospitals-6360427) and [Mission Hospital-McDowell](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/mcdowell-hospital-6360970). He received his medical degree from University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Walter H. Hall has expertise in treating hysterectomy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/walter-hall-311502#expertise). Dr. Walter H. Hall accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/walter-hall-311502#insurance). Dr. Walter H. Hall is highly recommended by patients.
