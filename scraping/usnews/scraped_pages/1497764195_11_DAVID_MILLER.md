## Overview

Dr. David Miller is a pulmonologist in Boston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mount Auburn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/mount-auburn-hospital-6140780) and [Spaulding Hospital for Continuing Medical Care Cambridge](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/spaulding-hospital-for-continuing-medical-care-cambridge-6140770). He received his medical degree from Harvard Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Miller has expertise in treating bronchitis, pulmonary heart disease, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-miller-1013814#expertise). Dr. David Miller accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-miller-1013814#insurance).
