## Overview

Dr. James J. Lee is a plastic surgeon in Santa Clarita, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Providence Holy Cross Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-holy-cross-medical-center-6932860) and [Providence Little Company of Mary Medical Center Torrance](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-little-company-of-mary-6933681). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James J. Lee has expertise in treating lymphedema, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-lee-418886#expertise). Dr. James J. Lee accepts Medicare, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-lee-418886#insurance). Dr. James J. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
