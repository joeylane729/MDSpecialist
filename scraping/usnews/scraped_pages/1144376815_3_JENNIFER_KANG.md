## Overview

Dr. Jennifer A. Kang is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Redding, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mercy Medical Center Redding](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-medical-center-redding-6932520), [Patients' Hospital of Redding](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/patients-hospital-of-redding-6930114) and [Mercy Surgery Center-Redding](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/mercy-surgery-center-redding-A1636838).
