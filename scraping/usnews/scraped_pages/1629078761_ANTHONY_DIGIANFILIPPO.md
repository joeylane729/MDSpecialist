## Overview

Dr. Anthony Digianfilippo is a neurosurgeon in Hinsdale, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UChicago Medicine AdventHealth Hinsdale](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/adventist-hinsdale-hospital-6431920) and [Advocate Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-good-samaritan-hospital-6431475). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Anthony Digianfilippo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/anthony-digianfilippo-18224#expertise). Dr. Anthony Digianfilippo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/anthony-digianfilippo-18224#insurance).
