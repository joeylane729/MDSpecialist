## Overview

Dr. John M. Lopez is an internist in Kenosha, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [Aurora Medical Center-Kenosha](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aurora-medical-center-6450019). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John M.
