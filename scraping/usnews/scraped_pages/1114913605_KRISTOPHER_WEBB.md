## Overview

Dr. Kristopher M. Webb is a neurosurgeon in Austin, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. David's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/st-davids-medical-center-6740190) and [Baylor Scott and White Institute for Rehabilitation-Lakeway](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/vibra-rehabilitation-hospital-lake-travis-6741008). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Kristopher M. Webb has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kristopher-webb-184454#expertise).
