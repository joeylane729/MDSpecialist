## Overview

Dr. Donald L. Hilton is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Methodist Hospital-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-hospital-6743122), [Methodist Hospital-Stone Oak](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-stone-oak-hospital-6740417), [Stone Oak Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/stone-oak-surgery-center-A6250131) and [Stone Oak Surgicenter](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/stone-oak-surgicenter-A2486414). Hilton has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/donald-hilton-29524#expertise).
