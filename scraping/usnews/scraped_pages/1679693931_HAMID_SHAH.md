## Overview

Dr. Hamid Shah is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Tennessee Valley Healthcare System-Nashville and Murfreesboro Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/tennessee-valley-healthcare-system-6520020) and [Vanderbilt University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/vanderbilt-university-medical-center-6521060).
