## Overview

Hunter is a gastroenterologist in Cedar Bluff, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Clinch Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/clinch-valley-medical-center-6340800) and [Russell County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/russell-county-medical-center-6340390).
