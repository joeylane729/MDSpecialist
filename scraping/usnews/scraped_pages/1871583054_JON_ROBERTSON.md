## Overview

Dr. Jon H. Robertson is a neurosurgeon in Florence, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [North Alabama Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/eliza-coffee-memorial-hospital-6530440) and [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Memphis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-memphis-6520681). He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jon H. Robertson has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jon-robertson-12469#expertise). Dr. Jon H. Robertson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jon-robertson-12469#insurance). Dr. Jon H. Robertson is highly recommended by patients.
