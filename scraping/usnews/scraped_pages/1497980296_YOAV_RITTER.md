## Overview

Dr. Yoav Ritter is a neurosurgeon in Plantation, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westside Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/westside-regional-medical-center-6390180) and [McLaren Oakland Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/poh-regional-medical-center-6442148). He received his medical degree from Nova Southeastern University - College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Yoav Ritter has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yoav-ritter-812801#expertise). Dr. Yoav Ritter accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/yoav-ritter-812801#insurance).
