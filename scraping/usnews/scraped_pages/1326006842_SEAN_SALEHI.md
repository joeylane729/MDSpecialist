## Overview

Dr. Sean A. Salehi is a neurosurgeon in Mokena, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Advocate Good Samaritan Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-good-samaritan-hospital-6431475), [Community First Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/presence-our-lady-of-the-resurrection-medical-center-6430964) and [Center for Minimally Invasive Surgery-Mokena](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/center-for-minimally-invasive-surgery-mokena-A7987712). He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sean A. Salehi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sean-salehi-337290#expertise). Dr. Sean A. Salehi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sean-salehi-337290#insurance).
