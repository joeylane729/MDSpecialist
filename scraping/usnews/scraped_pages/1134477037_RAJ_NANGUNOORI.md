## Overview

Dr. Raj K. Nangunoori is a neurosurgeon in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Seton Hays Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/seton-medical-center-hays-6740549) and [Mercy Hospital Northwest Arkansas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/mercy-medical-center-6710535). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Raj K. Nangunoori has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raj-nangunoori-895364#expertise).
