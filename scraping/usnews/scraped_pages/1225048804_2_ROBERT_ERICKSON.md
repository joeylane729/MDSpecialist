## Overview

Dr. Robert C. Erickson is an ophthalmologist in Birmingham, Michigan. He received his medical degree from University of Nebraska College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert C. Erickson has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-erickson-176628#expertise).
