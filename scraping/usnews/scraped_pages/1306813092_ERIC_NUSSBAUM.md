## Overview

Dr. Eric Nussbaum is an emergency medicine physician in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with [Virginia Mason Franciscan Health-St. Francis Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/st-francis-hospital-6918055). He received his medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Eric Nussbaum accepts Medicare, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-nussbaum-193409#insurance).
