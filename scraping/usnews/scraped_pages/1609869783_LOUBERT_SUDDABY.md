## Overview

Dr. Loubert S. Suddaby is a neurosurgeon in Hamburg, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Erie County Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/erie-county-medical-center-6211070) and [Mercy Hospital-Buffalo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mercy-hospital-6211100).
