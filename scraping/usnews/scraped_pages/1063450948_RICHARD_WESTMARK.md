## Overview

Dr. Richard M. Westmark is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890), [HCA Houston Healthcare Clear Lake](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/clear-lake-regional-medical-center-6740149) and [Memorial Hermann Surgery Center Preston Road](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/memorial-hermann-surgery-center-preston-road-A5584429).
