## Overview

Dr. Hiren Patel is a psychiatrist in Hershey, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn State Health Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-hershey-childrens-hospital-PA6230705) and [Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-milton-s-hershey-medical-center-6230705). He received his medical degree from B J Medical College Gujarat University. and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Hiren Patel has expertise in treating anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hiren-patel-3159809#expertise). Dr. Hiren Patel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hiren-patel-3159809#insurance).
