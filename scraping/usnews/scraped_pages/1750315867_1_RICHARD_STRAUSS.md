## Overview

Dr. Richard C. Strauss is a neurosurgeon in Vineland, New Jersey and is affiliated with [UPMC Western Maryland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/western-maryland-regional-medical-center-6320206) and [Surgcenter of Western Maryland](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/surgcenter-of-western-maryland-A2602552).
