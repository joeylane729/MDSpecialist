## Overview

Dr. Luis A. Gonzalez is an ophthalmologist in Teaneck, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Clara Maass Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/clara-maass-medical-center-6220758), [Hackensack University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/hackensack-university-medical-center-6220340) and [Bergen-Passaic Eye Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nj/bergen-passaic-eye-surgery-center-A3075210).
