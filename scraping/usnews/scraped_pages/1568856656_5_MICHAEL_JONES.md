## Overview

Dr. Michael D. Jones is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Mexico, Missouri and is affiliated with [Hannibal Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/hannibal-regional-hospital-6630230) and [Surgery Center of Columbia](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mo/surgery-center-of-columbia-A5948878). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael D. Jones has expertise in treating hysterectomy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-jones-464904#expertise). Dr. Michael D. Jones accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-jones-464904#insurance).
