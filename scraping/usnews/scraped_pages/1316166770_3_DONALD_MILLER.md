## Overview

Dr. Donald Miller is a pediatrician in San Marcos, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rady Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/rady-childrens-hospital-6932775) and [Tri-City Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/tri-city-medical-center-6932267).
