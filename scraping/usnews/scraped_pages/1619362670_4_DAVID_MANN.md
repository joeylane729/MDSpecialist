## Overview

Dr. David A. Mann is an ENT-otolaryngologist in Herrin, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Herrin Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/herrin-hospital-6431870) and [SIH Memorial Hospital of Carbondale](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/memorial-hospital-of-carbondale-6430327).
