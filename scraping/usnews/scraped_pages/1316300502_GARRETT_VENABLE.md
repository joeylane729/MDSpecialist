## Overview

Dr. Garrett T. Venable is a doctor in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baton Rouge General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-general-medical-center-6720042) and [Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/our-lady-of-lake-regional-medical-center-6720028). Venable accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/garrett-venable-1036637#insurance). Dr. Garrett T.
