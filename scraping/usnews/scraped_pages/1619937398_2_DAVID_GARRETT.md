## Overview

Dr. David Garrett is a neurosurgeon in Temple, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Temple](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/scott-and-white-healthcare-6743413) and [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Hillcrest](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hillcrest-baptist-medical-center-6743650). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Garrett has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-garrett-302704#expertise). Dr. David Garrett accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-garrett-302704#insurance).
