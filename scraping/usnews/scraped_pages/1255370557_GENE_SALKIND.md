## Overview

Dr. Gene Z. Salkind is a neurosurgeon in Huntingdon Valley, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Holy Redeemer Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/holy-redeemer-hospital-6231345). He received his medical degree from Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gene Z. Salkind has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gene-salkind-383885#expertise). Dr. Gene Z. Salkind accepts Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gene-salkind-383885#insurance). Dr. Gene Z. Salkind is highly recommended by patients.
