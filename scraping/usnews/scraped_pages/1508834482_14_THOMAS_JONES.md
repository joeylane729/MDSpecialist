## Overview

Dr. Thomas L. Jones is an orthopedist in Pearland, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Houston Physicians Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/houston-physicians-hospital-6740335), [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890) and [Memorial Hermann Surgery Center Main Street](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/memorial-hermann-surgery-center-main-street-A9069277). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas L. Jones has expertise in treating spinal fusion, knee injury, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-jones-652231#expertise). Dr. Thomas L. Jones accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-jones-652231#insurance).
