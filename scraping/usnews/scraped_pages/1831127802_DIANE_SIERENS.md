## Overview

Dr. Diane Sierens is a neurosurgeon in Rockford, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Endeavor Health Edward Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/edward-hospital-6432350) and [John H. Stroger Jr. Hospital of Cook County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/john-h-stroger-jr-hospital-of-cook-county-6430600). She received her medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Diane Sierens has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/diane-sierens-421589#expertise). Dr. Diane Sierens accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/diane-sierens-421589#insurance). Dr. Diane Sierens is highly recommended by patients.
