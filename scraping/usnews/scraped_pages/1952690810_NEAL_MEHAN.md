## Overview

Dr. Neal Mehan is a neurosurgeon in Dayton, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Miami Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/miami-valley-hospital-6411190) and [Mercy Health-Springfield Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/springfield-regional-medical-center-6412050). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Neal Mehan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neal-mehan-829932#expertise). Dr. Neal Mehan accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neal-mehan-829932#insurance). Dr. Neal Mehan is highly recommended by patients.
