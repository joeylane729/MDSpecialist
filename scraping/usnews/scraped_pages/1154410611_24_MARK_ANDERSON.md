## Overview

Dr. Mark R. Anderson is a cardiologist in Salem, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Salem Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/north-shore-medical-center-6140002) and [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/massachusetts-general-hospital-6140430).
