## Overview

Dr. Robert W. Nudelman is a neurosurgeon in Greensboro, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Moses H. Cone Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/moses-h-cone-memorial-hospital-6360002) and [Greensboro Specialty Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/greensboro-specialty-surgery-center-A8413483). Nudelman accepts Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-nudelman-207731#insurance). Dr. Robert W. Nudelman is highly recommended by patients.
