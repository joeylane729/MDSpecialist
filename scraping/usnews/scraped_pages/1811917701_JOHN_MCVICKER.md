## Overview

Dr. John H. McVicker is a neurosurgeon in Colorado Springs, Colorado and is affiliated with [UCHealth Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/memorial-hospital-6840190). He received his medical degree from University of Colorado School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John H.
