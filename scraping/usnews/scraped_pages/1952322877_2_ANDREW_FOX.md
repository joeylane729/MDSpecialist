## Overview

Dr. Andrew Fox is a neurosurgeon in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with [Providence Cedars-Sinai Tarzana Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-tarzana-medical-center-6933670), [Calabasas Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/calabasas-surgery-center-A8496169) and [Premier Specialty Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/premier-specialty-surgical-center-A8636141). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andrew Fox has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-fox-467836#expertise).
