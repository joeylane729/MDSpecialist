## Overview

Dr. David G. Hall is a geriatrician in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [UPMC Magee-Womens Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/magee-womens-hospital-of-upmc-6232460).
