## Overview

Dr. Richard G. Johnson is a pulmonologist in Glendora, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Foothill Presbyterian Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/foothill-presbyterian-hospital-6930859) and [San Dimas Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/san-dimas-community-hospital-6932850). He received his medical degree from University of Louisville School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard G. Johnson has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, anxiety disorders, asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-johnson-237913#expertise). Dr. Richard G. Johnson accepts Medicare, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-johnson-237913#insurance).
