## Overview

Dr. George A. Alsina is a neurosurgeon in Bowling Green, Kentucky and is affiliated with [Novant Health New Hanover Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/new-hanover-regional-medical-center-6360019) and [Wilmington Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/wilmington-surgery-center-A6223367).
