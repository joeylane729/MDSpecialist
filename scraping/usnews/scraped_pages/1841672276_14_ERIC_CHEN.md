## Overview

Dr. Eric Chen is a neonatologist in Longview, Texas and is affiliated with [Willis-Knighton Medical Center DBA Willis Knighton Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/willis-knighton-medical-center-6720877).
