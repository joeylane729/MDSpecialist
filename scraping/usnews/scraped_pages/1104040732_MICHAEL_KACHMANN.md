## Overview

Dr. Michael C. Kachmann is a neurosurgeon in Cincinnati, Ohio. He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael C. Kachmann has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kachmann-606531#expertise). Dr. Michael C. Kachmann accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-kachmann-606531#insurance). Dr. Michael C. Kachmann is highly recommended by patients.
