## Overview

Dr. James W. Stone is an orthopedist in Milwaukee, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Francis Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/wheaton-franciscan-healthcare-st-francis-6451145), [Aurora St. Luke's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aurora-st-lukes-medical-center-6450017) and [Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wi/surgery-center-A8651080). He received his medical degree from Harvard Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James W. Stone has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-stone-535587#expertise). Dr. James W. Stone accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-stone-535587#insurance).
