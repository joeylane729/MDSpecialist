Wogu is a neurosurgeon in San Luis Obispo, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sierra Vista Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sierra-vista-regional-medical-center-6933295), [Twin Cities Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/twin-cities-community-hospital-6933687) and [Posada Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/posada-ambulatory-surgery-center-A3265283).
