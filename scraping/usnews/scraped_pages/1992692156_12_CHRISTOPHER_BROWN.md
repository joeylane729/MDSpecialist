## Overview

Dr. Christopher Brown is an orthopedist in Plantation, Florida. He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher Brown has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-brown-2426228#expertise). Dr. Christopher Brown accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-brown-2426228#insurance).
