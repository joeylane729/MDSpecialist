## Overview

Dr. Alan P. Cohen is a dermatologist in Kearny, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cooperman Barnabas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/st-barnabas-medical-center-6220800) and [Veterans Affairs New Jersey Health Care System-East Orange](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/veterans-affairs-new-jersey-health-care-system-6220006). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Alan P. Cohen has expertise in treating shingles, psoriasis, acne, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-cohen-118680#expertise). Dr. Alan P. Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/alan-cohen-118680#insurance).
