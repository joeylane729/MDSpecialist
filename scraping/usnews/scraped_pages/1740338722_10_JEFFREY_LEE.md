## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey Lee is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Glendora, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emanate Health Queen of The Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/citrus-valley-medical-center-6930689) and [Pomona Valley Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/pomona-valley-hospital-medical-center-6932450). He received his medical degree from National Taiwan University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey Lee accepts United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-lee-504848#insurance).
