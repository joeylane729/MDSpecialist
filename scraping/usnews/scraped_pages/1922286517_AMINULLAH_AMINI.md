## Overview

Dr. Aminullah Amini is a neurosurgeon in Silver Spring, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Maryland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/university-of-maryland-medical-center-6320330), [Adventist Healthcare Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/adventist-rehabilitation-hospital-of-maryland-6320013) and [Surgcenter of Silver Spring](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/surgcenter-of-silver-spring-A1094217).
