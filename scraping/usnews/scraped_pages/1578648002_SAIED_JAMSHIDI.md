## Overview

Dr. Saied Jamshidi is a neurosurgeon in Oxon Hill, Maryland and is affiliated with [Adventist Healthcare Fort Washington Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/fort-washington-medical-center-6320008), [NSPC Surgery Center of Maryland](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/nspc-surgery-center-of-maryland-A3522311) and [White Flint Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/white-flint-surgery-A7218166). He received his medical degree from Iran University of Medical Sciences and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Saied Jamshidi has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/saied-jamshidi-1376555#expertise). Dr. Saied Jamshidi accepts Medicare, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/saied-jamshidi-1376555#insurance).
