## Overview

Dr. Luke A. Corsten is a neurosurgeon in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baton Rouge General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-general-medical-center-6720042) and [Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/our-lady-of-lake-regional-medical-center-6720028). He received his medical degree from Medical College of Wisconsin and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Luke A. Corsten has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/luke-corsten-805689#expertise). Dr. Luke A. Corsten accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/luke-corsten-805689#insurance).
