## Overview

Dr. Benjamin Rosenbaum is a neurosurgeon in Bozeman, Montana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health St. Vincent Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mt/st-vincent-healthcare-6810030), [Alaska Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ak/alaska-regional-hospital-6940015) and [Creekside Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ak/creekside-surgical-center-A4069456). He received his medical degree from Vanderbilt University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Benjamin Rosenbaum has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/benjamin-rosenbaum-667061#expertise). Dr. Benjamin Rosenbaum accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/benjamin-rosenbaum-667061#insurance).
