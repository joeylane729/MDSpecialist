## Overview

Dr. Timothy E. Lindley is a neurosurgeon in Alexandria, Minnesota. He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy E. Lindley has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-lindley-622508#expertise). Dr. Timothy E. Lindley accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-lindley-622508#insurance). Dr. Timothy E. Lindley is highly recommended by patients.
