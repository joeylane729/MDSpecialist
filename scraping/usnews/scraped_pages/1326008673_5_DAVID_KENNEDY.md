## Overview

Dr. David R. Kennedy is an internal medicine/pediatric specialist in Merriam, Kansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Advent Health Shawnee Mission](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/shawnee-mission-medical-center-6670783) and [Liberty Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/liberty-hospital-6630605).
