## Overview

Dr. Anthony Lee is a neurologist in Kalamazoo, Michigan and is affiliated with [Beacon Kalamazoo Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/borgess-medical-center-6440017). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Anthony Lee has expertise in treating dementia, multiple sclerosis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/anthony-lee-989036#expertise). Dr. Anthony Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/anthony-lee-989036#insurance). Dr. Anthony Lee is highly recommended by patients.
