## Overview

Dr. John T. Williams is an orthopedist in Lady Lake, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UF Health Leesburg Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/leesburg-regional-medical-center-6390004), [UF Health The Villages Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/the-villages-health-system-6390102) and [TLC Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/tlc-surgery-center-A2502133). Williams has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, bursitis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-williams-304123#expertise). Dr. John T. Williams accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-williams-304123#insurance).
