## Overview

Dr. Robert Lieberson is a neurosurgeon in Stanford, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MarinHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marin-general-hospital-6933370) and [Mercy General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-general-hospital-6932670).
