## Overview

Dr. Nicholas D. Coppa is a neurosurgeon in Hyannis, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Cape Cod Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/cape-cod-hospital-6141130). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Nicholas D. Coppa has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicholas-coppa-88598#expertise).
