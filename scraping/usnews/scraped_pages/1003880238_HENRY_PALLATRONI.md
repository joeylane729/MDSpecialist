## Overview

Dr. Henry F. Pallatroni is a neurosurgeon in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. He received his medical degree from Tufts University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Henry F. Pallatroni has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/henry-pallatroni-1468#expertise).
