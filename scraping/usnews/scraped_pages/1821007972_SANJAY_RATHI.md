## Overview

Dr. Sanjay P. Rathi is a neurologist in North Haven, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Griffin Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/griffin-hospital-6160120) and [Yale New Haven Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/yale-new-haven-hospital-6160400). He received his medical degree from M.S. Ramaiah Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sanjay P. Rathi has expertise in treating Alzheimer's dementia, Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-rathi-772695#expertise). Dr. Sanjay P. Rathi accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-rathi-772695#insurance).
