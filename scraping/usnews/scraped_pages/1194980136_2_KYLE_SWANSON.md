## Overview

Dr. Kyle E. Swanson is an orthopedist in Carson City, Nevada and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Barton Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/barton-memorial-hospital-6933659) and [Carson Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/carson-valley-medical-center-6880027).
