## Overview

Dr. Patrick Maloney is an allergist-immunologist in Mamaroneck, New York and is affiliated with [White Plains Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/white-plains-hospital-6215330). He received his medical degree from Stony Brook University Health Sciences Center School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Patrick Maloney has expertise in treating asthma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-maloney-27801#expertise). Dr. Patrick Maloney accepts Medicare, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-maloney-27801#insurance). Dr. Patrick Maloney is highly recommended by patients.
