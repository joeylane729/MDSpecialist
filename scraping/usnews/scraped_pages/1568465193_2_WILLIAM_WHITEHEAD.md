## Overview

Dr. William F. Whitehead is a vascular surgeon in San Angelo, Texas and is affiliated with [Shannon Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/shannon-medical-center-6740129). He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William F. Whitehead has expertise in treating colon removal, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-whitehead-212544#expertise). Dr. William F. Whitehead accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-whitehead-212544#insurance).
