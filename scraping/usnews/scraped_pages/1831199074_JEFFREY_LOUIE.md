## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey P. Louie is a pediatric emergency medicine physician in Minneapolis, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [M Health Fairview Masonic Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/university-of-minnesota-childrens-hospital-PA6610004) and [M Health Fairview University of Minnesota Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/university-of-minnesota-medical-center-6610004).
