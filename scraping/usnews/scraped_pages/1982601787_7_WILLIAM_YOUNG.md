## Overview

Dr. William F. Young is a neurosurgeon in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dupont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/dupont-hospital-6420033) and [Lutheran Hospital of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/lutheran-hospital-6420420). He received his medical degree from Weill Cornell Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William F. Young has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-young-144954#expertise). Dr. William F. Young accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-young-144954#insurance).
