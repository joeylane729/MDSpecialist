## Overview

Dr. Nima Alan is a neurosurgeon in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [UCSF Health-UCSF Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ucsf-medical-center-6930043).
