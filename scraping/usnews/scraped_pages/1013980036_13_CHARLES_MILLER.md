## Overview

Dr. Charles Miller is a neurosurgeon in Richmond, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bon Secours St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/bon-secours-st-marys-hospital-6340921) and [Chippenham and Johnston-Willis Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/johnston-willis-hospital-6340009). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles Miller has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-miller-899103#expertise). Dr. Charles Miller accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-miller-899103#insurance).
