## Overview

Dr. Michael Feldman is an internist in West Bloomfield, Michigan and is affiliated with [Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/beaumont-hospital-6442245). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Feldman has expertise in treating obesity, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-feldman-871142#expertise). Dr. Michael Feldman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-feldman-871142#insurance). Dr. Michael Feldman is highly recommended by patients.
