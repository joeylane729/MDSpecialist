## Overview

Dr. Carl G. Bevering is a neurosurgeon in Tupelo, Mississippi and is affiliated with [North Mississippi Medical Center-Tupelo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/north-mississippi-medical-center-6540810). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Carl G. Bevering has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carl-bevering-263408#expertise). Dr. Carl G. Bevering accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carl-bevering-263408#insurance).
