## Overview

Dr. Zachary L. Tataryn is a neurosurgeon in Brighton, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Boston Medical Center-Brighton](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/st-elizabeths-medical-center-6140620). He received his medical degree from Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Zachary L. Tataryn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/zachary-tataryn-831412#expertise). Dr. Zachary L. Tataryn accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/zachary-tataryn-831412#insurance). Dr. Zachary L. Tataryn is highly recommended by patients.
