## Overview

Dr. Beejal Amin is a neurosurgeon in Oak Lawn, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Advocate Christ Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-christ-medical-center-6432413) and [UChicago Medicine AdventHealth La Grange](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/adventist-la-grange-memorial-hospital-6432055). He received his medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Beejal Amin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/beejal-amin-2416431#expertise). Dr. Beejal Amin accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/beejal-amin-2416431#insurance). Dr. Beejal Amin is highly recommended by patients.
