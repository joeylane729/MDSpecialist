## Overview

Dr. Norbert Roosen is a neurosurgeon in Detroit, Michigan. He received his medical degree from University of Antwerp Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Norbert Roosen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/norbert-roosen-569353#expertise). Dr. Norbert Roosen accepts Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/norbert-roosen-569353#insurance).
