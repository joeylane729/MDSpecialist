## Overview

Dr. Patrick C. Murray is an orthopedist in Honolulu, Hawaii and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kapiolani Medical Center for Women and Children](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/kapiolani-medical-center-for-women-6950335) and [Queen's Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/hi/queens-medical-center-6950380). He received his medical degree from St. George's University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Patrick C. Murray has expertise in treating spinal fusion, knee replacement, arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-murray-115219#expertise). Dr. Patrick C. Murray accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-murray-115219#insurance). Dr. Patrick C. Murray is highly recommended by patients.
