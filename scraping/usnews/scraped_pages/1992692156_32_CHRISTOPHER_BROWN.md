## Overview

Dr. Christopher R. Brown is an orthopedist in Raleigh, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Duke Raleigh Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/duke-raleigh-hospital-6361180). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher R. Brown has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-brown-2562231#expertise). Dr. Christopher R. Brown accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-brown-2562231#insurance).
