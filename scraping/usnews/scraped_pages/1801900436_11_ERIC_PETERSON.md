## Overview

Dr. Eric M. Peterson is a family medicine doctor in Boone, Iowa and is affiliated with [Boone County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/boone-county-hospital-6620100). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Eric M. Peterson has expertise in treating obesity, coronary artery disease, sleep apnea, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-292113#expertise). Dr. Eric M. Peterson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-peterson-292113#insurance). Dr. Eric M. Peterson is highly recommended by patients.

### Family Medicine

Family medicine doctors, also known as primary care physicians or PCPs, are trained to meet the basic health needs of children and adults. They often refer patients to specialists or for further testing, and they treat common conditions such as diabetes, high cholesterol and allergies. They also perform physicals, give vaccines and may do some in-office lab testing.
