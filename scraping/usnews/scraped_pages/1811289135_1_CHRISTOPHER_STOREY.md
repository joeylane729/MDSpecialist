## Overview

Dr. Christopher Storey is an internist in Penfield, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Rhode Island Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/rhode-island-hospital-6150150) and [South County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/south-county-hospital-6150200). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Christopher Storey has expertise in treating heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-storey-1061127#expertise). Dr. Christopher Storey accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-storey-1061127#insurance).
