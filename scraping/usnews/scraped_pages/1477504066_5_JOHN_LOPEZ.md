## Overview

Dr. John P. Lopez is an internal medicine/pediatric specialist in Perth Amboy, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Monmouth Medical Center-Long Branch Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/monmouth-medical-center-6220560) and [The Unterberg Children's Hospital at Monmouth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/childrens-hospital-at-monmouth-medical-center-PA6220560). He received his medical degree from Albany Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John P. Lopez has expertise in treating bronchitis, hypertension & high blood pressure, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lopez-547221#expertise). Dr. John P. Lopez accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lopez-547221#insurance). Dr. John P. Lopez is highly recommended by patients.
