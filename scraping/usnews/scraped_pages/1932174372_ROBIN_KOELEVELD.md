## Overview

Dr. Robin F. Koeleveld is a neurosurgeon in Raleigh, North Carolina. He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robin F. Koeleveld has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robin-koeleveld-276037#expertise). Dr. Robin F.
