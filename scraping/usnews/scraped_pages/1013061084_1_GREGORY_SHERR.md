## Overview

Dr. Gregory Sherr is a neurosurgeon in Gainesville, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA Florida North Florida Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/north-florida-regional-medical-center-6390225) and [Presbyterian Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/presbyterian-hospital-6850055). He received his medical degree from New York Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory Sherr has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-sherr-3832400#expertise). Dr. Gregory Sherr accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-sherr-3832400#insurance).
