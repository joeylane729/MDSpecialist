## Overview

Dr. Young Lee is a gastroenterologist in East Syracuse, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Crouse Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/crouse-hospital-6214890), [Guthrie Cortland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/cortland-regional-medical-center-6211370) and [Syracuse Endoscopy Associates](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ny/syracuse-endoscopy-associates-A8111100). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Young Lee has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/young-lee-81630#expertise).
