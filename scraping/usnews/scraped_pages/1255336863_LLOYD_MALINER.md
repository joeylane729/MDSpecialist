## Overview

Dr. Lloyd I. Maliner is a neurosurgeon in Plantation, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westside Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/westside-regional-medical-center-6390180) and [Broward Health Coral Springs](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/broward-health-coral-springs-medical-center-6390150).
