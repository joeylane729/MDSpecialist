## Overview

Dr. Nicholas P. Slimack is a neurosurgeon in Santa Maria, California and is affiliated with [Marian Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marian-regional-medical-center-6933440). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Nicholas P. Slimack has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicholas-slimack-735859#expertise). Dr. Nicholas P. Slimack accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nicholas-slimack-735859#insurance). Dr. Nicholas P. Slimack is highly recommended by patients.
