## Overview

Dr. Kenneth D. Lee is an internist in South San Francisco, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kaiser Permanente South San Francisco Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/south-san-francisco-medical-center-6933580) and [UCSF Health-UCSF Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ucsf-medical-center-6930043).
