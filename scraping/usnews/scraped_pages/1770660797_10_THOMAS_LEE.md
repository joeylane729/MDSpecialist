## Overview

Dr. Thomas G. Lee is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Peoria, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carle Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/methodist-medical-center-of-illinois-6432570) and [OSF Healthcare St. Francis Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/osf-st-francis-medical-center-6432620). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas G. Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-lee-464325#insurance). Dr. Thomas G. Lee is highly recommended by patients.
