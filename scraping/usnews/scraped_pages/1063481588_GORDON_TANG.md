## Overview

Dr. Gordon Tang is a neurosurgeon in Berkeley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Alta Bates Summit Medical Center-Berkeley](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alta-bates-summit-medical-center-6930290), [Alta Bates Summit Medical Center-Oakland](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alta-bates-summit-medical-center-6930031) and [The Surgery Center of Alta Bates Summit Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/the-surgery-center-of-alta-bates-summit-medical-center-A1568389). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gordon Tang has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gordon-tang-2735#expertise). Dr. Gordon Tang accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gordon-tang-2735#insurance).
