## Overview

Dr. David Forshaw is a neurosurgeon in Waterbury, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Mary's Hospital-Waterbury](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/st-marys-hospital-6160740) and [Waterbury Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/waterbury-hospital-6160750). He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Forshaw has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-forshaw-635067#expertise). Dr. David Forshaw accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-forshaw-635067#insurance).
