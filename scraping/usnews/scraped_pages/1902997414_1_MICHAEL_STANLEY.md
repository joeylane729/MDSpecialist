## Overview

Dr. Michael S. Stanley is a psychiatrist in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [OHSU Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/oregon-health-and-science-university-6920570) and [Columbia Memorial Hospital-Astoria](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/columbia-memorial-hospital-6920015).
