## Overview

Dr. Gilbert Cadena is a neurosurgeon in Saint Cloud, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CentraCare-St. Cloud Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/st-cloud-hospital-6611540) and [Providence St. Joseph Hospital-Orange](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-joseph-hospital-6932300). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Gilbert Cadena has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gilbert-cadena-672949#expertise). Dr. Gilbert Cadena accepts Medicare, Aetna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gilbert-cadena-672949#insurance). Dr. Gilbert Cadena is highly recommended by patients.
