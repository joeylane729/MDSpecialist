## Overview

Dr. Herbert L. Cares is a neurosurgeon in Newton, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/massachusetts-general-hospital-6140430) and [Milford Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/milford-regional-medical-center-6141395). He received his medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Herbert L. Cares has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herbert-cares-379923#expertise). Dr. Herbert L. Cares accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herbert-cares-379923#insurance).
