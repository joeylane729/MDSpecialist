## Overview

Dr. Saravanan Karuppiah is a neurosurgeon in Ocala, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AdventHealth Ocala](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/munroe-regional-medical-center-6390670) and [HCA Florida Ocala Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/ocala-regional-medical-center-6390680).
