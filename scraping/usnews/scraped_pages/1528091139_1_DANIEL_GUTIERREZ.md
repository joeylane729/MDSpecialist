Dr. Daniel Gutierrez is a dermatologist in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [NYC Health and Hospitals-Bellevue](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/bellevue-hospital-center-6212630) and [NYU Langone Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyu-langone-medical-center-6212925).
