## Overview

Dr. Andrew Cutler is an internist in Watertown, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Mount Auburn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/mount-auburn-hospital-6140780). He received his medical degree from Chicago Medical School/Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine Science and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andrew Cutler has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-cutler-156530#expertise). Dr. Andrew Cutler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-cutler-156530#insurance).
