## Overview

Dr. Victor C. Udekwu is a neurosurgeon in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Wilkes-Barre General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/wilkes-barre-general-hospital-6230026). He received his medical degree from University of Nigeria Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Victor C. Udekwu has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-udekwu-1109195#expertise). Dr. Victor C. Udekwu accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-udekwu-1109195#insurance).
