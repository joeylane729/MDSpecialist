## Overview

Dr. Daniel Laich is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Endeavor Health Swedish Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/swedish-covenant-hospital-6431180) and [Loyola University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/loyola-university-medical-center-6430841). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
