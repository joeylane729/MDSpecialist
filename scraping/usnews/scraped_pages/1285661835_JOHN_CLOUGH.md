## Overview

Dr. John A. Clough is a neurosurgeon in Overland Park, Kansas. He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John A. Clough has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-clough-418047#expertise). Dr. John A. Clough accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-clough-418047#insurance).
