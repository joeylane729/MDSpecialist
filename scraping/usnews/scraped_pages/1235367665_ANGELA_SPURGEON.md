## Overview

Dr. Angela Spurgeon is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Missouri and is affiliated with [CoxHealth Springfield](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/cox-health-hospital-6631320). She received her medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Angela Spurgeon has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/angela-spurgeon-813699#expertise). Dr. Angela Spurgeon accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/angela-spurgeon-813699#insurance). Dr. Angela Spurgeon is highly recommended by patients.
