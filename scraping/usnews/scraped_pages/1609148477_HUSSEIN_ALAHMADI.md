## Overview

Dr. Hussein I. Alahmadi is a neurosurgeon in New Britain, Connecticut and is affiliated with [Hospital of Central Connecticut](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/hospital-of-central-connecticut-6160013). He received his medical degree from King Saud University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Hussein I. Alahmadi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hussein-alahmadi-2425247#expertise). Dr. Hussein I. Alahmadi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/hussein-alahmadi-2425247#insurance). Dr. Hussein I. Alahmadi is highly recommended by patients.
