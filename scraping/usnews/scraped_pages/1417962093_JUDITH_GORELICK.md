## Overview

Dr. Judith L. Gorelick is a neurosurgeon in Fairfield, Connecticut and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bridgeport Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/bridgeport-hospital-6160002) and [Griffin Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/griffin-hospital-6160120). She received her medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Judith L. Gorelick has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/judith-gorelick-2948312#expertise). Dr. Judith L. Gorelick accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/judith-gorelick-2948312#insurance).
