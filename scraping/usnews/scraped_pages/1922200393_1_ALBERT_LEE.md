## Overview

Dr. Albert K. Lee is an internist in Bethesda, Maryland and is affiliated with [Johns Hopkins Medicine-Suburban Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/suburban-hospital-6320350). He received his medical degree from St. George's University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Albert K. Lee has expertise in treating bronchitis, heart failure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/albert-lee-289823#expertise).
