## Overview

Dr. David E. Adler is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Legacy Emanuel Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-emanuel-hospital-and-health-center-6920003), [Legacy Good Samaritan Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-good-samaritan-hospital-and-health-center-6920418) and [Wilshire Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/wilshire-surgery-center-A1775951).
