## Overview

Dr. Paul D. Kim is an ENT-otolaryngologist in Loma Linda, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Loma Linda University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/loma-linda-university-medical-center-6930053) and [Kaiser Permanente Fontana and Ontario Medical Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-fontana-medical-center-6930737).
