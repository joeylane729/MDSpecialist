## Overview

Dr. John Lee is an orthopedist in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with [Kaiser Permanente South Bay Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaiser-permanente-south-bay-medical-center-6930925). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Lee has expertise in treating knee replacement, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1487391#expertise).
