## Overview

Dr. Mahmood Moradi is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/childrens-memorial-hermann-hospital-PA6741890) and [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890). He received his medical degree from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center at Lubbock and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mahmood Moradi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mahmood-moradi-1499640#expertise). Dr. Mahmood Moradi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mahmood-moradi-1499640#insurance). Dr. Mahmood Moradi is highly recommended by patients.
