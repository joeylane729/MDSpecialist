## Overview

Dr. Nathan Pratt is a neurosurgeon in Galveston, Texas and is affiliated with [University of Texas Medical Branch](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/university-of-texas-medical-branch-hospitals-6741615). He received his medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Nathan Pratt has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/nathan-pratt-1041077#expertise).
