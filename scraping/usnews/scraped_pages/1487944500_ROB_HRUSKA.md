## Overview

Dr. Rob Hruska is a neurosurgeon in Bedford, Texas and is affiliated with [Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital Fort Worth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-harris-methodist-hospital-6741440). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Rob Hruska has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rob-hruska-3260969#expertise). Dr. Rob Hruska accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rob-hruska-3260969#insurance). Dr. Rob Hruska is highly recommended by patients.
