## Overview

Dr. Michael E. Walsh is a psychiatrist in New Orleans, Louisiana and is affiliated with [University Medical Center-New Orleans](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/medical-center-of-louisiana-6720027). He received his medical degree from Louisiana State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael E. Walsh has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-walsh-416051#expertise). Dr. Michael E. Walsh accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-walsh-416051#insurance). Dr. Michael E. Walsh is highly recommended by patients.
