## Overview

Dr. Robert Miller is an internist in Daytona Beach, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Halifax Health Medical Center of Daytona Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/halifax-health-6390056) and [AdventHealth Palm Coast](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/florida-hospital-flagler-6390060). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Robert Miller has expertise in treating stroke, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-miller-978638#expertise).
