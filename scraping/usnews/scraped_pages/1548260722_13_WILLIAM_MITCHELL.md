## Overview

Dr. William N. Mitchell is an ophthalmologist in Huntsville, Alabama. He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William N. Mitchell has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-mitchell-343976#expertise).
