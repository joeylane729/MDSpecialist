## Overview

Dr. Robert E. Kim is an interventional radiology doctor in Cartersville, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Piedmont Cartersville Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/cartersville-medical-center-6380395) and [AdventHealth Gordon](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/gordon-hospital-6380365).
