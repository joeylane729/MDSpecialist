## Overview

Dr. Mudit Sharma is a neurosurgeon in Manassas, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UVA Health System Prince William Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/novant-health-prince-william-medical-center-6340454), [Inova Fair Oaks Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/inova-fair-oaks-hospital-6340231), [Haymarket Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/va/haymarket-surgery-center-A9156426) and [NSPC Surgery Center of Maryland](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/md/nspc-surgery-center-of-maryland-A3522311).
