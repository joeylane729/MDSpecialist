## Overview

Dr. John C. Olson is an ophthalmologist in Orlando, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Orlando Health-Orlando Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/orlando-regional-medical-center-6390695) and [AdventHealth Orlando](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/florida-hospital-6390690). Olson accepts Humana, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-olson-422255#insurance). Dr. John C. Olson is highly recommended by patients.
