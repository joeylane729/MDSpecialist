## Overview

Dr. Daniel Carr is a pediatric emergency medicine physician in Washington, District of Columbia and is affiliated with [Children's National Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/dc/childrens-national-medical-center-6330030). He received his medical degree from Trinity School of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Daniel Carr accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-carr-3021191#insurance).
