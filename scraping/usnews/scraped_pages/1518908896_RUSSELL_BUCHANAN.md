## Overview

Dr. Russell I. Buchanan is a neurosurgeon in Waterloo, Iowa and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MercyOne Waterloo Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/covenant-medical-center-6621375), [UnityPoint Health-Waterloo](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/allen-memorial-hospital-6621360) and [United Medical Park ASC](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ia/united-medical-park-asc-A3795645). He received his medical degree from University of California, Davis, School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Russell I. Buchanan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/russell-buchanan-392496#expertise). Dr. Russell I. Buchanan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/russell-buchanan-392496#insurance).
