## Overview

Dr. Mohamed H. Elnabtity is a neurosurgeon in East Lansing, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [McLaren Greater Lansing Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/mclaren-greater-lansing-hospital-6440009), [University of Michigan Health-Sparrow](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/sparrow-hospital-6441595) and [Red Cedar Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/red-cedar-surgery-center-A1684865). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mohamed H. Elnabtity has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mohamed-elnabtity-360858#expertise). Dr. Mohamed H. Elnabtity accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mohamed-elnabtity-360858#insurance).
