## Overview

Dr. John Lee is a nuclear medicine specialist in Ashtabula, Ohio and is affiliated with [Ashtabula County Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/ashtabula-county-medical-center-6410120). He received his medical degree from Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Lee has expertise in treating obesity, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1005618#expertise). Dr. John Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-lee-1005618#insurance).
