## Overview

Dr. Donald T. Schleicher is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma and is affiliated with [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Donald T. Schleicher has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/donald-schleicher-1225294#expertise). Dr. Donald T. Schleicher accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/donald-schleicher-1225294#insurance).
