## Overview

Dr. Jennifer Hong is a pediatric gastroenterologist in Baltimore, Maryland and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [University of Maryland Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/university-of-maryland-hospital-for-children-PA6320330) and [University of Maryland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/university-of-maryland-medical-center-6320330).
