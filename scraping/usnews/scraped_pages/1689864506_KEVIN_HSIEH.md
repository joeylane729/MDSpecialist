## Overview

Dr. Kevin E. Hsieh is a neurosurgeon in Sacramento, California and is affiliated with [Mercy General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/mercy-general-hospital-6932670) and [Fort Sutter Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/fort-sutter-surgery-center-A6208892).
