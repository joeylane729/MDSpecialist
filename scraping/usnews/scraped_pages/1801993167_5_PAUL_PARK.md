## Overview

Dr. Paul Park is an ophthalmologist in Niles, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Loyola University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/loyola-university-medical-center-6430841), [Northwestern Medicine Palos Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/palos-community-hospital-6431060) and [Palos Health Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/palos-health-surgery-center-A8621914). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Paul Park has expertise in treating glaucoma, shingles, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-park-1071530#expertise). Dr. Paul Park accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-park-1071530#insurance).
