## Overview

Dr. Richard Kim is a pulmonologist in Louisville, Kentucky and is affiliated with [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-6741050).
