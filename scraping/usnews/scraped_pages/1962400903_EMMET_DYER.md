## Overview

Dr. Emmet H. Dyer is a neurosurgeon in Charlotte, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-6360280), [Atrium Health-University City](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-university-6360305) and [Carolina Center for Specialty Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/carolina-center-for-specialty-surgery-A1872533). He received his medical degree from University of Mississippi School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Emmet H. Dyer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/emmet-dyer-148900#expertise).
