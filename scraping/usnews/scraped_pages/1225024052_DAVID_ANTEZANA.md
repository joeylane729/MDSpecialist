## Overview

Dr. David F. Antezana is a neurosurgeon in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Legacy Emanuel Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/legacy-emanuel-hospital-and-health-center-6920003), [Providence Portland Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-portland-medical-center-6920520), [East Pavilion Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/east-pavilion-surgery-center-A3578112) and [Plaza Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/plaza-ambulatory-surgery-center-A8248816).
