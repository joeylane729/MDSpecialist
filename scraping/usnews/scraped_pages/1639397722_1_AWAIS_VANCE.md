## Overview

Dr. Awais Vance is a neurosurgeon in Temple, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baylor Scott and White Continuing Care Hospital-Temple](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/scott-and-white-continuing-care-hospital-6740403) and [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Temple](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/scott-and-white-healthcare-6743413). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Awais Vance accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/awais-vance-3116179#insurance). Dr. Awais Vance is highly recommended by patients.
