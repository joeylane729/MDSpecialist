## Overview

Dr. Troy H. Middleton is a neurosurgeon in Mobile, Alabama and is affiliated with [Mobile Infirmary Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/mobile-infirmary-medical-center-6530006).
