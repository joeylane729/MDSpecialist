## Overview

Dr. David Chen is an ophthalmologist in West Caldwell, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Hackensack University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/hackensack-university-medical-center-6220340) and [Vassar Brothers Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/vassar-brothers-medical-center-6214140). He received his medical degree from Harvard Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Chen has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-chen-4069002#expertise). Dr. David Chen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-chen-4069002#insurance).
