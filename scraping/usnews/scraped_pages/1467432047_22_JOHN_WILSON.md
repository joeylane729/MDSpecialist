## Overview

Dr. John Wilson is an urologist in Winston Salem, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Novant Health Forsyth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/novant-health-forsyth-medical-center-6361685) and [Novant Health Kernersville Outpatient Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/novant-health-kernersville-outpatient-surgery-A8666640). He received his medical degree from University of Kentucky College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John Wilson has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-wilson-4041791#expertise). Dr. John Wilson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-wilson-4041791#insurance).
