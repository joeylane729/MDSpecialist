## Overview

Dr. Michael Nanaszko is a neurosurgeon in Salem, Oregon and is affiliated with [Salem Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/salem-hospital-6920708) and [Salem Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/salem-surgery-center-A7476267). He received his medical degree from Weill Cornell Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael Nanaszko has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-nanaszko-2392940#expertise). Dr. Michael Nanaszko accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-nanaszko-2392940#insurance).
