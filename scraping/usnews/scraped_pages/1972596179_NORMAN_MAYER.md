## Overview

Dr. Norman W. Mayer is a neurosurgeon in Pikeville, Kentucky and is affiliated with [Pikeville Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/pikeville-medical-center-6510860). He received his medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Norman W. Mayer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/norman-mayer-171308#expertise). Dr. Norman W. Mayer accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/norman-mayer-171308#insurance).
