## Overview

Dr. Richard P. Murray is an internist in Lexington, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Lexington Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/lexington-medical-center-6370671) and [Moncrief Army Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/moncrief-army-community-hospital-6370280). He received his medical degree from Medical University of South Carolina College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard P. Murray has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-murray-497890#expertise).
