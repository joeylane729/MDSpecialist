## Overview

Dr. Melissa M. Stamates is a neurosurgeon in Fayetteville, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Cape Fear Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/cape-fear-valley-medical-center-6360023).
