## Overview

Dr. David W. Peters is a geriatrician in Kerrville, Texas. He received his medical degree from McGovern Medical School at UTHealth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David W. Peters has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-peters-229896#expertise).
