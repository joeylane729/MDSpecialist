## Overview

Dr. Matthew C. Johnson is an ophthalmologist in Sycamore, Illinois and is affiliated with [Northwestern Medicine Kishwaukee Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/kishwaukee-community-hospital-6431420) and [Dekalb Surgical Services](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/il/dekalb-surgical-services-A6348709).
