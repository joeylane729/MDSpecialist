## Overview

Dr. Christopher A. King is a cardiologist in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Grandview Medical Center-Birmingham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/trinity-medical-center-6530161) and [UAB St. Vincent's Birmingham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/st-vincents-birmingham-6530260).
