## Overview

Dr. Marc Manix is a neurosurgeon in Mobile, Alabama and is affiliated with [Pioneers Memorial Healthcare District](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/pioneers-memorial-healthcare-district-6930335). He received his medical degree from Tufts University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Marc Manix has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marc-manix-1083044#expertise). Dr. Marc Manix accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marc-manix-1083044#insurance).
