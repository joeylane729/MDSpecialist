## Overview

Dr. Steven R. Kanter is a vascular surgeon in Miami, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Health Baptist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/baptist-hospital-of-miami-6390578), [Baptist Health South Miami Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/south-miami-hospital-6390994) and [Surgical Park Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/surgical-park-center-A9250342).
