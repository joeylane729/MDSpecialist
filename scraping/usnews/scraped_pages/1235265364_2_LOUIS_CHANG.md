## Overview

Dr. Louis K. Chang is an ophthalmologist in Los Angeles, California. He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Louis K. Chang has expertise in treating visual impairment and blindness, retinal detachment, cataract, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/louis-chang-591196#expertise).
