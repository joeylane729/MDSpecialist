## Overview

Dr. Simon Morr is a neurosurgeon in New York, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westchester Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/westchester-medical-center-6215150) and [Kaleida Health-Buffalo General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/buffalo-general-medical-center-6211025). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Simon Morr has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/simon-morr-1047433#expertise). Dr. Simon Morr accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/simon-morr-1047433#insurance). Dr. Simon Morr is highly recommended by patients.
