## Overview

Dr. Richard Simon is an orthopedist in Plantation, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Broward Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/broward-general-medical-center-6390210), [Westside Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/westside-regional-medical-center-6390180) and [Memorial Same Day Surgery Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/memorial-same-day-surgery-centers-A8039869). He received his medical degree from Ohio State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard Simon has expertise in treating knee injury, arthritis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-simon-546392#expertise). Dr. Richard Simon accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-simon-546392#insurance).
