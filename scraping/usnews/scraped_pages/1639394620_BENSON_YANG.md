## Overview

Dr. Benson P. Yang is a neurosurgeon in Chicago, Illinois. He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Benson P. Yang has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/benson-yang-607217#expertise). Dr. Benson P. Yang accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/benson-yang-607217#insurance). Dr. Benson P. Yang is highly recommended by patients.
