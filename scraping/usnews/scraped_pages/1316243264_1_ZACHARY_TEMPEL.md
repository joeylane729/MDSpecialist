## Overview

Dr. Zachary J. Tempel is a neurosurgeon in Cincinnati, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bethesda North Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/bethesda-north-hospital-6410382), [The Christ Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/christ-hospital-6410430) and [Mayfield Spine Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/oh/mayfield-spine-surgery-center-A6223510).
