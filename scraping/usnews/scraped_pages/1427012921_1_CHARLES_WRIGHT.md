## Overview

Dr. Charles C. Wright is an urologist in Hot Springs, Arkansas. He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Charles C. Wright has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-wright-226553#expertise).
