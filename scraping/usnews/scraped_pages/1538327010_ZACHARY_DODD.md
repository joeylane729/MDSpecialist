## Overview

Dr. Zachary H. Dodd is a neurosurgeon in New Orleans, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ochsner Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/ochsner-medical-center-6720631) and [Indiana University Health Bloomington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-bloomington-hospital-6420130).
