Cutler is a neurosurgeon in Pomona, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Casa Colina Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/casa-colina-hospital-for-rehabilitative-medicine-6930399) and [Pomona Valley Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/pomona-valley-hospital-medical-center-6932450).
