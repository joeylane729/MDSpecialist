## Overview

Dr. Joseph P. Martin is an internist in Carmel, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. Vincent Carmel](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-vincent-carmel-hospital-6420175) and [Ascension St. Vincent Heart Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-vincent-heart-center-of-indiana-6420036). Martin has expertise in treating obesity, arthritis, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-martin-22271#expertise).
