## Overview

Dr. Neda Heidari is a neurologist in Oxnard, California and is affiliated with [Community Memorial Hospital-Ventura](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/community-memorial-hospital-6930131). She received her medical degree from Tehran University of Medical Sciences School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Neda Heidari has expertise in treating dementia, Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neda-heidari-615353#expertise). Dr. Neda Heidari accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/neda-heidari-615353#insurance).
