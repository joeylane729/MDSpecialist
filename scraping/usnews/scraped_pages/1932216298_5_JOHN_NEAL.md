Neal is an orthopedist in Raleigh, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [WakeMed Raleigh Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/wakemed-health-and-hospitals-6360001) and [University Hospital-Newark](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/university-hospital-6220785).
