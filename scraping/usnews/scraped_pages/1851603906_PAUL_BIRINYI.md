## Overview

Dr. Paul V. Birinyi is a neurosurgeon in Opelousas, Louisiana and is affiliated with [Stormont Vail Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/stormont-vail-healthcare-6671025) and [Ambulatory Surgery Center of Opelousas](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/la/ambulatory-surgery-center-of-opelousas-A1607739).
