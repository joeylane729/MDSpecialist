## Overview

Dr. John C. Edwards is an orthopedist in Bountiful, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health LDS Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/lds-hospital-6870260), [Intermountain Health Layton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/layton-hospital-6870379) and [Mountain West Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ut/mountain-west-surgery-center-A3546725). He received his medical degree from University of Utah School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John C. Edwards has expertise in treating knee injury, arthritis, knee arthroscopy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-edwards-587291#expertise). Dr. John C. Edwards accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-edwards-587291#insurance). Dr. John C. Edwards is highly recommended by patients.
