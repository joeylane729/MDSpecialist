## Overview

Dr. Mina Foroohar is a neurosurgeon in Arlington Heights, Illinois and is affiliated with [Endeavor Health Northwest Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northwest-community-healthcare-6432485). She received her medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mina Foroohar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mina-foroohar-394131#expertise). Dr. Mina Foroohar accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mina-foroohar-394131#insurance). Dr. Mina Foroohar is highly recommended by patients.
