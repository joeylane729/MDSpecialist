## Overview

Dr. Jonathan T. Morgan is a neurosurgeon in Pocatello, Idaho and is affiliated with [Portneuf Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/portneuf-medical-center-6820005). He received his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine - Seton Hill and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jonathan T. Morgan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-morgan-705910#expertise). Dr. Jonathan T. Morgan accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-morgan-705910#insurance). Dr. Jonathan T. Morgan is highly recommended by patients.
