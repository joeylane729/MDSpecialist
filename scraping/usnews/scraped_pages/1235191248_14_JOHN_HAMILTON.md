## Overview

Dr. John H. Hamilton is an emergency medicine physician in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Neighborhood Hospital-Thousand Oaks](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-emergency-hospital-6740937) and [Methodist Hospital Hill Country](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hill-country-memorial-hospital-6741511). Hamilton accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-hamilton-1411719#insurance).
