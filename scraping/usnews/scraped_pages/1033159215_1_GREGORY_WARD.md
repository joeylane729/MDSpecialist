## Overview

Dr. Gregory Ward is an ENT-otolaryngologist in Sandy Springs, Georgia and is affiliated with [Northside Hospital Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northside-hospital-6380171). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Gregory Ward accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-ward-2415861#insurance).
