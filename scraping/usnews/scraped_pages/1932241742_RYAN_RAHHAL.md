## Overview

Dr. Ryan C. Rahhal is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Oklahoma Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/oklahoma-surgical-hospital-6730057) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215). He received his medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ryan C. Rahhal has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ryan-rahhal-55938#expertise). Dr. Ryan C. Rahhal accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ryan-rahhal-55938#insurance).
