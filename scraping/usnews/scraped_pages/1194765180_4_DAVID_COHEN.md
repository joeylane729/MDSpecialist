## Overview

Dr. David Cohen is a gastroenterologist in North Miami, Florida and is affiliated with [Mount Sinai Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/mount-sinai-medical-center-6390640), [Baptist Health Endoscopy Center at Miami Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/baptist-health-endoscopy-center-at-miami-beach-A3922837) and [Surgery Center of Aventura](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/surgery-center-of-aventura-A9148635). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Cohen has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-cohen-2413742#expertise). Dr. David Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-cohen-2413742#insurance). Dr. David Cohen is highly recommended by patients.
