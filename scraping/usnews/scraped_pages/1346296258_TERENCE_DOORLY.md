## Overview

Dr. Terence P. Doorly is a neurosurgeon in Peabody, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Salem Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/north-shore-medical-center-6140002). He received his medical degree from University of Dublin Trinity College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Terence P. Doorly has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/terence-doorly-368722#expertise). Dr. Terence P. Doorly accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/terence-doorly-368722#insurance).
