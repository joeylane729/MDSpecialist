## Overview

Dr. Ali W. Luqman is a neurosurgeon in Royal Oak, Michigan and is affiliated with [DMC Detroit Receiving Hospital and University Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/dmc-detroit-receiving-hospital-6440555). He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Ali W. Luqman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-luqman-2415784#expertise).
