## Overview

Dr. James C. Johnston is an orthopedist in Baltimore, Maryland and is affiliated with [Greater Baltimore Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/md/greater-baltimore-medical-center-6320125). He received his medical degree from University of Maryland School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James C. Johnston has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-johnston-583387#expertise).
