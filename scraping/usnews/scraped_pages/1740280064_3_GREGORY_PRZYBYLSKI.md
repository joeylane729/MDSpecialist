## Overview

Dr. Gregory J. Przybylski is a neurosurgeon in Edison, New Jersey and is affiliated with [JFK University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/jfk-medical-center-6220247). He received his medical degree from Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory J. Przybylski has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-przybylski-7951#expertise).
