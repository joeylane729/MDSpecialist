## Overview

Dr. David C. Waters is a neurosurgeon in Chesapeake, Virginia. He received his medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David C. Waters has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-waters-152757#expertise).
