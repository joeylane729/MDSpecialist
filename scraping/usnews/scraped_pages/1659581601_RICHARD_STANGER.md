## Overview

Dr. Richard Stanger is a neurosurgeon in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baton Rouge General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-general-medical-center-6720042) and [Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/our-lady-of-lake-regional-medical-center-6720028). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Richard Stanger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-stanger-624845#expertise). Dr. Richard Stanger accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-stanger-624845#insurance).
