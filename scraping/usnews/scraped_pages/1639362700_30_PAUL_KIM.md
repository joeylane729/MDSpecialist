## Overview

Dr. Paul S. Kim is a psychiatrist in Kansas City, Missouri and is affiliated with [Corewell Health Greenville Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-united-hospital-6440026). He received his medical degree from A. T. Still University Kirksville College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul S. Kim has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-kim-107609#expertise). Dr. Paul S. Kim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-kim-107609#insurance). Dr. Paul S. Kim is highly recommended by patients.
