## Overview

Dr. Leslie Lyness is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baystate Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/baystate-medical-center-6141955) and [Baystate Wing Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/wing-memorial-hospital-6141660).
