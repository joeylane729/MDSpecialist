## Overview

Dr. Thomas M. Anderson is a radiation oncologist in Columbus, Ohio and is affiliated with [OhioHealth Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/doctors-hospital-6410952). He received his medical degree from Ohio University Heritage College of Osteopathic Medicine in Athens and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas M.
