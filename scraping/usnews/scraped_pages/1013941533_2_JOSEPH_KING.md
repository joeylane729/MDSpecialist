## Overview

Dr. Joseph King is a family medicine doctor in Springboro, Ohio and is affiliated with [Kettering Health Dayton](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/grandview-medical-center-6411182). He received his medical degree from Ohio University Heritage College of Osteopathic Medicine in Athens and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Joseph King has expertise in treating arthritis, hypertension & high blood pressure, depression, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-king-1898195#expertise).
