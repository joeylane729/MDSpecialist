## Overview

Dr. Charles M. Gordon is an orthopedist in Daphne, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Thomas Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/thomas-hospital-6530417) and [USA Health Providence Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/providence-hospital-6530630). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Charles M. Gordon has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-gordon-1056568#expertise). Dr. Charles M. Gordon accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/charles-gordon-1056568#insurance).
