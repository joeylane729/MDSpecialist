Dr. David Garcia is a neurosurgeon in Kansas City, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Children's Mercy Kansas City Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/childrens-mercy-hospitals-and-clinics-6630340) and [University of Kansas Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ks/university-of-kansas-hospital-6670510).
