## Overview

Dr. Akram H. Mahmoud is a neurosurgeon in Hickory, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Cayuga Medical Center at Ithaca](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/cayuga-medical-center-6211960). He received his medical degree from West Virginia School of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Akram H. Mahmoud has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/akram-mahmoud-773658#expertise). Dr. Akram H. Mahmoud accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/akram-mahmoud-773658#insurance).
