## Overview

Dr. Thomas H. Lee is an interventional radiology doctor in Anaheim, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Coast Plaza Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/coast-plaza-doctors-hospital-6932122) and [Corona Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/corona-regional-medical-center-6930045). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas H. Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-lee-739574#insurance).
