## Overview

Dr. Michael Park is an allergist-immunologist in Portage, Michigan and is affiliated with [Bronson Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/bronson-methodist-hospital-6441550). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
