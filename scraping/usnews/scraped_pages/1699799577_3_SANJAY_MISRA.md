## Overview

Dr. Sanjay Misra is an orthopedist in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-medical-center-6740415) and [Christus Santa Rosa Health System-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/santa-rosa-hospital-6743120).
