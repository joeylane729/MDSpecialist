## Overview

Dr. Stephen Q. Parker is a physiatrist in Murrells Inlet, South Carolina and is affiliated with [Tidelands Waccamaw Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/waccamaw-community-hospital-6370018) and [Carolina Coast Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/sc/carolina-coast-surgery-center-A2814643).
