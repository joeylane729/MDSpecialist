## Overview

Dr. Stephen R. Lowe is a neurosurgeon in Charleston, South Carolina and is affiliated with [The University of Tennessee Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/university-of-tennessee-medical-center-6520575). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Stephen R. Lowe has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-lowe-912693#expertise). Dr. Stephen R. Lowe accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-lowe-912693#insurance).
