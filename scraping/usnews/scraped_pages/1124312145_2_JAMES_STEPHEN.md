## Overview

Dr. James H. Stephen is a neurosurgeon in South Weymouth, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [South Shore Hospital-South Weymouth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/south-shore-hospital-6141900), [Brigham and Women's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/brigham-and-womens-hospital-6140215) and [Dry Creek Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/co/dry-creek-surgery-center-A8573830). He received his medical degree from Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. James H. Stephen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-stephen-909319#expertise). Dr. James H. Stephen accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-stephen-909319#insurance).
