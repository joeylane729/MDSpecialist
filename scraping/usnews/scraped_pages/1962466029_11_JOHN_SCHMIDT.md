## Overview

Dr. John Schmidt is a physiatrist in Raleigh, North Carolina and is affiliated with [FirstHealth Moore Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/first-health-moore-regional-hospital-6361150). He received his medical degree from University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John Schmidt has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-schmidt-942870#expertise). Dr. John Schmidt accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-schmidt-942870#insurance).
