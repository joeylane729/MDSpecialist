## Overview

Dr. Brian Lee is a radiation oncologist in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Providence St. Vincent Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-st-vincent-medical-center-6920540) and [Providence Milwaukie Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-milwaukie-hospital-6920296). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Brian Lee accepts Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-lee-614102#insurance). Dr. Brian Lee is highly recommended by patients.
