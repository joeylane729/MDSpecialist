## Overview

Dr. Brian D. Sindelar is a neurosurgeon in Raleigh, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Duke Raleigh Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/duke-raleigh-hospital-6361180). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Brian D. Sindelar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-sindelar-1224538#expertise). Dr. Brian D. Sindelar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-sindelar-1224538#insurance).
