## Overview

Dr. Philip Y. Smucker is a neurosurgeon in Santa Fe, New Mexico and is affiliated with [Christus St. Vincent Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/christus-st-vincent-regional-medical-center-6850390). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Philip Y. Smucker has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/philip-smucker-671392#expertise). Dr. Philip Y. Smucker accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/philip-smucker-671392#insurance). Dr. Philip Y. Smucker is highly recommended by patients.
