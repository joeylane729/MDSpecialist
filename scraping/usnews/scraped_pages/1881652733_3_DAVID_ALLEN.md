## Overview

Dr. David R. Allen is an internist in Omaha, Nebraska and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHI Health Lakeside](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/alegent-creighton-lakeside-hospital-6660011) and [Nebraska Medicine-Nebraska Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/nebraska-medical-center-6660005). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David R. Allen has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-allen-65828#expertise). Dr. David R. Allen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-allen-65828#insurance). Dr. David R. Allen is highly recommended by patients.
