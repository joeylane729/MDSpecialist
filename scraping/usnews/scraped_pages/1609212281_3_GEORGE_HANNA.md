## Specialties

Specialty

### Infectious Disease

Infectious disease (ID) doctors research, diagnose and treat diseases caused by microbes, including bacteria, viruses, parasites and fungi. ID doctors rarely see patients with common illnesses, such as the flu. Instead, they study rare and complicated infections.
