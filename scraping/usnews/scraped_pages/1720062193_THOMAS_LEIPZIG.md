## Overview

Dr. Thomas Leipzig is a neurosurgeon in Indianapolis, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-academic-health-center-6420020) and [Community Hospital North](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/community-hospital-north-6420023).
