## Overview

Dr. Christopher D. Hargett is a neurosurgeon in Dothan, Alabama and is affiliated with [Southeast Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/southeast-alabama-medical-center-6530373). He received his medical degree from University of Pikeville Kentucky College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher D. Hargett has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-hargett-1007835#expertise).
