Dr. Guy Music is a neurosurgeon in Omaha, Nebraska and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Midwest Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ne/midwest-surgical-hospital-6660133) and [Methodist Jennie Edmundson Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/methodist-jennie-edmundson-hospital-6620300).
