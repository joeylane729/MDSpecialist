## Overview

Dr. Allan M. Jorge is a neurosurgeon in South Miami, Florida. He received his medical degree from University of Chicago Division of the Biological Sciences The Pritzker School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Allan M. Jorge has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/allan-jorge-106377#expertise). Dr. Allan M. Jorge accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/allan-jorge-106377#insurance).
