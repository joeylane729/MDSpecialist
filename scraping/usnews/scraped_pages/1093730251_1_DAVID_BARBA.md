## Overview

Dr. David Barba is an orthopedist in Monterey Park, California and is affiliated with [Adventist Health-White Memorial](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/white-memorial-medical-center-6931770) and [Plaza Surgical Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/plaza-surgical-medical-center-A5843703). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. David Barba has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, rotator cuff injury, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-barba-711557#expertise). Dr. David Barba accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-barba-711557#insurance).
