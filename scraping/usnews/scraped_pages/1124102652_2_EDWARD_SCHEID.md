## Overview

Dr. Edward H. Scheid is a neurosurgeon in Paramus, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Chilton Medical Center Atlantic Health System](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/chilton-hospital-6221175) and [Hudson Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/meadowlands-hospital-medical-center-6221315). Scheid has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-scheid-563865#expertise).
