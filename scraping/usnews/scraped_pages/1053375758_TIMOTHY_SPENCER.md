## Overview

Dr. Timothy E. Spencer is a neurosurgeon in Battle Creek, Michigan and is affiliated with [Oaklawn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/oaklawn-hospital-6441750). He received his medical degree from A. T. Still University Kirksville College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Timothy E. Spencer has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/timothy-spencer-782626#expertise).
