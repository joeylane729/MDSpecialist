## Overview

Dr. Manish Singh is a general surgeon in Edinburg, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [South Texas Health System-Edinburg](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/edinburg-regional-medical-center-6740646) and [Rio Grande Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/rio-grande-regional-hospital-6744010). He received his medical degree from Netaji S Chandra Bose Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Manish Singh is highly recommended by patients.
