## Overview

Dr. Thomas S. Ellis is a neurosurgeon in Fort Worth, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baylor Scott and White All Saints Medical Center-Fort Worth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baylor-all-saints-medical-center-at-fort-worth-6741390) and [Baylor Scott and White Surgical Hospital Fort Worth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baylor-surgical-hospital-at-fort-worth-6740297).
