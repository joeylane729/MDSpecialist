## Overview

Dr. Ghassan F. Khayyat is a neurosurgeon in Fairlawn, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [Cleveland Clinic Akron General](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/akron-general-medical-center-6410010). He received his medical degree from American University of Beirut Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ghassan F. Khayyat has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ghassan-khayyat-179058#expertise). Dr. Ghassan F. Khayyat accepts Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ghassan-khayyat-179058#insurance). Dr. Ghassan F. Khayyat is highly recommended by patients.
