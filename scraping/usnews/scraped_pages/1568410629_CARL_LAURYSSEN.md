## Overview

Dr. Carl Lauryssen is a neurosurgeon in Round Rock, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. David's Round Rock Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/st-davids-round-rock-medical-center-6742345) and [Methodist Hospital Hill Country](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hill-country-memorial-hospital-6741511). He received his medical degree from University of Cape Town Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
