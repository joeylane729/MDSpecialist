## Overview

Dr. Kevin T. Foley is a geriatrician in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with [Trinity Health Grand Rapids Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-marys-health-care-6441210).
