## Overview

Dr. Fangxiang Chen is a neurosurgeon in Saint Louis, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [John J. Cochran Veterans Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/veterans-affairs-st-louis-health-care-system-6630280) and [Mercy Hospital South](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/st-anthonys-medical-center-6631160). He received his medical degree from Hunan Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Fangxiang Chen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fangxiang-chen-621685#expertise). Dr. Fangxiang Chen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fangxiang-chen-621685#insurance). Dr. Fangxiang Chen is highly recommended by patients.
