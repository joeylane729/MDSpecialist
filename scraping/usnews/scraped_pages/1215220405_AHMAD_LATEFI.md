## Overview

Dr. Ahmad Latefi is a neurosurgeon in Greenvale, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Huntington Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/huntington-hospital-6211880) and [Lenox Hill Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/lenox-hill-hospital-6212990).
