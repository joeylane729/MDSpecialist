## Overview

Dr. Peter C. Shin is a neurosurgeon in Albuquerque, New Mexico and is affiliated with [University of New Mexico Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/university-hospital-6850037). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter C. Shin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-shin-100285#expertise). Dr. Peter C. Shin accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-shin-100285#insurance).
