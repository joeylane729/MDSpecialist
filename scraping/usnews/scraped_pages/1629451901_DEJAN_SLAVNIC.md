## Overview

Dr. Dejan Slavnic is a neurosurgeon in Novi, Michigan and is affiliated with [Brandon Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/brandon-regional-hospital-6390070) and [Tampa Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/tampa-minimally-invasive-spine-surgery-center-A3526855). He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine at Midwestern University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Dejan Slavnic has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/dejan-slavnic-934098#expertise). Dr. Dejan Slavnic accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/dejan-slavnic-934098#insurance). Dr. Dejan Slavnic is highly recommended by patients.
