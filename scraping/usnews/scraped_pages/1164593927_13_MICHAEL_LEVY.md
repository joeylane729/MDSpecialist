## Overview

Dr. Michael I. Levy is a psychiatrist in Nyack, New York and is affiliated with [Montefiore Nyack Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/nyack-hospital-6213720). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael I. Levy has expertise in treating dementia, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-levy-16421#expertise). Dr. Michael I. Levy accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-levy-16421#insurance).
