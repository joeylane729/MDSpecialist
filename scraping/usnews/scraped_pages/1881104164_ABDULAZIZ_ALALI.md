## Overview

Dr. Abdulaziz Alali is a neurosurgeon in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with [HSHS St. Elizabeth's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/st-elizabeths-hospital-6430200). He received his medical degree from King Saud University College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Abdulaziz Alali has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/abdulaziz-alali-1431323#expertise). Dr. Abdulaziz Alali accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/abdulaziz-alali-1431323#insurance).
