## Overview

Dr. Chad J. Prusmack is a neurosurgeon in Lone Tree, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA HealthONE Sky Ridge](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/sky-ridge-medical-center-6840023), [HCA HealthONE Aurora](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/medical-center-of-aurora-6840014) and [Dry Creek Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/co/dry-creek-surgery-center-A8573830).
