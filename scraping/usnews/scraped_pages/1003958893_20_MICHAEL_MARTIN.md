## Overview

Dr. Michael E. Martin is an internist in Portsmouth, Ohio and is affiliated with [Southern Ohio Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/southern-ohio-medical-center-6411905). He received his medical degree from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael E.
