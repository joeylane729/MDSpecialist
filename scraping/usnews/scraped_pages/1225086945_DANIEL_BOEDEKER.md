## Overview

Dr. Daniel J. Boedeker is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Oklahoma Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/oklahoma-surgical-hospital-6730057) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215). He received his medical degree from University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Daniel J. Boedeker has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-boedeker-341808#expertise). Dr. Daniel J. Boedeker accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-boedeker-341808#insurance).
