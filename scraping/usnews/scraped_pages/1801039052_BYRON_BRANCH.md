## Overview

Dr. Byron C. Branch is a neurosurgeon in Charlotte, North Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-center-6360280), [Atrium Health-Pineville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/carolinas-medical-centerpineville-6360018) and [Carolina Center for Specialty Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/carolina-center-for-specialty-surgery-A1872533). He received his medical degree from Wake Forest School of Medicine of Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Byron C. Branch has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/byron-branch-881287#expertise).
