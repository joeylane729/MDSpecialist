## Overview

Dr. David B. Jones is an orthopedist in Redding, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Shasta Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/shasta-regional-medical-center-6932525) and [St. Elizabeth Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-elizabeth-community-hospital-6932500). He received his medical degree from University of South Alabama College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David B. Jones has expertise in treating spinal fusion, bursitis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-jones-530664#expertise). Dr. David B. Jones accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-jones-530664#insurance). Dr. David B. Jones is highly recommended by patients.
