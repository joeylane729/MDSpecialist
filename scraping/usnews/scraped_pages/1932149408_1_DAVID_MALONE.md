## Overview

Dr. David G. Malone is a neurosurgeon in Tulsa, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension St. John Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-john-medical-center-6731220) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215). Malone is highly recommended by patients.
