## Overview

Dr. Raghu Ramaswamy is a neurosurgeon in Syracuse, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Crouse Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/crouse-hospital-6214890) and [Syracuse Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/syracuse-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6214923). He received his medical degree from Bangalore University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Raghu Ramaswamy has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raghu-ramaswamy-920154#expertise). Dr. Raghu Ramaswamy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/raghu-ramaswamy-920154#insurance).
