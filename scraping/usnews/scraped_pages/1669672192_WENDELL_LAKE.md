## Overview

Dr. Wendell Lake is a neurosurgeon in Madison, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UW Health University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/university-of-wisconsin-hospital-and-clinics-6450820) and [Beloit Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/beloit-memorial-hospital-6450160). He received his medical degree from University of Kentucky College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years.
