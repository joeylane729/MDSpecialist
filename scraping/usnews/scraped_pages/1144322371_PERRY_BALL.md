## Overview

Dr. Perry Ball is a neurosurgeon in Lebanon, New Hampshire and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nh/dartmouth-hitchcock-medical-center-6120170) and [White River Junction Veterans Affairs Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/vt/white-river-junction-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6130270). He received his medical degree from Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Perry Ball accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/perry-ball-530308#insurance). Dr. Perry Ball is highly recommended by patients.
