## Overview

Dr. John F. Cole is a general surgeon in Abilene, Texas and is affiliated with [Hendrick Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/hendrick-medical-center-6740020). He received his medical degree from TCU and UNTHSC School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John F. Cole has expertise in treating colonoscopy, colon removal, upper gi endoscopy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-cole-799268#expertise).
