## Overview

Dr. Gregory J. Rubino is a neurosurgeon in Lake Charles, Louisiana. He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory J. Rubino has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-rubino-203815#expertise). Dr. Gregory J. Rubino accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-rubino-203815#insurance).
