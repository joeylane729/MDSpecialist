## Overview

Dr. William B. Kuhn is a neurosurgeon in Lafayette, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Indiana University Health Arnett Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-arnett-hospital-6420076) and [Halifax Health Medical Center of Daytona Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/halifax-health-6390056). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William B. Kuhn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-kuhn-646269#expertise). Dr. William B. Kuhn accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-kuhn-646269#insurance). Dr. William B. Kuhn is highly recommended by patients.
