## Overview

Dr. Christopher Villar is a neurosurgeon in Pensacola, Florida and is affiliated with [Ascension Sacred Heart Pensacola Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/sacred-heart-hospital-of-pensacola-6390830) and [Sacred Heart ASC](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/sacred-heart-asc-A1807928). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years.
