## Overview

Dr. Todd T. Trier is a neurosurgeon in Shreveport, Louisiana. He received his medical degree from University of Arizona College of Medicine-Tucson and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Todd T. Trier has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/todd-trier-371991#expertise). Dr. Todd T. Trier accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/todd-trier-371991#insurance). Dr. Todd T. Trier is highly recommended by patients.
