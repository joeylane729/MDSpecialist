## Overview

Dr. Robert R. Kraus is a neurosurgeon in Decatur, Illinois and is affiliated with [Decatur Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/decatur-memorial-hospital-6431370). He received his medical degree from Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert R. Kraus has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-kraus-330454#expertise). Dr. Robert R. Kraus accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-kraus-330454#insurance).
