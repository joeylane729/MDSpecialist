## Overview

Dr. Joseph A. Shehadi is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic Mercy Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/mercy-medical-center-6410290) and [Memorial Hospital-Marysville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/memorial-hospital-of-union-county-6411665).
