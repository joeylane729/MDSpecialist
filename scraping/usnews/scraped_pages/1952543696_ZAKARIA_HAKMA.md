## Overview

Dr. Zakaria Hakma is a neurosurgeon in Lawrenceville, New Jersey. He received his medical degree from Damascus University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Zakaria Hakma has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/zakaria-hakma-702235#expertise).
