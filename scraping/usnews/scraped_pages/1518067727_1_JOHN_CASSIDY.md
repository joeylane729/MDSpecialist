## Overview

Dr. John R. Cassidy is a neurosurgeon in Venice, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Englewood Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/englewood-community-hospital-6391450), [HCA Florida Sarasota Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/doctors-hospital-of-sarasota-6390956) and [Advanced Surgery Center of Sarasota](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/advanced-surgery-center-of-sarasota-A3898555). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John R. Cassidy has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-cassidy-20646#expertise). Dr. John R. Cassidy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-cassidy-20646#insurance).
