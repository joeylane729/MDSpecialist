## Overview

Dr. Chong C. Lee is a vascular surgeon in Weston, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Aspirus Wausau Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/aspirus-wausau-hospital-6452085) and [Mercy Medical Center-Cedar Rapids](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/mercy-medical-center-6620170). Lee is highly recommended by patients.
