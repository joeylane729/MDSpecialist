## Overview

Dr. Paul M. MacMahon is a neurosurgeon in Ypsilanti, Michigan and is affiliated with [Trinity Health Ann Arbor Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-joseph-mercy-ann-arbor-hospital-6440087).
