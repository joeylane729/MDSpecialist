## Overview

Dr. Mohamed Somji is a doctor in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is affiliated with [Corewell Health Grand Rapids Hospitals-Blodgett and Butterworth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/spectrum-health-6440021).
