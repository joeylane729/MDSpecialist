## Overview

Dr. Adam C. Brown is a psychiatrist in Maywood, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Loyola University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/loyola-university-medical-center-6430841) and [Saint Joseph Medical Center - Joliet](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/presence-st-joseph-medical-center-6431990).
