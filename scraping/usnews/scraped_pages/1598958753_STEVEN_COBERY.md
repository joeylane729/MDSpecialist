## Overview

Dr. Steven T. Cobery is a neurosurgeon in North Dartmouth, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Southcoast Hospitals-Fall River, New Bedford and Wareham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/southcoast-hospitals-group-6140016). He received his medical degree from The Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven T. Cobery has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-cobery-648144#expertise).
