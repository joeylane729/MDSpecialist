## Overview

Dr. Blake N. Staub is a neurosurgeon in Plano, Texas and is affiliated with [Medical City Dallas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/medical-city-dallas-hospital-6741025). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Blake N. Staub has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/blake-staub-1008357#expertise). Dr. Blake N. Staub is highly recommended by patients.
