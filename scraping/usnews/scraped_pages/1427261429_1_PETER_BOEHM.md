## Overview

Dr. Peter E. Boehm is a neurosurgeon in Chattanooga, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Erlanger Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/erlanger-medical-center-6520065). He received his medical degree from University College of Dublin National Univ SOM and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Peter E. Dr. Peter E. Boehm accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/peter-boehm-615945#insurance).
