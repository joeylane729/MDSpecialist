## Overview

Dr. Matthew Johnson is an emergency medicine physician in Port Charlotte, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Flowers Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/flowers-hospital-6530350) and [HCA Florida Lake City Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/lake-city-medical-center-6390445). He received his medical degree from Lincoln Memorial University Debusk College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew Johnson has expertise in treating pneumonia, gastroenteritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-johnson-1176720#expertise). Dr. Matthew Johnson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-johnson-1176720#insurance).
