## Overview

Dr. Gregory J. Withers is a neurosurgeon in Arcadia, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [USC Arcadia Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/methodist-hospital-of-southern-california-6931630) and [Huntington Health Medical Center-Pasadena](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/huntington-memorial-hospital-6932350). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Gregory J. Withers has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-withers-458885#expertise). Dr. Gregory J. Withers accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/gregory-withers-458885#insurance).
