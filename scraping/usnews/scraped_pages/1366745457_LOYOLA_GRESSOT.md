## Overview

Dr. Loyola Gressot is a neurosurgeon in Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Houston Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/houston-methodist-hospital-6741960), [Houston Methodist West Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-west-houston-hospital-6740878) and [Houston Premiersurgery Center The Villages](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/houston-premiersurgery-center-the-villages-A2745432).
