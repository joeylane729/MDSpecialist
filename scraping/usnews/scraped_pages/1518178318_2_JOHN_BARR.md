## Overview

Dr. John C. Barr is a neurosurgeon in Durham, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Duke University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/duke-university-medical-center-6360355). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John C.
