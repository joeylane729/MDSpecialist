## Overview

Dr. Victor M. Awuor is a neurosurgeon in Pickerington, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [OhioHealth Grant Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/grant-medical-center-ohio-health-6411010) and [OhioHealth Riverside Methodist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/riverside-methodist-hospital-ohio-health-6411110). He received his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine - Seton Hill and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Victor M. Awuor has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-awuor-892377#expertise). Dr. Victor M. Awuor accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/victor-awuor-892377#insurance). Dr. Victor M. Awuor is highly recommended by patients.
