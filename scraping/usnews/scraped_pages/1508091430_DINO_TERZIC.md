## Overview

Dr. Dino Terzic is a neurosurgeon in Duluth, Minnesota. He received his medical degree from Weill Cornell Medical College in Qatar and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Dino Terzic has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/dino-terzic-707693#expertise).
