## Overview

Dr. John Adair Prall is a neurosurgeon in Littleton, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/university-of-colorado-hospital-6840430), [AdventHealth Littleton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/littleton-adventist-hospital-6840805) and [South Denver Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/co/south-denver-surgery-center-A3924533).
