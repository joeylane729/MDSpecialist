## Overview

Dr. Mirza S. Baig is a geriatrician in Alton, Illinois and is affiliated with [Alton Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/alton-memorial-hospital-6430010). He received his medical degree from Kakatiya Medical College NTR and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mirza S. Baig has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mirza-baig-893188#expertise). Dr. Mirza S. Baig accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mirza-baig-893188#insurance).
