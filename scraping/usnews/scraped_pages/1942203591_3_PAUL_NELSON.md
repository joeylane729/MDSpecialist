## Overview

Dr. Paul B. Nelson is a neurosurgeon in Hershey, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Penn State Health Milton S. Hershey Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/penn-state-milton-s-hershey-medical-center-6230705) and [Mount Nittany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/mount-nittany-medical-center-6230130). He received his medical degree from Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Paul B. Nelson has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-nelson-125262#expertise). Dr. Paul B. Nelson accepts Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/paul-nelson-125262#insurance).
