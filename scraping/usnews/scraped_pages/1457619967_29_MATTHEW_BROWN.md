## Overview

Dr. Matthew Brown is an orthopedist in Camden, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cooper University Health Care-Camden](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/cooper-university-hospital-6220190) and [St. Luke's Hospital Quakertown Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/st-lukes-hospital-quakertown-campus-6232820). He received his medical degree from University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Matthew Brown has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/matthew-brown-3019193#expertise). Dr. Matthew Brown is highly recommended by patients.
