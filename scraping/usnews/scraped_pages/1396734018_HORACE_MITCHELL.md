## Overview

Dr. Horace L. Mitchell is a neurosurgeon in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baton Rouge General Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/baton-rouge-general-medical-center-6720042) and [Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/our-lady-of-lake-regional-medical-center-6720028). He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Horace L. Mitchell has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/horace-mitchell-196694#expertise). Dr. Horace L. Mitchell accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/horace-mitchell-196694#insurance).
