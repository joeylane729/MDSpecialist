## Overview

Dr. Stephen M. Smith is a neurosurgeon in South Bend, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hospital of South Bend](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/memorial-hospital-of-south-bend-6421290) and [Mishawaka Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-joseph-regional-medical-center-6421300). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stephen M. Smith has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-smith-432516#expertise). Dr. Stephen M. Smith accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-smith-432516#insurance). Dr. Stephen M. Smith is highly recommended by patients.
