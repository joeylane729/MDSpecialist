## Overview

Dr. John V. Cuff is a neurosurgeon in Athens, Georgia. He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John V. Cuff has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-cuff-465316#expertise).
