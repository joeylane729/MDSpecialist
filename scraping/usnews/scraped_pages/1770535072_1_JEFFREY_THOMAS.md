## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey B. Thomas is a nephrologist in Denver, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/university-of-colorado-hospital-6840430) and [Porter Adventist Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/porter-adventist-hospital-6840360).
