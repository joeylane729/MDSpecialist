## Overview

Dr. Michael Young is an interventional radiology doctor in Cissna Park, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [DeSoto Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/desoto-memorial-hospital-6390002) and [UofL Health-Jewish Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/jewish-hospital-6510510).
