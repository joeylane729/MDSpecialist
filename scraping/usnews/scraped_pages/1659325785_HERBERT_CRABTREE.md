## Overview

Dr. Herbert M. Crabtree is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Missouri and is affiliated with [CoxHealth Springfield](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/cox-health-hospital-6631320). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Herbert M. Crabtree has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herbert-crabtree-363579#expertise). Dr. Herbert M. Crabtree accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herbert-crabtree-363579#insurance).
