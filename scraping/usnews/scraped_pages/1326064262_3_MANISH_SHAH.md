## Overview

Dr. Manish Shah is an urologist in Gadsden, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Gadsden Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/gadsden-regional-medical-center-6530460) and [Riverview Regional Medical Center-Gadsden](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/riverview-regional-medical-center-6530470). He received his medical degree from University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Manish Shah has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/manish-shah-333625#expertise). Dr. Manish Shah accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/manish-shah-333625#insurance).
