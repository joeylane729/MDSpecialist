## Overview

Dr. Samuel C. Kim is an ophthalmologist in Upland, California and is affiliated with [Jerry L. Pettis VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/veterans-affairs-loma-linda-healthcare-system-6931280) and [Pacific Eye Institute Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/pacific-eye-institute-surgery-center-A7201840). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Samuel C. Kim has expertise in treating visual impairment and blindness, retinal detachment, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/samuel-kim-1226895#expertise). Dr. Samuel C. Kim accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/samuel-kim-1226895#insurance). Dr. Samuel C. Kim is highly recommended by patients.
