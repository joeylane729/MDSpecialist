## Overview

Dr. Michael Levy is an emergency medicine physician in Anchorage, Alaska and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Alaska Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ak/alaska-regional-hospital-6940015) and [Providence Alaska Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ak/providence-alaska-medical-center-6940020).
