## Overview

Dr. Guillermo A. Pasarin is a neurosurgeon in Sunrise, Florida. He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Guillermo A. Pasarin has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/guillermo-pasarin-179802#expertise). Dr. Guillermo A. Pasarin accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/guillermo-pasarin-179802#insurance). Dr. Guillermo A. Pasarin is highly recommended by patients.
