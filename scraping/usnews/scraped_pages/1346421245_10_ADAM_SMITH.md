## Overview

Dr. Adam R. Smith is an orthopedist in Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CHI St. Vincent Infirmary](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/st-vincent-infirmary-6710400), [Baptist Health Medical Center-North Little Rock](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/baptist-health-medical-center-6710497) and [Arkansas Specialty Surgery Center-OrthoArkansas](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ar/arkansas-specialty-surgery-center-orthoarkansas-A9649883).
