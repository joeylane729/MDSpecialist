## Overview

Dr. Dave J. Seecharan is a neurosurgeon in Suwanee, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory Johns Creek Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-johns-creek-hospital-6380400) and [Northeast Georgia Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northeast-georgia-medical-center-6380019).
