## Overview

Dr. Paul S. Kim is a plastic surgeon in Exton, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Penn Medicine Chester County Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/chester-county-hospital-6233400) and [The Surgery Center of Chester County](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/pa/the-surgery-center-of-chester-county-A9625384).
