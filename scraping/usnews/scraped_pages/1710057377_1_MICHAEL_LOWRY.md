## Overview

Dr. Michael R. Lowry is a psychiatrist in Salt Lake City, Utah and is affiliated with [University of Utah Health, University of Utah Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/university-of-utah-health-care-6870315). He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael R. Lowry has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lowry-487615#expertise). Dr. Michael R. Lowry accepts Medicare, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-lowry-487615#insurance). Dr. Michael R. Lowry is highly recommended by patients.
