## Overview

Dr. Suguna Pappu is a neurosurgeon in Urbana, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Carle Foundation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/carle-foundation-hospital-6433070) and [Edward Hines, Jr. Veterans Affairs Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/edward-hines-jr-veterans-affairs-hospital-6431910).
