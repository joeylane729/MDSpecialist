## Overview

Dr. Richard J. Gorman is a neurosurgeon in Kettering, Ohio and is affiliated with [Kettering Health Main Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/kettering-medical-center-6411466). He received his medical degree from Kansas City University of Medicine and Biosciences College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Richard J. Gorman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-gorman-3257587#expertise). Dr. Richard J. Gorman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-gorman-3257587#insurance). Dr. Richard J. Gorman is highly recommended by patients.
