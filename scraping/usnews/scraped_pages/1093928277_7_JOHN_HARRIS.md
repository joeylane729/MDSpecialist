## Overview

Dr. John P. Harris is an interventional radiology doctor in San Francisco, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Francis Memorial Hospital-San Francisco](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-francis-memorial-hospital-6933020) and [UCSF Health St. Mary's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-marys-hospital-and-medical-center-6933050). He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John P. Harris accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-harris-562153#insurance). Dr. John P. Harris is highly recommended by patients.
