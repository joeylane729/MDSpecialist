## Overview

Dr. Christopher J. Abood is a neurosurgeon in East Lansing, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [McLaren Greater Lansing Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/mclaren-greater-lansing-hospital-6440009), [University of Michigan Health-Sparrow](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/sparrow-hospital-6441595) and [Red Cedar Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/mi/red-cedar-surgery-center-A1684865). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher J. Abood has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-abood-359957#expertise). Dr. Christopher J. Abood accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-abood-359957#insurance).
