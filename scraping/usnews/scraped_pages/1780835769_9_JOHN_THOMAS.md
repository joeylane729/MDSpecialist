## Overview

Dr. John A. Thomas is a neurosurgeon in Wilmington, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Novant Health New Hanover Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/new-hanover-regional-medical-center-6360019) and [Wilmington Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nc/wilmington-surgery-center-A6223367). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John A. Thomas has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-thomas-695657#expertise). Dr. John A. Thomas accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-thomas-695657#insurance). Dr. John A. Thomas is highly recommended by patients.
