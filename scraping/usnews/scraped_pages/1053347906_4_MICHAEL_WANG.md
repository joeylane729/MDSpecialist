## Overview

Dr. Michael M. Wang is an internist in Covina, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emanate Health Queen of The Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/citrus-valley-medical-center-6930689) and [Foothill Presbyterian Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/foothill-presbyterian-hospital-6930859).
