## Overview

Dr. John D. Campbell is a neurologist in Naples, Florida. He received his medical degree from University of Saskatchewan College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John D. Campbell has expertise in treating Parkinson's disease, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-campbell-20558#expertise). Dr. John D. Campbell accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-campbell-20558#insurance). Dr. John D. Campbell is highly recommended by patients.
