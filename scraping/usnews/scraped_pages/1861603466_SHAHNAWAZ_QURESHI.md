## Overview

Dr. Shahnawaz H. Qureshi is a neurosurgeon in Fort Wayne, Indiana and is affiliated with [Lutheran Hospital of Indiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/lutheran-hospital-6420420). He received his medical degree from University of Pikeville Kentucky College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Shahnawaz H. Qureshi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shahnawaz-qureshi-886581#expertise). Dr. Shahnawaz H. Qureshi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/shahnawaz-qureshi-886581#insurance).
