## Overview

Dr. Christopher Holland is an orthopedist in Germantown, Tennessee and is affiliated with [Baptist Memorial Hospital-Memphis](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/baptist-memorial-hospital-memphis-6520681). He received his medical degree from University of California Riverside School of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Christopher Holland has expertise in treating arthritis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-holland-1374215#expertise).
