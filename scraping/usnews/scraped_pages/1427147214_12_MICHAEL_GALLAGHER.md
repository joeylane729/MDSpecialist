## Overview

Dr. Michael T. Gallagher is an orthopedist in Butte, Montana. He received his medical degree from University of Chicago Division of the Biological Sciences The Pritzker School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael T. Gallagher has expertise in treating hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-gallagher-70135#expertise). Dr. Michael T.
