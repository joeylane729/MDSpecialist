## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey B. Randall is a neurosurgeon in Castro Valley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Eden Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/eden-medical-center-6930133) and [Alta Bates Summit Medical Center-Berkeley](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/alta-bates-summit-medical-center-6930290). He received his medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey B. Randall has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-randall-337086#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey B. Randall accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-randall-337086#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey B. Randall is highly recommended by patients.
