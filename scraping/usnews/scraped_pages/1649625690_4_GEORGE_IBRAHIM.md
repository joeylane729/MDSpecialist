## Overview

Dr. George Ibrahim is a general surgeon in Newark, Delaware and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ChristianaCare Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/christiana-care-hospital-6310120), [St. Francis Hospital-Wilmington](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/st-francis-hospital-6310140), [American Surgery Center-Wilmington](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/american-surgery-center-wilmington-A8891886) and [Delaware Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/delaware-surgery-center-A9330076). He received his medical degree from Ain Shams University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. George Ibrahim has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-ibrahim-1001472#expertise). Dr. George Ibrahim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/george-ibrahim-1001472#insurance).
