## Overview

Dr. James Cooper is a nephrologist in Aurora, Colorado and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/university-of-colorado-hospital-6840430) and [Aspen Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/aspen-valley-hospital-district-6840020). He received his medical degree from Wayne State University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
