## Overview

Dr. David R. Oliver-Smith is a neurosurgeon in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AHN Allegheny General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/allegheny-general-hospital-6230041) and [AHN Wexford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/ahn-wexford-hospital-6230602). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David R. Oliver-Smith has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-oliver-smith-172115#expertise). Dr. David R. Oliver-Smith accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-oliver-smith-172115#insurance).
