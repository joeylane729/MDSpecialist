## Overview

Dr. Mitesh Shah is an orthopedist in Wynnewood, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Main Line Health Lankenau Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/lankenau-hospital-6231970) and [Surgical Institute of Reading](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/surgical-institute-of-reading-6230289). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Mitesh Shah has expertise in treating knee replacement, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mitesh-shah-1104140#expertise). Dr. Mitesh Shah accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mitesh-shah-1104140#insurance).
