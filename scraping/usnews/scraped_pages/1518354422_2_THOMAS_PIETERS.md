## Overview

Dr. Thomas Pieters is a doctor in Worcester, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [UMass Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/umass-memorial-medical-center-6141095). He received his medical degree from McGovern Medical School at UTHealth and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Thomas Pieters has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-pieters-3407276#expertise). Dr. Thomas Pieters accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-pieters-3407276#insurance).
