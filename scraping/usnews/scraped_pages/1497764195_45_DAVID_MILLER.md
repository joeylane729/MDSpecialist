## Overview

Dr. David M. Miller is an internist in Boston, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [MelroseWakefield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/hallmark-hospital-6140011). He received his medical degree from Washington University in St. Louis School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David M. Miller has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-miller-478344#expertise). Dr. David M. Miller accepts Medicare, Aetna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-miller-478344#insurance). Dr. David M. Miller is highly recommended by patients.
