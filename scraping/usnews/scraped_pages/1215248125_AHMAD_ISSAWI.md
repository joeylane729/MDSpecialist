## Overview

Dr. Ahmad Issawi is a neurosurgeon in Ypsilanti, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Trinity Health Ann Arbor Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/st-joseph-mercy-ann-arbor-hospital-6440087) and [Chelsea Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/chelsea-community-hospital-6440331). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Ahmad Issawi has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ahmad-issawi-735287#expertise). Dr. Ahmad Issawi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ahmad-issawi-735287#insurance).
