## Overview

Dr. David Chang is an infectious disease specialist in Murray, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Intermountain Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/intermountain-medical-center-6870141) and [Intermountain Health Alta View Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/alta-view-hospital-6870500).
