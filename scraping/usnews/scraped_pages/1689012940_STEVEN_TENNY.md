## Overview

Dr. Steven Tenny is a neurosurgeon in Salina, Kansas. He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Steven Tenny has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-tenny-1022751#expertise). Dr. Steven Tenny accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-tenny-1022751#insurance). Dr. Steven Tenny is highly recommended by patients.
