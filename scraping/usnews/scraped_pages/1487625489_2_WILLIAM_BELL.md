## Overview

Dr. William J. Bell is an emergency medicine physician in Louisville, Mississippi and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Winston Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/winston-medical-center-6540455) and [Forrest General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/forrest-general-hospital-6540315). Bell has expertise in treating dementia, coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-bell-340838#expertise).
