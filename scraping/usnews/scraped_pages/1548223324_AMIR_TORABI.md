## Overview

Dr. Amir M. Torabi is a neurologist in Mesquite, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Dallas Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/dallas-regional-medical-center-6740379) and [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Sunnyvale](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-regional-medical-center-at-sunnyvale-6740550). He received his medical degree from Tehran University of Medical Sciences School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Amir M. Torabi has expertise in treating dementia, sleep apnea, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amir-torabi-315856#expertise). Dr. Amir M. Torabi accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amir-torabi-315856#insurance).
