## Overview

Dr. James C. Liu is an ophthalmologist in Cupertino, California. He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James C. Liu has expertise in treating retinal detachment, cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-liu-387020#expertise). Dr. James C. Liu accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-liu-387020#insurance). Dr. James C. Liu is highly recommended by patients.
