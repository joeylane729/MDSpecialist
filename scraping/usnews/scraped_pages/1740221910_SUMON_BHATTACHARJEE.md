## Overview

Dr. Sumon Bhattacharjee is a neurosurgeon in Neenah, Wisconsin and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [ThedaCare Regional Medical Center-Neenah](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/theda-clark-medical-center-6451290), [Ascension NE Wisconsin-Mercy Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/mercy-medical-center-6451410) and [Advanced Spine Center of Wisconsin](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wi/advanced-spine-center-of-wisconsin-A8564726). He received his medical degree from Meharry Medical College School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sumon Bhattacharjee has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sumon-bhattacharjee-392691#expertise). Dr. Sumon Bhattacharjee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sumon-bhattacharjee-392691#insurance).
