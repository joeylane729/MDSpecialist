## Overview

Dr. Ali Krisht is a neurosurgeon in Sherwood, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arkansas Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-childrens-hospital-6710340) and [CHI St. Vincent Infirmary](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/st-vincent-infirmary-6710400).
