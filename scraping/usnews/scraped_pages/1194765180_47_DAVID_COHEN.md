## Overview

Dr. David Cohen is an emergency medicine physician in Stony Brook, New York and is affiliated with [Stony Brook University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/stony-brook-university-hospital-6214775). He received his medical degree from Tufts University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Cohen accepts insurance plans - see [insurance accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-cohen-423609#insurance) below. Dr. David Cohen is highly recommended by patients.
