## Overview

Dr. Mark A. White is a neurosurgeon in Westerville, Ohio and is affiliated with [Mount Carmel St. Ann's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/mount-carmel-st-anns-6411060) and [Dublin Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/oh/dublin-surgery-center-A7770672). He received his medical degree from TCU and UNTHSC School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark A. White has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-white-3973304#expertise). Dr. Mark A. White accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-white-3973304#insurance). Dr. Mark A. White is highly recommended by patients.
