## Overview

Dr. Christopher M. King is a neurologist in Ithaca, New York and is affiliated with [Cayuga Medical Center at Ithaca](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/cayuga-medical-center-6211960). He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher M. King has expertise in treating Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, sleep apnea, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-king-686937#expertise). Dr. Christopher M. King accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-king-686937#insurance).
