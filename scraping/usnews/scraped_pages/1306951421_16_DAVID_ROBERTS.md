## Overview

Dr. David A. Roberts is an orthopedist in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Methodist Hospital-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-hospital-6743122) and [South Texas Spine and Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/south-texas-spine-and-surgical-hospital-6740245).
