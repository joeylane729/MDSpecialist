## Overview

Dr. John F. Floyd is a geriatrician in Spokane, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MultiCare Deaconess Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/deaconess-medical-center-spokane-6910880) and [Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center and Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/providence-sacred-heart-medical-center-and-childrens-hospital-6910980). He received his medical degree from University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John F. Floyd has expertise in treating kidney disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-floyd-184520#expertise). Dr. John F. Floyd accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-floyd-184520#insurance). Dr. John F. Floyd is highly recommended by patients.
