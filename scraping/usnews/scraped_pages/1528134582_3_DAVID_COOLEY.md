## Overview

Dr. David Cooley is a gastroenterologist in Bryan, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Community Hospitals and Wellness Centers](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/community-hospitals-and-wellness-center-6410225) and [Parkview Montpelier Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/community-hospitals-and-wellness-centers-montpelier-6410049). He received his medical degree from Ohio State University College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. David Cooley has expertise in treating colonoscopy, upper gi endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-cooley-1218917#expertise). Dr. David Cooley accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-cooley-1218917#insurance).

### Gastroenterology

Gastroenterologists diagnose and treat problems of the esophagus, stomach, pancreas, gallbladder, liver and intestines. Common conditions seen by gastroenterologists include swallowing problems (dysphagia), inflammatory bowel disease (ulcerative colitis or Crohn's disease) and fatty liver disease.
