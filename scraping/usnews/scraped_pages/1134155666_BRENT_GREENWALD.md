## Overview

Greenwald is a neurosurgeon in Idaho Falls, Idaho and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Eastern Idaho Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/eastern-idaho-regional-medical-center-6820165) and [Mountain View Hospital-Idaho Falls](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/mountain-view-hospital-6820006).
