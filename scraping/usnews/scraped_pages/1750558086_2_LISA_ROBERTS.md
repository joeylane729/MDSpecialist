## Overview

Dr. Lisa A. Roberts is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Las Vegas, Nevada and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Southern Hills Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/southern-hills-hospital-and-medical-center-6880013) and [St. Rose Dominican Hospitals-San Martin Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/st-rose-domincian-hospitals-6880032). She received her medical degree from University of Nevada Reno School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Lisa A. Roberts has expertise in treating hysterectomy, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lisa-roberts-240317#expertise). Dr. Lisa A. Roberts accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lisa-roberts-240317#insurance).
