## Overview

Dr. Daniel Chang is an emergency medicine physician in La Mesa, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Garden Grove Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/garden-grove-hospital-and-medical-center-6930833) and [Sharp Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sharp-memorial-hospital-6932785).
