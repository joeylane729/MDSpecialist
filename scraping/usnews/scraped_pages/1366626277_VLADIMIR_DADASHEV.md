## Overview

Dr. Vladimir Dadashev is a neurosurgeon in Rockville Centre, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mercy Hospital-Rockville Centre](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mercy-medical-center-6214360) and [St. Catherine of Siena Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-catherine-of-siena-medical-center-6214616).
