## Overview

Dr. Jaafar Basma is a neurosurgeon in Sherwood, Arkansas. He received his medical degree from St Joseph's University Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Jaafar Basma has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jaafar-basma-1101383#expertise). Dr. Jaafar Basma is highly recommended by patients.
