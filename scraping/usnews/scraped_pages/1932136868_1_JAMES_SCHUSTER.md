## Overview

Dr. James E. Schuster is a dermatologist in Fond du Lac, Wisconsin. He received his medical degree from University of Wisconsin School of Medicine and Public Health and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James E. Schuster has expertise in treating psoriasis, acne, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-schuster-231321#expertise). Dr. James E. Schuster accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-schuster-231321#insurance). Dr. James E.
