## Overview

Dr. Christopher G. Wilkerson is a neurosurgeon in Murray, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health Intermountain Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/intermountain-medical-center-6870141) and [Intermountain Health LDS Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/lds-hospital-6870260).
