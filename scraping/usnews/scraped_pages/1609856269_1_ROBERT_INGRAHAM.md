## Overview

Dr. Robert Ingraham is a neurosurgeon in North Little Rock, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Arkansas Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/arkansas-surgical-hospital-6710028) and [Baptist Memorial Hospital-North Mississippi](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/baptist-memorial-hospital-north-mississippi-6540693).
