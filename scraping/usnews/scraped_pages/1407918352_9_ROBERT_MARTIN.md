## Overview

Dr. Robert Martin is a cardiologist in Newport, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Tennova North Knoxville Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/tennova-north-knoxville-medical-center-6520047) and [The University of Tennessee Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/university-of-tennessee-medical-center-6520575). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert Martin has expertise in treating heart bypass surgery, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-martin-1144662#expertise). Dr. Robert Martin accepts Humana, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-martin-1144662#insurance). Dr. Robert Martin is highly recommended by patients.
