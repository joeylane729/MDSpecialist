## Overview

Dr. Mike K. Chen is a pediatric (general) surgery doctor in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Children's Hospital of Alabama at UAB](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/childrens-hospital-of-alabama-at-uab-6530170) and [University of Alabama at Birmingham Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/university-of-alabama-hospital-at-birmingham-6530304).
