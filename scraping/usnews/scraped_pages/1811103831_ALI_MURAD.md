## Overview

Dr. Ali K. Murad is a neurosurgeon in Pueblo, Colorado and is affiliated with [Parkview Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/parkview-medical-center-6840850). He received his medical degree from Aga Khan Medical College and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ali K. Murad has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-murad-620099#expertise). Dr. Ali K. Murad accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-murad-620099#insurance).
