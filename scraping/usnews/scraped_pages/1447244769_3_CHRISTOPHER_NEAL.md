## Overview

Neal is a neurosurgeon in Scarborough, Maine and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Maine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/me/maine-medical-center-6110430) and [Mary Washington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/mary-washington-hospital-6340290).
