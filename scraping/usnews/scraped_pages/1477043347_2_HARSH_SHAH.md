## Overview

Dr. Harsh A. Shah is an orthopedist in Eatontown, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Community Medical Center-Toms River](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/community-medical-center-6221395) and [Jersey Shore University Medical Center at Hackensack Meridian Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/jersey-shore-university-medical-center-6220740).
