## Overview

Dr. David Roberts is a radiologist in Medford, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Virtua Marlton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/virtua-marlton-6220600) and [Virtua Voorhees Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/virtua-voorhees-6221600). He received his medical degree from Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Roberts accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-roberts-4416#insurance).
