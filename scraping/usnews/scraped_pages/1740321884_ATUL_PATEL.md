## Overview

Dr. Atul K. Patel is a neurosurgeon in Fairfield, California and is affiliated with [NorthBay Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/northbay-medical-center-6930403). He received his medical degree from University of California (San Francisco) School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Atul K. Patel has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/atul-patel-2472627#expertise). Dr. Atul K. Patel accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/atul-patel-2472627#insurance).
