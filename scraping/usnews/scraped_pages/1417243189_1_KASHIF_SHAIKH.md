## Overview

Dr. Kashif Shaikh is a neurosurgeon in South Bend, Indiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hospital of South Bend](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/memorial-hospital-of-south-bend-6421290) and [Mishawaka Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/st-joseph-regional-medical-center-6421300). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Kashif Shaikh has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kashif-shaikh-1073818#expertise). Dr. Kashif Shaikh accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/kashif-shaikh-1073818#insurance).
