## Overview

Dr. John R. Orphanos is a neurosurgeon in South Charleston, West Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [CAMC Charleston Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/eye-and-ear-clinic-of-charleston-6350200) and [Charleston Area Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wv/charleston-area-medical-center-6350120). He received his medical degree from West Virginia University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John R. Orphanos has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-orphanos-64466#expertise). Dr. John R. Orphanos accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-orphanos-64466#insurance). Dr. John R. Orphanos is highly recommended by patients.
