## Overview

Dr. Edward T. Cullom is a neurosurgeon in Flowood, Mississippi and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Merit Health River Oaks](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/river-oaks-hospital-6540385), [Merit Health Central](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/central-mississippi-medical-center-6540365) and [Surgicare of Jackson](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ms/surgicare-of-jackson-A3094200). Cullom has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/edward-cullom-493853#expertise).
