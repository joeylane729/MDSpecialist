## Overview

Dr. Michael Cohen is a neurologist in Stafford, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mary Washington Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/mary-washington-hospital-6340290) and [Sentara Northern Virginia Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/sentara-northern-virginia-medical-center-6341210). He received his medical degree from University of Maryland School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael Cohen has expertise in treating dementia, multiple sclerosis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-192853#expertise). Dr. Michael Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-cohen-192853#insurance).
