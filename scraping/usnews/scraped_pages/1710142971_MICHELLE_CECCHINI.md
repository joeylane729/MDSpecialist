## Overview

Dr. Michelle M. Cecchini is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with [Methodist Hospital-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-hospital-6743122). She received her medical degree from Rush Medical College of Rush University Medical Center and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michelle M. Cecchini has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michelle-cecchini-43324#expertise). Dr. Michelle M. Cecchini accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michelle-cecchini-43324#insurance).
