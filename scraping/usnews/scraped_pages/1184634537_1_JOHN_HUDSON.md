## Overview

Dr. John K. Hudson is an oncologist in Augusta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Doctors Hospital of Augusta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/doctors-hospital-6380265) and [Piedmont Augusta Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/university-hospital-6380270).
