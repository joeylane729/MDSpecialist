## Overview

Dr. Paul R. Mitchell is an anesthesiologist in Hilton Head, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Coastal Carolina Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/coastal-carolina-hospital-6370023) and [Hilton Head Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/hilton-head-hospital-6370390).
