## Overview

Dr. Lawrence E. Clark is a neurosurgeon in Riverside, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Riverside Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/riverside-community-hospital-6932585) and [Parkview Community Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/parkview-community-hospital-medical-center-6930120). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Lawrence E. Clark accepts Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/lawrence-clark-334206#insurance). Dr. Lawrence E. Clark is highly recommended by patients.
