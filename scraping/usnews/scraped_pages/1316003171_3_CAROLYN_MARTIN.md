## Overview

Dr. Carolyn Martin is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with [Swedish Medical Center-Cherry Hill](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/swedish-medical-center-cherry-hill-6910680). She received her medical degree from University of Washington School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
