## Overview

Dr. Marshall A. Cain is a neurosurgeon in Monroe, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Monroe Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/monroe-surgical-hospital-6720098), [Glenwood Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/glenwood-regional-medical-center-6720891) and [Advanced Surgery Center of Northern Louisiana](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/la/advanced-surgery-center-of-northern-louisiana-A9831932). He received his medical degree from McGovern Medical School at UTHealth and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Marshall A. Cain has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marshall-cain-657778#expertise). Dr. Marshall A. Cain accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/marshall-cain-657778#insurance). Dr. Marshall A. Cain is highly recommended by patients.
