## Overview

Dr. James P. Burke is a neurosurgeon in Altoona, Pennsylvania. He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James P. Burke has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-burke-779728#expertise).
