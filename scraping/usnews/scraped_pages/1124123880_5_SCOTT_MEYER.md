## Overview

Dr. Scott A. Meyer is a neurosurgeon in Cedar Knolls, New Jersey and is affiliated with [Kessler Institute for Rehabilitation](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/kessler-institute-for-rehabilitation-6220253) and [Florham Park Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/nj/florham-park-surgery-center-A5168318).
