## Overview

Dr. Herman C. Lawson is a neurosurgeon in West Reading, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [Reading Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/reading-hospital-and-medical-center-6233443). He received his medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Herman C. Lawson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herman-lawson-584018#expertise). Dr. Herman C. Lawson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/herman-lawson-584018#insurance).
