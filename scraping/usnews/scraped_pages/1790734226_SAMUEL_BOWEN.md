## Overview

Dr. Samuel R. Bowen is a neurosurgeon in Birmingham, Alabama and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [UAB St. Vincent's Birmingham](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/st-vincents-birmingham-6530260) and [Coosa Valley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/coosa-valley-medical-center-6530900).
