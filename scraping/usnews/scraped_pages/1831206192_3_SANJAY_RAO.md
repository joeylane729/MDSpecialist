## Overview

Dr. Sanjay S. Rao is a psychiatrist in La Mesa, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sharp Grossmont Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sharp-grossmont-hospital-6930887) and [San Diego County Psychiatric Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/san-diego-county-psychiatric-hospital-6932813). He received his medical degree from The Flinders University of South Australia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sanjay S. Rao has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-rao-824783#expertise). Dr. Sanjay S. Rao accepts Medicare, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-rao-824783#insurance).
