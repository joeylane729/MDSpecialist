## Overview

Dr. Brandon J. Kelly is a resident physician in Eagan, Minnesota and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [M Health Fairview Woodwinds Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/woodwinds-health-campus-6610009) and [Minneapolis VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/veterans-affairs-medical-center-6611080). Kelly has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brandon-kelly-1191556#expertise). Kelly is highly recommended by patients.
