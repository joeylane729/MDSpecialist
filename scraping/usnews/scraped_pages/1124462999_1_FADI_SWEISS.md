## Overview

Dr. Fadi Sweiss is a neurosurgeon in Williamsport, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with [UPMC Williamsport](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/williamsport-regional-medical-center-6233500). He received his medical degree from Northeast Ohio Medical University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Fadi Sweiss has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fadi-sweiss-1174935#expertise). Dr. Fadi Sweiss accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/fadi-sweiss-1174935#insurance). Dr. Fadi Sweiss is highly recommended by patients.
