## Overview

Dr. John W. German is a neurosurgeon in Albany, New York and is affiliated with [Albany Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/albany-medical-center-6210030). He received his medical degree from University of Vermont College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John W. German has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-german-469791#expertise). Dr. John W. German accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-german-469791#insurance).
