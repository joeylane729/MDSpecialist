## Overview

Dr. Jayashree Srinivasan is a neurosurgeon in Seattle, Washington and is affiliated with [Swedish First Hill Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/swedish-medical-center-first-hill-6910006) and [Seattle Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wa/seattle-surgery-center-A1174550). She received her medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jayashree Srinivasan has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jayashree-srinivasan-3062740#expertise). Dr. Jayashree Srinivasan accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jayashree-srinivasan-3062740#insurance). Dr. Jayashree Srinivasan is highly recommended by patients.
