## Overview

Dr. Mark D. Meyer is an ophthalmologist in Mason City, Iowa. He received his medical degree from University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille A. Carver College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mark D. Meyer has expertise in treating retinal detachment, cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-meyer-519389#expertise). Dr. Mark D. Meyer accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-meyer-519389#insurance). Dr. Mark D. Meyer is highly recommended by patients.
