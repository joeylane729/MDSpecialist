## Overview

Dr. Ronnie I. Mimran is a neurosurgeon in Castro Valley, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Eden Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/eden-medical-center-6930133), [San Ramon Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/san-ramon-regional-medical-center-6930041) and [Hacienda Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/hacienda-surgery-center-A7318955). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ronnie I. Mimran has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ronnie-mimran-2734#expertise). Dr. Ronnie I. Mimran accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ronnie-mimran-2734#insurance).
