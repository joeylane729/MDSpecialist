## Overview

Dr. David Wrubel is a neurosurgeon in Atlanta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Children's Healthcare of Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/childrens-healthcare-of-atlanta-6380021) and [Emory University Hospital Midtown](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-university-hospital-midtown-6380080). He received his medical degree from University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David Wrubel accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-wrubel-331999#insurance).
