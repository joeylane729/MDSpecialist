## Overview

Dr. Rajiv D. Desai is a neurosurgeon in Scarborough, Maine and is affiliated with [Maine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/me/maine-medical-center-6110430). He received his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Rajiv D. Desai has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rajiv-desai-845936#expertise). Dr. Rajiv D. Desai accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rajiv-desai-845936#insurance).
