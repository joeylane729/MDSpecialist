## Overview

Dr. Andrew E. Wakefield is a neurosurgeon in Hartford, Connecticut and is affiliated with [Hartford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/hartford-hospital-6160003). He received his medical degree from University of Connecticut and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Andrew E. Wakefield has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-wakefield-498955#expertise). Dr. Andrew E. Wakefield accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/andrew-wakefield-498955#insurance).
