## Overview

Dr. Sebastian Rubino is a neurosurgeon in Staten Island, New York and is affiliated with [Staten Island University Hospital at Northwell Health](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/staten-island-university-hospital-6214735). He received his medical degree from Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Sebastian Rubino has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sebastian-rubino-1085293#expertise). Dr. Sebastian Rubino accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sebastian-rubino-1085293#insurance).
