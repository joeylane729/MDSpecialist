## Overview

Dr. Michael E. Seiff is a neurosurgeon in Las Vegas, Nevada and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MountainView Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/mountain-view-hospital-6880007) and [Sunrise Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nv/sunrise-hospital-and-medical-center-6880069).
