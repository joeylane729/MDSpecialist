## Overview

Dr. Mark Anderson is an urologist in Charlottesville, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sentara Martha Jefferson Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/martha-jefferson-hospital-6340120), [Augusta Health-Fishersville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/augusta-health-6340005) and [Culpeper Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/va/culpeper-surgery-center-A5019799). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Mark Anderson has expertise in treating kidney disease, kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-anderson-4049887#expertise). Dr. Mark Anderson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mark-anderson-4049887#insurance).
