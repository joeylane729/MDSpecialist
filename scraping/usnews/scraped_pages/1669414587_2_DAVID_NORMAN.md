## Overview

Dr. David C. Norman is a pediatrician in Belleville, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hospital-Belleville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/memorial-hospital-6430195) and [St. Louis Children's Hospital-Washington University](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/st-louis-childrens-hospital-6631180).
