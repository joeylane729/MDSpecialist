## Overview

Dr. Jennifer Kim is a family medicine doctor in Mcalester, Oklahoma and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [McAlester Regional Health Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/mcalester-regional-health-center-6730590) and [St. Francis Hospital-Tulsa](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ok/st-francis-hospital-6731215).
