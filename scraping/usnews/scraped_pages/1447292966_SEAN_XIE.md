## Overview

Dr. Sean Xie is a neurosurgeon in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Adventist Health-White Memorial](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/white-memorial-medical-center-6931770) and [Dignity Health - California Hospital Medical Center - Los Angeles](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/california-hospital-medical-center-6931440).
