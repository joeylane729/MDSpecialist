## Overview

Dr. Cristina D. Ghinda is a doctor in Mansfield, Ohio and is affiliated with [OhioHealth Mansfield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/medcentral-hospital-6410016). She received her medical degree from University of Montreal Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Cristina D. Ghinda has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/cristina-ghinda-2677325#expertise). Dr. Cristina D. Ghinda accepts Aetna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/cristina-ghinda-2677325#insurance).
