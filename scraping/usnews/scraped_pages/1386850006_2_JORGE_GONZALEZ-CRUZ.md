## Overview

Dr. Jorge Gonzalez-Cruz is a neurosurgeon in Prescott, Arizona and is affiliated with [Presbyterian Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/presbyterian-hospital-6850055). He received his medical degree from Escuela de Medicina Ignacio A. Santos and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jorge Gonzalez-Cruz has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jorge-gonzalez-cruz-3330094#expertise). Dr. Jorge Gonzalez-Cruz accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jorge-gonzalez-cruz-3330094#insurance). Dr. Jorge Gonzalez-Cruz is highly recommended by patients.
