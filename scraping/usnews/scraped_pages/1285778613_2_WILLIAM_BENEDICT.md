## Overview

Dr. William J. Benedict is a neurosurgeon in Marietta, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [WellStar Kennestone Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/kennestone-hospital-6380775) and [Emory University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-university-hospital-6380570). Benedict accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-benedict-115231#insurance).
