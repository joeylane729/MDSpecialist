## Overview

Dr. Robert O. Robinson is an ophthalmologist in Beaumont, Texas. He received his medical degree from University of Texas Medical Branch School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert O. Robinson has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-robinson-526109#expertise). Dr. Robert O. Robinson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-robinson-526109#insurance). Dr. Robert O. Robinson is highly recommended by patients.
