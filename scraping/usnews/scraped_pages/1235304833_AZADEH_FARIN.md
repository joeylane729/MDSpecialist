## Overview

Dr. Azadeh Farin is a neurosurgeon in Long Beach, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MemorialCare Miller Children's and Women's Hospital Long Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/miller-childrens-hospital-6930103) and [St. Mary Medical Center-Long Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-mary-medical-center-6931360).
