## Overview

Dr. Charles Rosen is a neurosurgeon in Bloomington, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [OSF St. Joseph Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/osf-st-joseph-medical-center-6430260) and [Carle BroMenn Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/advocate-bromenn-medical-center-6430245).
