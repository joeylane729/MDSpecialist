Hurlbert is a neurosurgeon in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Banner-University Medical Center Tucson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/university-of-arizona-medical-center-6860516) and [Tucson VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/southern-arizona-veterans-affairs-health-care-system-6860520).
