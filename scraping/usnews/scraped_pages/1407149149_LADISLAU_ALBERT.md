## Overview

Dr. Ladislau Albert is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Baptist Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baptist-medical-center-6740415) and [Christus Santa Rosa Health System-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/santa-rosa-hospital-6743120). He received his medical degree from State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.
