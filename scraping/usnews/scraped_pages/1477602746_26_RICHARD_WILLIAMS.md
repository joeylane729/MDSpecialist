## Overview

Dr. Richard B. Williams is a neurosurgeon in Woodland Hills, California and is affiliated with [Kaweah Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/kaweah-delta-health-care-district-6933850). He received his medical degree from Stanford University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard B. Williams has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-williams-577168#expertise). Dr. Richard B. Williams accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-williams-577168#insurance). Dr. Richard B. Williams is highly recommended by patients.
