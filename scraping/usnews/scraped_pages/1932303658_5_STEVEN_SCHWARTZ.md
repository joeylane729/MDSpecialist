## Overview

Dr. Steven Schwartz is a geriatrician in Independence, Ohio and is affiliated with [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670). He received his medical degree from Tel Aviv University Sackler and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven Schwartz has expertise in treating heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-schwartz-975394#expertise).
