## Overview

Dr. Ali Najafi is a neurosurgeon in Fresno, California and is affiliated with [Community Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/community-regional-medical-center-6930790). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ali Najafi has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-najafi-3378438#expertise). Dr. Ali Najafi accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ali-najafi-3378438#insurance).
