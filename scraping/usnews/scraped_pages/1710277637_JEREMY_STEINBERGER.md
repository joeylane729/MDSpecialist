## Overview

Dr. Jeremy M. Steinberger is a neurosurgeon in New York, New York and is affiliated with [Mount Sinai Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/mount-sinai-medical-center-6213140). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Jeremy M. Steinberger has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeremy-steinberger-871718#expertise). Dr. Jeremy M. Steinberger accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeremy-steinberger-871718#insurance). Dr. Jeremy M. Steinberger is highly recommended by patients.
