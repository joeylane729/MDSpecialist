## Overview

Dr. John W. Thomas is an ophthalmologist in Decatur, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory Decatur Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/dekalb-medical-6380488) and [Emory St. Joseph's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/st-josephs-hospital-6380200). He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John W. Thomas has expertise in treating retinal detachment, cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-thomas-1367743#expertise).
