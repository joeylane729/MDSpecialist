## Overview

Dr. William McAllister is a psychiatrist in Pontiac, Michigan and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Henry Ford Providence Southfield Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/providence-hospital-6440780) and [Hurley Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/hurley-medical-center-6441030). He received his medical degree from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William McAllister has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-mcallister-401931#expertise). Dr. William McAllister accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-mcallister-401931#insurance).
