## Overview

Dr. Ewell L. Nelson is a neurosurgeon in Boulder, Colorado. He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ewell L. Nelson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ewell-nelson-388248#expertise). Dr. Ewell L. Nelson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ewell-nelson-388248#insurance). Dr. Ewell L. Nelson is highly recommended by patients.
