## Overview

Dr. David R. Wallace is a pediatrician in Lake Charles, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Christus Lake Area Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/women-and-childrens-hospital-6724000) and [Lake Charles Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/lake-charles-memorial-hospital-6720355).
