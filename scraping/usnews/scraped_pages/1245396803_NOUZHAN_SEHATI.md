## Overview

Dr. Nouzhan Sehati is a neurosurgeon in Burbank, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Providence St. Joseph Medical Center-Burbank](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-st-joseph-medical-center-6930340) and [Providence Cedars-Sinai Tarzana Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/providence-tarzana-medical-center-6933670).
