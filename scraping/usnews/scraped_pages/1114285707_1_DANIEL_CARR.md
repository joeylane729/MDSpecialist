## Overview

Dr. Daniel L. Carr is an orthopedist in Syracuse, New York and is affiliated with [Auburn Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/auburn-community-hospital-6210180). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Daniel L. Carr has expertise in treating arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-carr-171811#expertise). Dr. Daniel L. Carr accepts Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/daniel-carr-171811#insurance).
