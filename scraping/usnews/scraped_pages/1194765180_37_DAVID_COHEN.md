## Overview

Dr. David M. Cohen is a nephrologist in Portland, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [OHSU Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/oregon-health-and-science-university-6920570) and [Portland VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/portland-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6920590).
