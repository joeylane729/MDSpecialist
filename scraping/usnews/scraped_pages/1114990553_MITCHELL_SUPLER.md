## Overview

Dr. Mitchell L. Supler is a neurosurgeon in Ocoee, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Orlando Health-Health Central Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/health-central-6391150), [Orlando Health St. Cloud Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/st-cloud-regional-medical-center-6390895) and [Global Microsurgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/global-microsurgical-center-A2369033). He received his medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Mitchell L. Supler has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mitchell-supler-266836#expertise). Dr. Mitchell L. Supler accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/mitchell-supler-266836#insurance).
