## Overview

Dr. David A. Miller is an internist in Atlantis, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA Florida Palms West Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/palms-west-hospital-6390753) and [Lakeside Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/lakeside-medical-center-6390035). He received his medical degree from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David A. Miller has expertise in treating lymphedema, hypertension & high blood pressure, gastroenteritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-miller-342012#expertise).
