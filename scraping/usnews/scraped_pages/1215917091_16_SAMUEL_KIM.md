## Overview

Dr. Samuel Kim is an urologist in Lafayette, Indiana and is affiliated with [Indiana University Health Ball Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/iu-health-ball-memorial-hospital-6421040) and [Ball Outpatient Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/in/ball-outpatient-surgery-center-A1893193). He received his medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Samuel Kim has expertise in treating kidney removal surgery, prostate cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/samuel-kim-3431404#expertise). Dr. Samuel Kim accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/samuel-kim-3431404#insurance). Dr. Samuel Kim is highly recommended by patients.
