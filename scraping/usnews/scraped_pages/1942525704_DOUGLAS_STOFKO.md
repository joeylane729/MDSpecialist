## Overview

Dr. Douglas L. Stofko is a neurosurgeon in Tucson, Arizona and is affiliated with [Trident Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/trident-medical-center-6370232). He received his medical degree from Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Douglas L. Stofko has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/douglas-stofko-816154#expertise). Dr. Douglas L. Stofko is highly recommended by patients.
