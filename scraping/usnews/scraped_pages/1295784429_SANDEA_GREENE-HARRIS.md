## Overview

Dr. Sandea A. Greene-Harris is a neurosurgeon in Stockbridge, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory Decatur Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/dekalb-medical-6380488) and [Southern Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/southern-regional-medical-center-6380945). She received her medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sandea A. Greene-Harris has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sandea-greene-harris-342248#expertise). Dr. Sandea A. Greene-Harris accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sandea-greene-harris-342248#insurance).
