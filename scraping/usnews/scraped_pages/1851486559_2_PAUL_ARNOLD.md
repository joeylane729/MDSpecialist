## Overview

Dr. Paul M. Arnold is an urologist in Palm Harbor, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [AdventHealth North Pinellas](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/helen-ellis-memorial-hospital-6391105) and [Mease Countryside Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/mease-countryside-hospital-6390160).
