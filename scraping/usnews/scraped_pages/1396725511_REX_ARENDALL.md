## Overview

Dr. Rex E. Arendall is a neurosurgeon in Nashville, Tennessee. He received his medical degree from Vanderbilt University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Rex E. Arendall has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rex-arendall-253037#expertise). Dr. Rex E. Arendall accepts Aetna, Cigna - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/rex-arendall-253037#insurance). Dr. Rex E. Arendall is highly recommended by patients.
