## Overview

Dr. John T. Main is a neurosurgeon in Ocala, Florida and is affiliated with [HCA Florida Ocala Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/ocala-regional-medical-center-6390680). He received his medical degree from Oklahoma State University Center for Health Sciences and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John T. Main has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-main-774616#expertise). Dr. John T. Main accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-main-774616#insurance).
