## Overview

Dr. Donald P. Atkins is a neurosurgeon in San Antonio, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Methodist Hospital-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-hospital-6743122) and [Methodist Hospital-Stone Oak](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-stone-oak-hospital-6740417). He received his medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Donald P. Atkins has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/donald-atkins-160549#expertise). Dr. Donald P. Atkins accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/donald-atkins-160549#insurance).
