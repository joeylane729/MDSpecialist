## Overview

Dr. Christopher R. Banerjee is a neurosurgeon in Lawrenceville, Georgia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emory University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/emory-university-hospital-6380570) and [Northside Hospital Atlanta](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/northside-hospital-6380171).
