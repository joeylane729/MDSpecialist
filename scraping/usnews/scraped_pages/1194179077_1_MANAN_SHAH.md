## Overview

Dr. Manan Shah is a neonatologist in New Brunswick, New Jersey and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/robert-wood-johnson-university-hospital-6220920) and [The Bristol-Myers Squibb Children's Hospital at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nj/bristol-myers-squibb-childrens-hospital-at-rwj-univ-hosp-PA6220920).
