## Overview

Dr. Susan C. Williams is an interventional radiology doctor in Scarborough, Maine and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Maine Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/me/maine-medical-center-6110430) and [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/massachusetts-general-hospital-6140430). She received her medical degree from Brody School of Medicine at East Carolina University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Susan C. Williams accepts Medicare, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/susan-williams-653431#insurance). Dr. Susan C. Williams is highly recommended by patients.
