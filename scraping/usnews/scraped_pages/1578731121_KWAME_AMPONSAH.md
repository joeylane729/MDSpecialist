## Overview

Dr. Kwame O. Amponsah is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Worcester, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Holy Family Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/caritas-holy-family-hospital-6141355).
