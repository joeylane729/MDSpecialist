## Overview

Dr. James A. Evans is a geriatrician in Quincy, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Emerson Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/emerson-hospital-6140850) and [Mount Auburn Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/mount-auburn-hospital-6140780). He received his medical degree from University of Vermont College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. James A. Evans has expertise in treating dementia, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-evans-468575#expertise). Dr. James A. Evans accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/james-evans-468575#insurance). Dr. James A. Evans is highly recommended by patients.
