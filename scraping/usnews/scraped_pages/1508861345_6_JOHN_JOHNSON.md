## Overview

Dr. John S. Johnson is an internist in Leachville, Arkansas and is affiliated with [St. Bernards Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/st-bernards-medical-center-6710320). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John S. Johnson has expertise in treating obesity, heart failure, hypertension & high blood pressure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-johnson-192374#expertise). Dr. John S. Johnson accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-johnson-192374#insurance).
