## Overview

Dr. Atilio E. Palma is a neurosurgeon in High Point, North Carolina and is affiliated with [Physicians Regional-Pine Ridge Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/physicians-regional-medical-center-pine-ridge-6390103). He received his medical degree from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Atilio E. Palma has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/atilio-palma-935658#expertise). Dr. Atilio E. Palma accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/atilio-palma-935658#insurance). Dr. Atilio E. Palma is highly recommended by patients.
