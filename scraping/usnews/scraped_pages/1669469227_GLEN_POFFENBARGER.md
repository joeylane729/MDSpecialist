## Overview

Dr. Glen J. Poffenbarger is a neurosurgeon in Cody, Wyoming and is affiliated with [Cody Regional Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/west-park-hospital-6830072) and [Northern Wyoming Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wy/northern-wyoming-surgical-center-A7940411).
