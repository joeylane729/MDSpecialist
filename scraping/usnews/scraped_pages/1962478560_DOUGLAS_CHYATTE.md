## Overview

Dr. Douglas Chyatte is a neurosurgeon in Saint Joseph, Missouri and is affiliated with [Mosaic Life Care-St. Joseph](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/heartland-regional-medical-center-6630009). He received his medical degree from McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Douglas Chyatte has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/douglas-chyatte-280227#expertise).
