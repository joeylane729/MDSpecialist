## Overview

Dr. Timothy E. Hopkins is a neurosurgeon in Terre Haute, Indiana and is affiliated with [Terre Haute Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/in/terre-haute-regional-hospital-6421360).
