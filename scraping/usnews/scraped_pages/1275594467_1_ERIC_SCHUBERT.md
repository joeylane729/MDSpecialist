## Overview

Dr. Eric B. Schubert is a neurosurgeon in Casper, Wyoming and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Campbell County Health-Gillette](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/campbell-county-memorial-hospital-6830115) and [Memorial Hospital of Converse County](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wy/memorial-hospital-of-converse-county-6830080). He received his medical degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Eric B. Schubert has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-schubert-305757#expertise). Dr. Eric B. Schubert accepts Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/eric-schubert-305757#insurance). Dr. Eric B. Schubert is highly recommended by patients.
