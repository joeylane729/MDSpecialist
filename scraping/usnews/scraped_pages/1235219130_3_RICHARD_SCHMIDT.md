## Overview

Dr. Richard G. Schmidt is an orthopedist in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Main Line Health Lankenau Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/lankenau-hospital-6231970) and [Temple University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/temple-university-hospital-6232300). Schmidt has expertise in treating knee replacement, arthritis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-schmidt-24263#expertise). Dr. Richard G.
