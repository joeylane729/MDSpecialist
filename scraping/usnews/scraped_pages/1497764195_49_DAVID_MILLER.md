## Overview

Dr. David C. Miller is an orthopedist in Tarboro, North Carolina and is affiliated with [ECU Health Edgecombe Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nc/vidant-edgecombe-hospital-6361470). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. David C.
