## Overview

Dr. Anders Cohen is a neurosurgeon in Brooklyn, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [The Brooklyn Hospital Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/brooklyn-hospital-center-6210545) and [Wyckoff Heights Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/wyckoff-heights-medical-center-6210970).
