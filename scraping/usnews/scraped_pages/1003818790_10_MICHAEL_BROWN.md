## Overview

Dr. Michael A. Brown is an orthopedist in Sonoma, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sonoma Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sonoma-valley-hospital-6933520) and [MarinHealth Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/marin-general-hospital-6933370).
