## Overview

Dr. John K. Capua is a neurosurgeon in Modesto, California and is affiliated with [Memorial Medical Center-Modesto](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/memorial-medical-center-6931929). He received his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine - Seton Hill and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. John K. Capua has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-capua-903198#expertise). Dr. John K. Capua accepts Medicare, Aetna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-capua-903198#insurance). Dr. John K. Capua is highly recommended by patients.
