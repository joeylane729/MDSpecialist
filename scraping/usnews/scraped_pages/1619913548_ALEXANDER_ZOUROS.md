## Overview

Dr. Alexander Zouros is a neurosurgeon in Boise, Idaho and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Luke's Boise Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/st-lukes-regional-medical-center-6820050) and [St. Luke’s Children’s Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/id/st-lukes-childrens-hospital-PA6820050).
