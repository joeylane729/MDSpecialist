## Overview

Dr. Jonathan D. Sherman is a neurosurgeon in Eugene, Oregon and is affiliated with [McKenzie-Willamette Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/mckenzie-willamette-medical-center-6920741) and [Spine Surgery Center of Eugene](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/or/spine-surgery-center-of-eugene-A9715431). He received his medical degree from Indiana University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jonathan D. Sherman has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-sherman-129910#expertise). Dr. Jonathan D. Sherman accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jonathan-sherman-129910#insurance).
