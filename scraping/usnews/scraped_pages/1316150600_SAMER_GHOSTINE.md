## Overview

Dr. Samer S. Ghostine is a neurosurgeon in Los Angeles, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Riverside Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/riverside-community-hospital-6932585) and [UCLA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/ucla-medical-center-6931755). Dr. Samer S. Ghostine is highly recommended by patients.
