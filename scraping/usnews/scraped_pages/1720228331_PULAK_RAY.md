## Overview

Dr. Pulak Ray is a neurosurgeon in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Temple University Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/temple-university-hospital-6232300), [Beebe Healthcare-Lewes Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/de/beebe-medical-center-6310030), [Delaware Outpatient Center for Surgery](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/delaware-outpatient-center-for-surgery-A7531820) and [Delaware Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/de/delaware-surgery-center-A9330076). He received his medical degree from University of Maryland School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Pulak Ray has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/pulak-ray-700860#expertise).
