## Overview

Dr. Amr O. El-Naggar is a neurosurgeon in Somerset, Kentucky and is affiliated with [Lake Cumberland Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ky/lake-cumberland-regional-hospital-6510940). He received his medical degree from Ain Shams University Faculty of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Amr O. El-Naggar has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amr-el-naggar-259250#expertise). Dr. Amr O. El-Naggar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amr-el-naggar-259250#insurance).
