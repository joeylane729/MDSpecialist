## Overview

Dr. Richard L. Kern is a neurosurgeon in Wichita Falls, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Kell West Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/kell-west-regional-hospital-6740182) and [United Regional Health Care System-Wichita Falls](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/united-regional-hospital-6740136). He received his medical degree from Brody School of Medicine at East Carolina University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Richard L. Kern has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-kern-567922#expertise). Dr. Richard L. Kern accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-kern-567922#insurance). Dr. Richard L. Kern is highly recommended by patients.
