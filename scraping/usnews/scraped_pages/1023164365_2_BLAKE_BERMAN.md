Berman is a neurosurgeon in La Mirada, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Chapman Global Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/chapman-medical-center-6932302) and [Desert Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/desert-regional-medical-center-6932325).
