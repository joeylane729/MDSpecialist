## Overview

Dr. Srinath Samudrala is a neurosurgeon in Pomona, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Pomona Valley Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/pomona-valley-hospital-medical-center-6932450), [Adventist Health Glendale](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/glendale-adventist-medical-center-6930831) and [Archibald Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ca/archibald-surgery-center-A2278990). He received his medical degree from David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Srinath Samudrala accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/srinath-samudrala-392604#insurance).
