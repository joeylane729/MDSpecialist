## Overview

Dr. Subu N. Magge is a neurosurgeon in Burlington, Massachusetts and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Lahey Hospital and Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/lahey-hospital-and-medical-center-6140690) and [Beverly Hospital-Massachusetts](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/beverly-hospital-6140017). He received his medical degree from Yale School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Subu N. Magge has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/subu-magge-27860#expertise). Dr. Subu N. Magge accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/subu-magge-27860#insurance). Dr. Subu N. Magge is highly recommended by patients.
