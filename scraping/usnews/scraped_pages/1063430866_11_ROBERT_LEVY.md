## Overview

Dr. Robert G. Levy is an ophthalmologist in Salem, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Salem Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/north-shore-medical-center-6140002) and [North Shore Cataract and Laser Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ma/north-shore-cataract-and-laser-center-A1578435). He received his medical degree from Boston University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert G. Levy has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, shingles, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-levy-148370#expertise). Dr. Robert G. Levy accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-levy-148370#insurance). Dr. Robert G. Levy is highly recommended by patients.
