## Overview

Dr. Michael Brown is an internist in Savannah, Georgia and is affiliated with [Memorial Health University Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/memorial-university-medical-center-6381027). He received his medical degree from Mercer University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Michael Brown has expertise in treating hypertension & high blood pressure, stroke, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-brown-2459686#expertise). Dr. Michael Brown accepts Medicare, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-brown-2459686#insurance).
