## Overview

Dr. Richard Ogbuji is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [Baystate Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/baystate-medical-center-6141955). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Richard Ogbuji has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-ogbuji-839437#expertise). Dr. Richard Ogbuji accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-ogbuji-839437#insurance). Dr. Richard Ogbuji is highly recommended by patients.
