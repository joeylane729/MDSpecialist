## Overview

Dr. Brian K. Hudson is a neurosurgeon in Spring Hill, Florida and is affiliated with [HCA Florida Oak Hill Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/oak-hill-hospital-6390997) and [Florida Springs Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/florida-springs-surgery-center-A4482172). He received his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine - Seton Hill and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Brian K. Hudson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-hudson-1400741#expertise). Dr. Brian K. Hudson accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-hudson-1400741#insurance). Dr. Brian K. Hudson is highly recommended by patients.
