## Overview

Dr. Kurt D. Bangerter is a neurosurgeon in Ogden, Utah and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Intermountain Health McKay-Dee Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/intermountain-mckay-dee-hospital-center-6870160) and [Intermountain Health Layton Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ut/layton-hospital-6870379).
