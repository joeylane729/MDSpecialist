## Overview

Dr. Breck Jones is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HSHS St. John's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/st-johns-hospital-6432980) and [Springfield Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/memorial-medical-center-6432950). He received his medical degree from Southern Illinois University School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Breck Jones has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/breck-jones-1121869#expertise). Dr. Breck Jones accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/breck-jones-1121869#insurance).
