## Overview

Dr. Barry J. Landau is a neurosurgeon in Bellingham, Washington and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [PeaceHealth St. Joseph Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/peacehealth-st-joseph-medical-center-6910001), [Skagit Regional Health-Mount Vernon](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/skagit-valley-hospital-6910420) and [Cascade Outpatient Spine Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/wa/cascade-outpatient-spine-center-A6096905).
