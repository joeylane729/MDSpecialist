## Overview

Dr. Christopher T. Taylor is an ophthalmologist in Knoxville, Tennessee. He received his medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Christopher T. Taylor has expertise in treating cataract, glaucoma, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/christopher-taylor-129388#expertise).
