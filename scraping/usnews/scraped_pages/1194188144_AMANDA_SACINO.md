## Overview

Dr. Amanda Sacino is a neurosurgeon in Naples, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [NCH Baker Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/naples-community-hospital-6390669) and [Physicians Regional-Pine Ridge Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/physicians-regional-medical-center-pine-ridge-6390103). She received her medical degree from University of Florida College of Medicine and has been in practice between 6-10 years. Dr. Amanda Sacino has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amanda-sacino-2554660#expertise). Dr. Amanda Sacino accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/amanda-sacino-2554660#insurance). Dr. Amanda Sacino is highly recommended by patients.
