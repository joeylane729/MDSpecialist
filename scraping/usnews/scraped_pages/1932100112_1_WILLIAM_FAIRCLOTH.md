## Overview

Dr. William B. Faircloth is a neurosurgeon in Mobile, Alabama and is affiliated with [Mobile Infirmary Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/al/mobile-infirmary-medical-center-6530006). He received his medical degree from University of Alabama School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. William B. Faircloth has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/william-faircloth-161864#expertise). Faircloth is highly recommended by patients.
