## Overview

Dr. Joseph J. Chen is a physiatrist in Clive, Iowa and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MercyOne Clive Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/mercyone-clive-rehabilitation-hospital-6620427) and [MercyOne Des Moines Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/mercy-medical-center-des-moines-6620003). He received his medical degree from The Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joseph J. Chen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joseph-chen-2297159#insurance). Dr. Joseph J. Chen is highly recommended by patients.
