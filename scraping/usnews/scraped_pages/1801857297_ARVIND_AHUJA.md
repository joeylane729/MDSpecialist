## Overview

Dr. Arvind Ahuja is a neurosurgeon in Franklin, Wisconsin and is affiliated with [Froedtert South-Kenosha Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wi/united-hospitals-kenosha-medical-center-campus-6450610).
