## Overview

Dr. John Spitalieri is a neurosurgeon in Prescott, Arizona and is affiliated with [Dignity Health Yavapai Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/az/yavapai-regional-medical-center-6860320) and [Prescott Outpatient Surgical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/az/prescott-outpatient-surgical-center-A7789057). He received his medical degree from Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John Spitalieri has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-spitalieri-2546384#expertise). Dr. John Spitalieri accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-spitalieri-2546384#insurance). Dr. John Spitalieri is highly recommended by patients.
