## Overview

Dr. Sheldon Lazar is a neurosurgeon in Evanston, Illinois and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Endeavor Health NorthShore Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/northshore-evanston-hospital-6430021) and [Saint Francis Hospital-Evanston](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/il/presence-st-francis-hospital-6431670). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sheldon Lazar has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sheldon-lazar-167357#expertise). Dr. Sheldon Lazar accepts Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sheldon-lazar-167357#insurance).
