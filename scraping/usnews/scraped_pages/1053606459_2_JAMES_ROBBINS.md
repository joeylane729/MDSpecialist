### Colon & Rectal Surgery

Colon and rectal surgeons diagnose and treat diseases of the lower digestive tract, including colon cancer. This often requires colorectal surgery, which is used to treat hemorrhoids, inflammatory bowel diseases (Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis), and cancer.
