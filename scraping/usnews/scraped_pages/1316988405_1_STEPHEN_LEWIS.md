## Overview

Dr. Stephen Lewis is a psychiatrist in Albuquerque, New Mexico and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Raymond G. Murphy VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/veterans-affairs-medical-center-6850080) and [University of New Mexico Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/nm/university-hospital-6850037). He received his medical degree from University of New Mexico School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Stephen Lewis has expertise in treating bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety disorders, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-lewis-486769#expertise). Dr. Stephen Lewis accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/stephen-lewis-486769#insurance). Dr. Stephen Lewis is highly recommended by patients.
