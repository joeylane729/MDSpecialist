## Overview

Dr. David K. Su is a neurosurgeon in Seattle, Washington. He received his medical degree from University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. David K. Su has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/david-su-55524#expertise).
