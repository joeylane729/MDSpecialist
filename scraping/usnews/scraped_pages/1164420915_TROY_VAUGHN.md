## Overview

Dr. Troy M. Vaughn is a neurosurgeon in Alexandria, Louisiana and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Central Louisiana Surgical Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/central-louisiana-surgical-hospital-6720500) and [Christus St. Frances Cabrini Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/la/christus-st-frances-cabrini-hospital-6720050). He received his medical degree from LSU School of Medicine in Shreveport and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Troy M. Vaughn has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/troy-vaughn-148322#expertise). Dr. Troy M. Vaughn accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/troy-vaughn-148322#insurance).
