## Overview

Dr. Ha T. Nguyen is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Deer Park, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Memorial Hermann Greater Heights Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-northwest-hospital-6741952) and [Memorial Hermann Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/memorial-hermann-texas-medical-center-6741890).
