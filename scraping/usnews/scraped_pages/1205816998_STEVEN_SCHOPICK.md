## Overview

Dr. Steven Schopick is a neurosurgeon in Cedar Rapids, Iowa and is affiliated with [Mercy Medical Center-Cedar Rapids](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ia/mercy-medical-center-6620170). He received his medical degree from Albert Einstein College of Medicine of Yeshiva University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Steven Schopick has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/steven-schopick-249517#expertise).
