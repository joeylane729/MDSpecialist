## Overview

Dr. Beth Gibbons is a neurosurgeon in Castle Rock, Colorado and is affiliated with [North Colorado Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/co/north-colorado-medical-center-6840650). She received her medical degree from University of Michigan Medical School and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Beth Gibbons accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/beth-gibbons-4052722#insurance).
