## Overview

Dr. Michael L. Mumert is a neurosurgeon in Springfield, Missouri. He received his medical degree from University of Kansas School of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Michael L. Mumert has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-mumert-102250#expertise).
