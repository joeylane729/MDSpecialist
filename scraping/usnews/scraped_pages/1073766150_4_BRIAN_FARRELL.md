## Overview

Dr. Brian T. Farrell is a neurosurgeon in Paducah, Kentucky. He received his medical degree from University of Nebraska College of Medicine and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Brian T. Farrell has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-farrell-697018#expertise).
