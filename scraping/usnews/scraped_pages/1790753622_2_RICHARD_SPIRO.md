## Overview

Dr. Richard M. Spiro is a neurosurgeon in Buffalo, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Erie County Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/erie-county-medical-center-6211070) and [Independence Health System Butler Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/pa/butler-hospital-6230270).
