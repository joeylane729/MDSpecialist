## Overview

Dr. Stephen G. Smith is an anesthesiologist in Englewood, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Englewood Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/englewood-community-hospital-6391450) and [SSM Select Rehabilitation Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/ssm-select-rehabilitation-hospital-6630467).
