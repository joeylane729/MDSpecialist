## Overview

Dr. Thomas Oliver is a neurosurgeon in Ocala, Florida and is affiliated with [Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Tallahassee](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/healthsouth-rehabilitation-hospital-of-tallahassee-6391055).
