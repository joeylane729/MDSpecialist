## Overview

Dr. Evan Lytle is a neurosurgeon in Saint Joseph, Michigan and is affiliated with [Corewell Health Lakeland Hospitals](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mi/lakeland-regional-medical-center-6440215). He received his medical degree from A.T. Still University, School of Osteopathic Medicine in Arizona and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Evan Lytle has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/evan-lytle-928260#expertise). Dr. Evan Lytle accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/evan-lytle-928260#insurance).
