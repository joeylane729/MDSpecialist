## Overview

Dr. Brian K. Lee is a radiation oncologist in Murfreesboro, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Saint Thomas Rutherford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/middle-tennessee-medical-center-6520920) and [TriStar StoneCrest Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/tristar-stonecrest-medical-center-6520028). Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-lee-380690#insurance).
