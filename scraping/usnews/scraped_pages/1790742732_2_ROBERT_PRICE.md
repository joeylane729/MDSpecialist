## Overview

Dr. Robert H. Price is a neurologist in San Luis Obispo, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Sierra Vista Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/sierra-vista-regional-medical-center-6933295) and [Twin Cities Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/twin-cities-community-hospital-6933687).
