## Overview

Dr. William Bingaman is a neurosurgeon in Cleveland, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/cleveland-clinic-6410670) and [Cleveland Clinic Children's Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/childrens-hospital-cleveland-clinic-PA6410670).
