## Overview

Dr. Richard H. Kim is a cardiologist in Delray Beach, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Delray Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/delray-medical-center-6390081) and [Baptist Health Bethesda Hospital East & West](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/bethesda-memorial-hospital-6390050). Kim has expertise in treating coronary artery disease, hypertension & high blood pressure, aneurysm, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/richard-kim-229551#expertise).
