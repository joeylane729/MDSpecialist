## Overview

Dr. Michael A. Lefkowitz is a neurosurgeon in New Hyde Park, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [St. Catherine of Siena Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-catherine-of-siena-medical-center-6214616) and [St. Francis Hospital & Heart Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/st-francis-hospital-6214410).
