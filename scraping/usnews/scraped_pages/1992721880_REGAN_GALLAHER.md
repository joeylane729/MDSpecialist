## Overview

Dr. Regan S. Gallaher is a neurosurgeon in Conway, Arkansas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Conway Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/conway-regional-medical-center-6710110) and [Jefferson Regional Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ar/jefferson-regional-medical-center-6710520). He received his medical degree from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Regan S. Gallaher has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/regan-gallaher-777370#expertise). Dr. Regan S. Gallaher accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/regan-gallaher-777370#insurance).
