## Overview

Dr. Ramin Javahery is a neurosurgeon in Long Beach, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MemorialCare Long Beach Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/long-beach-memorial-medical-center-6931370) and [MemorialCare Miller Children's and Women's Hospital Long Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/miller-childrens-hospital-6930103). He received his medical degree from University of Southern California School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Ramin Javahery has expertise in treating spinal fusion, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ramin-javahery-66455#expertise). Dr. Ramin Javahery accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/ramin-javahery-66455#insurance). Dr. Ramin Javahery is highly recommended by patients.
