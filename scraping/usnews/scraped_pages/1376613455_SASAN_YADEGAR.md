## Overview

Dr. Sasan Yadegar is a neurosurgeon in Inglewood, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Centinela Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/centinela-hospital-medical-center-6931050) and [Northridge Hospital Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/northridge-hospital-medical-center-6932118).
