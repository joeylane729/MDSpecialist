## Overview

Dr. Sanjay K. Gupta is an orthopedist in Brookfield, Connecticut and is affiliated with [Danbury Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/danbury-hospital-6160100), [Healthsouth Surgery Center of Danbury](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ct/healthsouth-surgery-center-of-danbury-A6180806) and [Lighthouse Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/ct/lighthouse-surgery-center-A4016173). He received his medical degree from Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Sanjay K. Gupta has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, hip replacement, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-gupta-341331#expertise). Dr. Sanjay K. Gupta accepts Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/sanjay-gupta-341331#insurance). Dr. Sanjay K. Gupta is highly recommended by patients.
