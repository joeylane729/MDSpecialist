## Overview

Dr. Grzegorz Brzezicki is a neurosurgeon in Jacksonville, Florida and is affiliated with [UF Health Jacksonville](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/shands-jacksonville-medical-center-6390082). Dr. Grzegorz Brzezicki has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/grzegorz-brzezicki-1146563#expertise).
