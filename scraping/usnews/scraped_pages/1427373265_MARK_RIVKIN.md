Rivkin is a neurosurgeon in Festus, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Mercy Hospital Jefferson](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/jefferson-regional-medical-center-6630192) and [Mercy Hospital South](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/st-anthonys-medical-center-6631160).
