## Overview

Dr. James J. Lee is an emergency medicine physician in Brunswick, Georgia and is affiliated with [Southeast Georgia Health System Brunswick Campus](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ga/southeast-georgia-hospital-brunswick-campus-6380340).
