## Overview

Dr. Steven A. Dutcher is a neurosurgeon in Lake Worth, Florida and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [HCA Florida JFK Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/jfk-medical-center-6390074), [HCA Florida Palms West Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/palms-west-hospital-6390753) and [Orthopedic Surgery Center of Palm Beach County](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/fl/orthopedic-surgery-center-of-palm-beach-county-A4358141).
