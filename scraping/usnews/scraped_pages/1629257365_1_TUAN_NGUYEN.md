## Overview

Dr. Tuan A. Nguyen is a plastic surgeon in Lake Oswego, Oregon and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Portland VA Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/portland-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6920590) and [Providence Hood River Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/or/providence-hood-river-memorial-hospital-6920190). He received his medical degree from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center at Lubbock and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Tuan A. Nguyen accepts Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/tuan-nguyen-109287#insurance).
