## Overview

Dr. Robert H. Cohen is an obstetrician-gynecologist in Long Beach, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [MemorialCare Long Beach Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/long-beach-memorial-medical-center-6931370) and [St. Mary Medical Center-Long Beach](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/st-mary-medical-center-6931360). He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert H. Cohen accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-cohen-352140#insurance).
