## Overview

Dr. Patrick J. Murray is an orthopedist in West Nyack, New York and is affiliated with [Good Samaritan Regional Medical Center-Suffern](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/good-samaritan-hospital-6214765). He received his medical degree from SUNY Upstate Medical University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Patrick J. Murray has expertise in treating arthritis, bursitis, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-murray-664025#expertise). Dr. Patrick J. Murray accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/patrick-murray-664025#insurance).
