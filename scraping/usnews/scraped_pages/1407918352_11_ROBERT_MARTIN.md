## Overview

Dr. Robert Martin is a general surgeon in Fort Sam Houston, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Brooke Army Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/san-antonio-military-medical-center-6741380) and [Methodist Hospital-San Antonio](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/methodist-hospital-6743122).
