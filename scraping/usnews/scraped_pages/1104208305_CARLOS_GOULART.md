## Overview

Dr. Carlos R. Goulart is a neurosurgeon in Duluth, Minnesota and is affiliated with [Essentia Health-Duluth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mn/essentia-health-medical-center-duluth-6610390). He received his medical degree from Faculdade Evangélica do Paraná and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Carlos R. Goulart has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carlos-goulart-3481438#expertise). Dr. Carlos R. Goulart accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/carlos-goulart-3481438#insurance).
