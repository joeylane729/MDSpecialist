## Overview

Dr. Robert L. Marsh is an oncologist in Gainesville, Virginia and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Fauquier Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/fauquier-hospital-6341165) and [UVA Health System Haymarket Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/va/novant-health-haymarket-medical-center-6340429). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert L. Marsh has expertise in treating lung cancer, chronic leukemia, breast cancer, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-marsh-289089#expertise).
