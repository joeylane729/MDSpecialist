## Overview

Dr. Michael L. Copeland is a neurosurgeon in Independence, Missouri and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Centerpoint Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mo/centerpoint-medical-center-6630039) and [Billings Clinic](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/mt/billings-clinic-6810020). He received his medical degree from University of Missouri-Columbia School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael L. Copeland has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-copeland-246688#expertise). Dr. Michael L. Copeland accepts Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-copeland-246688#insurance).
