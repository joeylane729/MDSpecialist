## Overview

Dr. Miguel Melgar is a neurosurgeon in Gulfport, Mississippi and is affiliated with [Memorial Hospital Gulfport](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ms/memorial-hospital-at-gulfport-6540300).
