## Overview

Dr. Robert E. Fox is an internist in Westerly, Rhode Island and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Westerly Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ri/westerly-hospital-6150220) and [Lawrence and Memorial Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ct/lawrence-and-memorial-hospital-6160480). He received his medical degree from Ross University School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Robert E. Fox has expertise in treating obesity, hypertension & high blood pressure, diabetes, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-fox-375254#expertise). Dr. Robert E. Fox accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/robert-fox-375254#insurance).
