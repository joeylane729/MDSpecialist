## Overview

Dr. Bertram Richter is a neurosurgeon in Columbia, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Columbia VA Health Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/wm-jennings-bryan-dorn-veterans-affairs-medical-center-6370210) and [Prisma Health Baptist Parkridge Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/palmetto-health-baptist-parkridge-6370429). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice between 11-20 years. Dr. Bertram Richter has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bertram-richter-1171037#expertise). Dr. Bertram Richter accepts Medicare, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bertram-richter-1171037#insurance).
