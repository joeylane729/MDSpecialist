## Overview

Dr. Paul A. Jackson is a cardiologist in Murfreesboro, Tennessee and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Ascension Saint Thomas Rutherford Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/middle-tennessee-medical-center-6520920) and [TriStar StoneCrest Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tn/tristar-stonecrest-medical-center-6520028).
