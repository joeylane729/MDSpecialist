## Overview

Dr. Madhav Sukumaran is a neurosurgeon in Worcester, Massachusetts and is affiliated with [UMass Memorial Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ma/umass-memorial-medical-center-6141095).
