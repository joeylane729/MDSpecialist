## Overview

Dr. Brian K. Lee is a pediatric cardiologist in Madera, California and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Valley Children's Healthcare and Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/valley-childrens-healthcare-and-hospital-6930814) and [Emanuel Medical Center-Turlock](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ca/emanuel-medical-center-6933730). Lee accepts Medicare, Aetna, Blue Cross, Kaiser, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/brian-lee-1042680#insurance).
