## Overview

Dr. Timothy R. Owens is a neurosurgeon in Brunswick, Georgia and is affiliated with [Ascension St. Vincent's Riverside Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/st-vincents-medical-center-6390390).
