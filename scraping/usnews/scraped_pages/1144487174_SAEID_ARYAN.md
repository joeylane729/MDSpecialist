## Overview

Dr. Saeid E. Aryan is a neurosurgeon in Fort Worth, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital Fort Worth](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-harris-methodist-hospital-6741440) and [Baylor Scott and White Medical Center-Grapevine](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/baylor-regional-medical-center-6741739).
