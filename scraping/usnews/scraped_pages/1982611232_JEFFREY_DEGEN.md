## Overview

Dr. Jeffrey W. Degen is a neurosurgeon in West Nyack, New York and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Bon Secours Community Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/bon-secours-community-hospital-6214053) and [Good Samaritan Regional Medical Center-Suffern](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/ny/good-samaritan-hospital-6214765). He received his medical degree from Weill Cornell Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Jeffrey W. Degen has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-degen-485747#expertise). Dr. Jeffrey W. Degen accepts Medicare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/jeffrey-degen-485747#insurance). Dr. Jeffrey W. Degen is highly recommended by patients.
