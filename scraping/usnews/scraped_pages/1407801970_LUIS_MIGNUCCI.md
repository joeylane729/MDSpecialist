## Overview

Dr. Luis A. Mignucci is a neurosurgeon in Plano, Texas and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Medical City Plano](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/medical-center-of-plano-6742865), [Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Plano](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/tx/texas-health-presbyterian-hospital-6740945) and [Windhaven Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/tx/windhaven-surgery-center-A4886040).
