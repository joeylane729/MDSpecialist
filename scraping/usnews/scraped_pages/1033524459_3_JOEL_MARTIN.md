## Overview

Dr. Joel E. Martin is an internist in Silverdale, Washington and is affiliated with [Virginia Mason Franciscan Health-St. Michael Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/wa/harrison-medical-center-6910115). He received his medical degree from Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Joel E. Martin has expertise in treating arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease (ibd), heart failure, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/joel-martin-447015#expertise).
