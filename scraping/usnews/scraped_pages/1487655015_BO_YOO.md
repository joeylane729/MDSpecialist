## Overview

Dr. Bo H. Yoo is a neurosurgeon in Elyria, Ohio and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Fisher-Titus Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/fisher-titus-medical-center-6411830) and [Mercy Health-Lorain Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/mercy-regional-medical-center-6410014). He received his medical degree from Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Bo H. Yoo has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bo-yoo-160137#expertise). Dr. Bo H. Yoo accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, Blue Cross - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/bo-yoo-160137#insurance).
