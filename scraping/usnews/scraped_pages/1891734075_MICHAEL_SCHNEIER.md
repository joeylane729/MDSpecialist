## Overview

Dr. Michael Schneier is a neurosurgeon in Las Vegas, Nevada. He received his medical degree from New York University Grossman School of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Michael Schneier has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-schneier-383661#expertise). Dr. Michael Schneier accepts Aetna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/michael-schneier-383661#insurance).
