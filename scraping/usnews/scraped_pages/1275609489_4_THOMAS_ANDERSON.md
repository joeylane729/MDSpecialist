## Overview

Dr. Thomas S. Anderson is a neurosurgeon in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina and is affiliated with multiple hospitals in the area, including [Grand Strand Regional Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/grand-strand-regional-medical-center-6370477), [East Cooper Medical Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/sc/east-cooper-hospital-6370460) and [The Southeastern Spine Institute Ambulatory Surgery Center](https://health.usnews.com/best-ascs/area/sc/the-southeastern-spine-institute-ambulatory-surgery-center-A1746851). He received his medical degree from Medical University of South Carolina College of Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Thomas S. Anderson has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-anderson-93964#expertise). Dr. Thomas S. Anderson accepts Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/thomas-anderson-93964#insurance). Dr. Thomas S. Anderson is highly recommended by patients.
