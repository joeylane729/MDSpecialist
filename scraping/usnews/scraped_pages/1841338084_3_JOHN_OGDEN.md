## Overview

Dr. John R. Ogden is a neurosurgeon in Findlay, Ohio and is affiliated with [Blanchard Valley Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/oh/blanchard-valley-hospital-6410017). He received his medical degree from University of Arizona College of Medicine-Tucson and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. John R. Ogden has expertise in treating spinal fusion, spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-ogden-3214122#expertise). Dr. John R. Ogden accepts Medicare, Aetna, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/john-ogden-3214122#insurance).
