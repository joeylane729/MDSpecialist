## Overview

Dr. Arash Farahvar is a neurosurgeon in Venice, Florida and is affiliated with [HCA Florida Sarasota Doctors Hospital](https://health.usnews.com/best-hospitals/area/fl/doctors-hospital-of-sarasota-6390956). He received his medical degree from University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago and has been in practice for more than 20 years. Dr. Arash Farahvar has expertise in treating spinal stenosis and spondylosis, among other conditions - see all areas of [expertise](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arash-farahvar-498725#expertise). Dr. Arash Farahvar accepts Medicare, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross, United Healthcare - see other [insurance plans accepted](https://health.usnews.com/doctors/arash-farahvar-498725#insurance).
