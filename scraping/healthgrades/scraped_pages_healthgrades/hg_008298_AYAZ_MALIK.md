Dr. Ayaz Malik, MD is a vascular surgeon in Saint Louis, MO and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Malik has extensive experience in Peripheral Vascular Disease and Carotid Artery Disease or Carotid Endarterectomy. He graduated from University of Texas Health Sciences Center at San Antonio Long School of Medicine in 1990. He is affiliated with medical facilities Mercy Hospital St. Louis and Mercy Hospital South. He is accepting new patients.
