Dr. Minh Nguyen, MD is a cardiologist in Bethlehem, PA and has over 40 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Nguyen has extensive experience in Adult Congenital Heart Conditions. He graduated from Temple University Lewis Katz School of Medicine in 1984. He is affiliated with medical facilities St. Luke's Hospital-Bethlehem Campus and Lehigh Valley Hospital-Cedar Crest. He is accepting new patients.
