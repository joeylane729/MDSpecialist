Dr. Gonzalo Sanchez Jr, MD is an orthopedic surgeon in Pierre, SD and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Sanchez Jr has extensive experience in Shoulder & Upper Arm Surgery, Knee & Lower Limb Surgery, and Hand & Wrist Surgery. He graduated from University of Kansas School of Medicine in 1998. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Avera St. Mary's Hospital and Avera Hand County Memorial Hospital. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
