## About Me

### care philosophy

Dr. Chen's approach to medicine is centered on education and honesty. He believes that being well-informed is paramount to patient satisfaction. Dr. Chen thoroughly addresses his patients' questions and concerns with an emphasis on conservative care prior to surgical intervention.

### biography

Dr. Eric Chen, DPM serves as a Podiatry Specialist for Foot and Ankle Clinics of Arizona in Phoenix and Laveen, AZ.  He is Board Qualified in both Foot and Reconstructive Rearfoot/Ankle Surgery by the American Board of Foot and Ankle Surgery.
