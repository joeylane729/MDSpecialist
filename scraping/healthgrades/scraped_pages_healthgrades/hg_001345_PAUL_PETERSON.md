## About Me

### care philosophy

Dr. Peterson is a podiatrist with special interests in surgically addressing biomechanical deformities of the foot, sports injuries, limb-threatening infections, diabetic foot care, and lower extremity wound care. He believes in attempting conservative clinical treatment prior to pursuing surgery whenever possible.

### biography

Dr. Peterson enjoys cooking, grilling, golfing, boating, snowmobiling, ice fishing and spending time with family.
