Dr. Shamsuddin Khwaja, MD is a cardiothoracic surgeon in Fresno, CA and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Khwaja has extensive experience in Aortic Aneurysm or Dissection and Cardiac Surgical Procedures. He graduated from Boston University in 1996.
