Dr. Kris Smith, MD is a neurosurgeon in Phoenix, AZ and has over 35 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Smith has extensive experience in Nervous System Neoplasms and Cranial Neurosurgery. He graduated from University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine in 1989. He is affiliated with medical facilities St. Joseph's Hospital and Medical Center and HonorHealth Scottsdale Osborn Medical Center. He is not accepting new patients.
