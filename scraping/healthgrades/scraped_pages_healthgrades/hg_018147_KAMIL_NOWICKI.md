## About Me

### biography

Kamil W. Nowicki, MD, PhD, is an endovascular neurosurgeon and physician-scientist. After receiving his combined MD-PhD from the University of Florida, he completed a neurosurgery residency at UPMC with one year of enfolded endovascular fellowship. He then completed an endovascular fellowship at Yale.
