Dr. Mark Luciano, MD is a neurosurgeon in Baltimore, MD and has over 35 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Luciano has extensive experience in Neurocritical Care Disorders. He graduated from University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine in 1985. He is affiliated with medical facilities The Johns Hopkins Hospital and Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center.
