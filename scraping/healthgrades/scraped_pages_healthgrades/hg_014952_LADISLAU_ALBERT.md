## About Me

### care philosophy

Each neurosurgical patient is a unique individual. The right treatment must be specifically tailored to a combination of that person's anatomical findings, emotional state, and family support system. A neurosurgeon must always remember to treat the person and not just the radiologic and clinical exam findings.

### biography

A cum laude graduate of New York University with an undergraduate degree in neuroscience. He recieved specialized training in spinal surgery, with focus in degenerative spinal stenosis and herniated disc repair. In addition, he specializes in spinal neurosurgical conditions.
