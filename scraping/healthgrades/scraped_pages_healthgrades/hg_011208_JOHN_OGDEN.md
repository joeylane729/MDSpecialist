Dr. John Ogden, MD is an orthopedic surgeon in Carthage, MO and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Ogden has extensive experience in Shoulder & Upper Arm Surgery. He graduated from LSU Health Sciences Center Shreveport School of Medicine in 1990. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Mercy Specialty Hospital Southeast Kansas and Mercy Hospital Carthage. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
