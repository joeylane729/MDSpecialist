Dr. Mark Johnson, MD is a pulmonologist in Lubbock, TX and has over 45 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Johnson has extensive experience in Interstitial Lung Disease and Obstructive Lung Disease. He graduated from University of Minnesota Medical School in 1978.
