Dr. Minh Tran, MD is a pulmonologist in Houston, TX and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Tran has extensive experience in Respiratory Tract Conditions, Obstructive Lung Disease, and Interstitial Lung Disease. He graduated from University of Texas Health San Antonio School of Dentistry in 1998. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Baylor Scott & White Medical Center-Grapevine and Houston Methodist West Hospital. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
