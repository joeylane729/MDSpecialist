Dr. Loan Nguyen, MD is a critical care medicine specialist in Oklahoma City, OK and has over 10 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Nguyen has extensive experience in Interstitial Lung Disease, Obstructive Lung Disease, and Pulmonary Vascular Conditions. She graduated from UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMA / HEALTH SCIENCES CENTER in 2011. She is affiliated with medical facilities such as Mercy Hospital Ada and Mercy Hospital Oklahoma City.
