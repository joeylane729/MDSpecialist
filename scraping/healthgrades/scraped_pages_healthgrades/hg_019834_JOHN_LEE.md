Dr. John Lee, MD is a general surgeon in Rancho Mirage, CA and has over 15 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Lee has extensive experience in Peripheral Vascular Procedures, Carotid Artery Disease or Carotid Endarterectomy, and Peripheral Vascular Disease. He graduated from Ross University School of Medicine in 2008. He is affiliated with medical facilities Desert Regional Medical Center and Eisenhower Medical Center. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
