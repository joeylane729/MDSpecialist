## About Me

### care philosophy

My Philosophy to patient care is to try and avoid the need for surgery unless there is no other option. When Surgery is indicated my approach is to always do the procedure that will provide the greatest benefit with the least invasiveness. Putting the patient first and ving the patient options is foremost in my paradigm.
