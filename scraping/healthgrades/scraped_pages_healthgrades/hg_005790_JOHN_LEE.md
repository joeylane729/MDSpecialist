Dr. John Lee, MD is a pulmonologist in Shelby Township, MI and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Lee has extensive experience in Obstructive Lung Disease and Interstitial Lung Disease. He graduated from University of Toronto Faculty of Medicine in 1993.
