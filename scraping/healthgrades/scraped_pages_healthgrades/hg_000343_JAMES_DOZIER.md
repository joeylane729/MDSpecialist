Dr. James Dozier, MD is an obstetrician gynecologist in Longview, TX and has over 15 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Dozier has extensive experience in Gynecologic Conditions, Obstetric Care, and Gynecologic Surgery. He graduated from University of Mississippi School of Medicine in 2006. He is affiliated with medical facilities CHRISTUS Good Shepherd Medical Center-Marshall and CHRISTUS Mother Frances Hospital-Tyler. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
