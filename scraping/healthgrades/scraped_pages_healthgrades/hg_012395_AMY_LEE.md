Dr. Amy Lee, MD is a pulmonologist in Columbus, OH and has over 20 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Lee has extensive experience in Interstitial Lung Disease, Respiratory Tract Conditions, and Obstructive Lung Disease. She graduated from West Virginia University School of Medicine in 2003.
