Dr. Patrick Kelly, MD is a vascular surgeon in Sioux Falls, SD and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Kelly has extensive experience in Carotid Artery Disease or Carotid Endarterectomy, Peripheral Vascular Procedures, and Aortic Aneurysm or Dissection. He graduated from University of South Dakota in 1998. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Avera Sacred Heart Hospital and Avera McKennan Hospital and University Health Center. He is accepting telehealth appointments.
