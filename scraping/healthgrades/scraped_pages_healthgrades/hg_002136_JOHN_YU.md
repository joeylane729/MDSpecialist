Dr. John Yu, MD is a critical care medicine specialist in Sioux Falls, SD and has over 35 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Yu has extensive experience in Obstructive Lung Disease, Sleep Apnea, and Interstitial Lung Disease. He graduated from University of Santo Tomas Faculty of Medicine and Surgery in 1988. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Sanford Usd Medical Center and Sanford Worthington Medical Center. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
