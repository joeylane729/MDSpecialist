Dr. Justin Cohen, MD is an oncology specialist in Charleston, WV and has over 40 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Cohen has extensive experience in Gastrointestinal Neoplasms, Pancreatic Neoplasms, and Renal Neoplasms & Resection. He graduated from Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine in 1981. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Boone Memorial Hospital and Braxton County Memorial Hospital. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
