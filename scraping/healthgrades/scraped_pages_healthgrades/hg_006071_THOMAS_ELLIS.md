Dr. Thomas Ellis, MD is an orthopedic surgeon in Columbus, OH and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Ellis has extensive experience in Hip & Pelvic Surgery. He graduated from Vanderbilt University School of Medicine in 1992. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Orthoindy Hospital and Crystal Clinic Orthopaedic Center. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
