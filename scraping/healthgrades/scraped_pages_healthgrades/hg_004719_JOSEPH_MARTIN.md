Dr. Joseph Martinelli, MD is a critical care medicine specialist in Duluth, MN and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Martinelli has extensive experience in Obstructive Lung Disease, Interstitial Lung Disease, and Sleep Apnea. He graduated from George Washington University School of Medicine & Health Sciences in 1996. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Essentia Health-Moose Lake and Community Memorial Hospital. He is not accepting new patients.
