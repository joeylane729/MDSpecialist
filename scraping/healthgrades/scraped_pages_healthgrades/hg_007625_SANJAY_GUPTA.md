Dr. Sanjay Gupta, MD is an orthopedic surgeon in Brookfield, CT and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Gupta has extensive experience in Knee & Lower Limb Surgery and Hip & Pelvic Surgery. He graduated from JIPMER Pondicherry/Med College in 1994. He is affiliated with Danbury Hospital. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
