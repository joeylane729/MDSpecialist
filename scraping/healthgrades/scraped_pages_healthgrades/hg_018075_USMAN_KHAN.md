Dr. Usman Khan, MD is a nephrologist in Ottawa, IL and has over 25 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Khan has extensive experience in Nephrological Conditions and Glomerulonephritis & Nephropathy. He graduated from Dow Medical College, Dow University Of Health Sciences in 1998. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as OSF Saint Clare Medical Center and OSF Saint Elizabeth Medical Center. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
