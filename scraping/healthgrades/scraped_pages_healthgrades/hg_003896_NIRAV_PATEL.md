Dr. Nirav Patel, MD is an internist in Jackson, TN and has over 20 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Patel has extensive experience in Adult Congenital Heart Conditions, Heart Conditions, and Interventional Cardiac Procedures. He graduated from Michigan State University College of Human Medicine in 2002. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as Jackson-Madison County General Hospital and Baptist Memorial Hospital Carroll County. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
