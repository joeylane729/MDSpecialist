Dr. Charles Wright, MD is a urologist in Hot Springs, AR and has over 45 years of experience in the medical field. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as CHI St. Vincent Hot Springs and Baptist Health Medical Center-Arkadelphia. He is accepting new patients and telehealth appointments.
