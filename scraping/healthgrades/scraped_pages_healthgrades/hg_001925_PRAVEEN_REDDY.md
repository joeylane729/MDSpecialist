Dr. Praveen Reddy, MD is a hematologist in Wichita Falls, TX and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Reddy has extensive experience in Gastrointestinal Neoplasms, Renal Neoplasms & Resection, and Lymphatic System Disorders. He graduated from UNIVERSIDAD DEL COMAHUE / FACULTAD DE MEDICINA in 1994. He is affiliated with medical facilities Kell West Regional Hospital and United Regional Health Care System.
