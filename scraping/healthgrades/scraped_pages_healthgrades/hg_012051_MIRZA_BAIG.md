Dr. Mirza Baig, MD is a vascular surgeon in Dallas, TX and has over 20 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Baig has extensive experience in Peripheral Vascular Procedures, Carotid Artery Disease or Carotid Endarterectomy, and Aortic Aneurysm or Dissection. He graduated from University of California, Irvine School of Medicine in 2000. He is affiliated with medical facilities such as United Regional Health Care System and Shannon Medical Center.
